演算法與複雜度

1990 年 BLAST 把 DNA 比對從 6 小時降到 90 秒——這是演算法的力量。寫得對只是起點，寫得快才是工程。本章從 Big-O 走到生物資訊最核心的演算法。

In 1990, BLAST cut DNA alignment from 6 hours to 90 seconds — that's the power of algorithms. Writing correct code is just the start; writing fast code is engineering. This chapter goes from Big-O to the algorithms at bioinformatics' core.

90 秒奇蹟

從 6 小時到 90 秒：BLAST 與演算法的力量

1990 年以前，要在 GenBank 找出與你序列相似的 DNA，需要用 Smith-Waterman 動態規劃 (1981)——對 100 萬條序列要跑 6 小時。然後，1990 年 Stephen Altschul、David Lipman 等人發表了 BLAST 演算法，用 k-mer 種子 + 局部延伸 + 啟發式 把同樣的查詢壓到 90 秒。同一台電腦、同樣的資料——只是換了演算法。BLAST 論文 (Altschul et al. 1990, J Mol Biol) 至今被引超過 11 萬次，是史上最被引用的 bioinformatics 論文。

這就是這一章要教你的核心信念：選對演算法的提速，遠大於選對語言、加更多 CPU、或升級硬體。

同一個問題，C 寫的 O(n²) 在 100 萬筆資料上跑 30 分鐘；Python 寫的 O(n log n) 只要 3 秒——這不是寫程式技巧的差別，是「複雜度等級」的差別。本章帶你看懂為什麼。

Before 1990, finding sequences in GenBank similar to yours required Smith-Waterman dynamic programming (1981) — about 6 hours for 1M sequences. Then in 1990, Stephen Altschul, David Lipman and others published BLAST — using k-mer seeds + local extension + heuristics, the same query took 90 seconds. Same computer, same data — just a different algorithm. Altschul et al. 1990 (J Mol Biol) has 110,000+ citations, the most-cited bioinformatics paper in history.

That's the core belief of this chapter: Picking the right algorithm beats picking the right language, adding CPUs, or upgrading hardware.

For 1M records, an O(n²) algorithm in C may run 30 minutes — an O(n log n) algorithm in Python runs in 3 seconds. That's not a coding-style difference; it's a complexity-class difference. This chapter shows why.

💡
Knuth 的演算法五特性：① 輸入 (input)、② 輸出 (output)、③ 有限性 (finiteness：一定要停)、④ 確定性 (definiteness：每一步明確)、⑤ 有效性 (effectiveness：每步可在有限時間執行)。少一條就不是演算法。 Knuth's five properties: (1) input, (2) output, (3) finiteness (must terminate), (4) definiteness (each step unambiguous), (5) effectiveness (each step executable in finite time). Miss any one and it's not an algorithm.
核心：Big-O

一、初學者必懂：Big-O 漸近分析

Big-O 描述「輸入大小 n 趨於無窮時，執行時間（或記憶體）的增長等級」。常數、低階項、係數通通忽略——只看「長相」。

用一個比喻：「從台北到高雄」。走路 8 小時，騎車 4 小時，開車 3 小時。距離變兩倍（台北到墾丁），三者都變兩倍。Big-O 描述的就是「距離 vs 時間」的曲線形狀——是線性、對數、還是平方？

Big-O describes the growth rate of running time (or memory) as input size n approaches infinity. Constants, lower-order terms, and coefficients are dropped — only the dominant term matters.

An analogy: "Taipei to Kaohsiung." Walking 8h, biking 4h, driving 3h. Double the distance (Taipei to Kenting) and all three double. Big-O describes the shape of the curve — linear, log, or quadratic?

3.1 為何 Python/Java 都用 TimSort？

2002 年 Tim Peters 為 Python 設計了 TimSort：發現實務資料常有「已排序片段 (runs)」，於是混合 Merge + Insertion，並利用這些 runs 達到最壞 O(n log n)、近乎排序資料 O(n) 的成績。2003 進 Python，2009 進 Java——成為全球最常被執行的排序演算法之一。

In 2002, Tim Peters designed TimSort for Python: real-world data often has pre-sorted "runs," so he combined Merge + Insertion to exploit them — worst O(n log n), nearly-sorted O(n). Adopted by Python (2003) and Java (2009), it's now among the most-executed sorts on Earth.

3.2 Master Theorem（主定理）

對 分治法 (Divide & Conquer) 的遞迴 T(n) = a·T(n/b) + f(n)，主定理告訴你最終複雜度：

  • 若 f(n) = O(nlogₐb − ε)：T(n) = Θ(nlogₐb)
  • 若 f(n) = Θ(nlogₐb)：T(n) = Θ(nlogₐb · log n)
  • 若 f(n) = Ω(nlogₐb + ε)：T(n) = Θ(f(n))

例：MergeSort T(n) = 2T(n/2) + O(n) → a=2, b=2, log₂2 = 1，f(n)=n → 第 2 種 → O(n log n)。

For divide & conquer recurrence T(n) = a·T(n/b) + f(n), the Master Theorem gives the final complexity:

  • If f(n) = O(nlogₐb − ε): T(n) = Θ(nlogₐb)
  • If f(n) = Θ(nlogₐb): T(n) = Θ(nlogₐb · log n)
  • If f(n) = Ω(nlogₐb + ε): T(n) = Θ(f(n))

Example: MergeSort T(n) = 2T(n/2) + O(n) → a=2, b=2, log₂2 = 1, f(n)=n → case 2 → O(n log n).

演算法傳奇

四、五個演算法傳奇

① Knuth & TAOCP (1962–)

Donald Knuth 1962 年開始寫 The Art of Computer Programming (TAOCP)，原本估計 7 本書 5 年寫完。60 年後 (2026) 還沒寫完——目前只到 Volume 4A，4B 草稿進行中。Knuth 用 MIX 與 MMIX 假想機進行分析，提倡「先正確、再快速、最後優美」。1974 年圖靈獎得主。發明 LaTeX。

Donald Knuth began The Art of Computer Programming (TAOCP) in 1962, planning 7 volumes in 5 years. 60 years later (2026) it's still unfinished — currently at Volume 4A, with 4B in draft. Knuth analyzes via MIX & MMIX hypothetical machines. His motto: "correct first, fast next, beautiful last." 1974 Turing Award. Also invented LaTeX.

② Dijkstra 的咖啡杯啟示 (1968)

1956 年 Edsger Dijkstra 在阿姆斯特丹咖啡廳，未婚妻喝咖啡時，他 20 分鐘 在腦中想出了「最短路徑演算法」。沒寫筆記，三年後才整理成論文 (1959)。他常說：「電腦科學跟天文學一樣，跟望遠鏡無關。」1972 年圖靈獎。Dijkstra 演算法仍是 GPS 導航、網路路由的底層。

1956, Edsger Dijkstra in an Amsterdam café — while his fiancée drank coffee, he mentally derived the shortest-path algorithm in 20 minutes. No notes; he published it three years later (1959). Famous quote: "Computer science is no more about computers than astronomy is about telescopes." 1972 Turing Award. Dijkstra's algorithm still powers GPS routing and network routing.

③ Smith-Waterman & Needleman-Wunsch

1970 年 Saul Needleman & Christian Wunsch 提出全域比對 DP；1981 年 Temple Smith & Michael Waterman 改成「局部比對」——只比對最相似的片段。這兩篇 DP 演算法奠定了現代生物序列比對的基礎，是 BLAST、BWA、minimap2 的數學根源。Waterman 也是「人類基因組計畫」的關鍵推手。

In 1970, Needleman & Wunsch proposed global-alignment DP; in 1981 Smith & Waterman adapted it for local alignment — matching the most-similar subregions. These two DP algorithms underpin all modern bio sequence alignment — the math behind BLAST, BWA, minimap2. Waterman was also key to the Human Genome Project.

④ Burrows-Wheeler Transform (1994)

1994 年 Michael Burrows & David Wheeler 在 DEC SRC 報告中發表 BWT——一種可逆的字串重排，把相似字元聚集，極利壓縮。它先成為 bzip2 的核心；2009 年 Heng Li 用它做出 BWA——把人類基因組索引壓到記憶體可載入，每次查詢 O(m)。Wheeler 也是 1951 年寫出第一個 subroutine 的人。

In 1994 Burrows & Wheeler at DEC SRC published BWT — a reversible permutation that clusters similar characters, enabling great compression. First powered bzip2; in 2009 Heng Li used it to build BWA — indexing the human genome into RAM with O(m) per query. Wheeler also wrote the first subroutine in 1951.

⑤ Hopcroft-Karp & Ford-Fulkerson

1956 年 Ford & Fulkerson 發表「最大流量」演算法，1973 Hopcroft & Karp 改進二部圖匹配到 O(√V·E)。這些演算法看似抽象，卻在現代單細胞配對 (CellPhoneDB)、RNA-seq read assignment、蛋白質-蛋白質交互作用網絡都有重要應用。

In 1956 Ford & Fulkerson introduced max-flow; in 1973 Hopcroft & Karp improved bipartite matching to O(√V·E). These abstract algorithms power modern single-cell matching (CellPhoneDB), RNA-seq read assignment, and protein-protein interaction networks.

⑥ Tim Roughgarden's Selfish Routing (2005)

2005 年 Stanford Tim Roughgarden 用博弈論解析「自私路由」——當每個用戶都選最快路徑，總體流量會比中央調度多 33%（Braess 悖論）。獲 Gödel 獎。同樣的數學被用在 internet BGP、城市交通、藥物分子網路。

In 2005, Stanford's Tim Roughgarden used game theory on "selfish routing" — when each user picks their fastest path, total flow is 33% worse than central scheduling (Braess's paradox). Gödel Prize. Same math applies to internet BGP, city traffic, and drug-molecule networks.

🧬 給生物資訊學家

六、🧬 生資連結：序列比對演算法全景

這一節是整本書的核心之一。幾乎所有 NGS 工具的速度突破，都源自巧妙的演算法選擇。

1. 動態規劃 (DP) — 序列比對的數學原點

Needleman-Wunsch (1970, global) 與 Smith-Waterman (1981, local) 都是 O(m·n) 的二維 DP，建構 m×n 矩陣，每格填入「最佳子問題解」。優點：保證找到最佳。缺點：對 30 億 bp 人類基因組，DP 需 9×10¹⁸ 格——根本算不完。所以實務工具用 啟發式 (heuristic) 加速。

2. BLAST (1990) — 啟發式 + k-mer 種子

BLAST 算法核心：① 把 query 切成 k-mer (k=11 for nucleotide)；② 在 DB 中找完全匹配 (種子)；③ 對種子做局部 DP 延伸。比 Smith-Waterman 快 50× 但只「近似最佳」。BLAST、PSI-BLAST、DELTA-BLAST 仍是 GenBank 標準。

3. BWA (2009) — Burrows-Wheeler Transform + FM-Index

BWT 把 reference 重排成可壓縮形式，搭配 FM-Index 達到「O(m) 查 read」。記憶體需求從 30+ GB 降到 3 GB。BWA-MEM (2013, Li) 是目前短 read 比對的事實標準。

4. STAR (2013) — Suffix Array + Maximal Mappable Prefix

RNA-seq 有 splice junction 跨越外顯子的問題。STAR 用 Suffix Array 索引 + MMP (Maximal Mappable Prefix) 演算法找最長匹配後跳到下個外顯子。Dobin et al. 2013 Bioinformatics。

5. minimap2 (2018) — Sketch + Chain + Base-level alignment

Heng Li 的 minimap2 對長 read (PacBio/Nanopore) 用 minimizer sketch + chaining + 局部 DP。比 BWA 快 30×，是長 read 比對的金標準。

6. Greedy 演算法 — OTU clustering

CD-HIT (Li & Godzik 2006) 與 UCLUST (Edgar 2010) 是貪婪式 clustering：拿一條 sequence 當「代表」，若新 sequence 與它相似度 >97% 就歸這群。簡單但實用，OTU 微生物分類常用。

7. Felsenstein Pruning — 系統發生樹的 DP

1981 年 Joseph Felsenstein 提出「pruning algorithm」算 likelihood：對樹從葉子往根遞迴，每節點存子樹條件機率。複雜度 O(n) 而非 O(2ⁿ)。是 RAxML、IQ-TREE、MrBayes 的底層。

8. HMM Viterbi — 基因預測與蛋白質結構域

Hidden Markov Model 的 Viterbi 演算法 (1967, Andrew Viterbi) 也是 DP，找出最可能的 state 路徑。GENSCAN (Burge 1997) 用 HMM 預測基因；HMMER3 (Eddy 2011) 用 profile HMM 在蛋白質找 domain (Pfam)。

This section is one of the book's cornerstones. Nearly every NGS speed breakthrough comes from a clever algorithm choice.

1. Dynamic Programming (DP) — the math origin

Needleman-Wunsch (1970, global) and Smith-Waterman (1981, local) are both O(m·n) 2D DP, building an m×n matrix where each cell holds the "best sub-problem solution." Pro: guaranteed optimal. Con: for the 3 Gbp human genome, DP needs 9×10¹⁸ cells — uncomputable. Hence the move to heuristics.

2. BLAST (1990) — heuristic + k-mer seeds

Core idea: (1) chop query into k-mers (k=11 for nucleotides); (2) find exact matches in DB (seeds); (3) extend with local DP. 50× faster than Smith-Waterman but "approximately optimal." BLAST, PSI-BLAST, DELTA-BLAST remain the GenBank standard.

3. BWA (2009) — Burrows-Wheeler Transform + FM-Index

BWT reorders the reference into a compressible form; with FM-Index, reads are queried in O(m). Memory drops from 30+ GB to ~3 GB. BWA-MEM (2013, Li) is the de facto short-read aligner.

4. STAR (2013) — Suffix Array + Maximal Mappable Prefix

RNA-seq has splice junctions across exons. STAR indexes with a Suffix Array and uses MMP (Maximal Mappable Prefix) to find the longest hit, then jumps to the next exon. Dobin et al. 2013 Bioinformatics.

5. minimap2 (2018) — sketch + chain + base-level align

Heng Li's minimap2 handles long reads (PacBio/Nanopore) via minimizer sketches + chaining + local DP. 30× faster than BWA — the long-read gold standard.

6. Greedy — OTU clustering

CD-HIT (Li & Godzik 2006) and UCLUST (Edgar 2010) use greedy clustering: pick a representative; if a new sequence is >97% similar, add to the group. Simple but effective, standard for OTU classification.

7. Felsenstein Pruning — phylogenetic-tree DP

In 1981 Joseph Felsenstein proposed the "pruning algorithm" for likelihood: recursively from leaves to root, each node stores subtree conditional probabilities. O(n), not O(2ⁿ). Powers RAxML, IQ-TREE, MrBayes.

8. HMM Viterbi — gene prediction & protein domains

The Hidden Markov Model's Viterbi algorithm (1967, Andrew Viterbi) is also DP, finding the most-likely state path. GENSCAN (Burge 1997) uses HMM for gene prediction; HMMER3 (Eddy 2011) uses profile HMM for protein domains (Pfam).

🎯
記住這條原則：從 DP (精確但慢) → 種子+延伸 (BLAST) → BWT/FM-Index (索引壓縮) → minimizer (sketch) — 生資演算法 35 年來都在用 「索引 + 啟發式」 來壓垮原本不可能的計算量。
The 35-year trajectory of bio algorithms: DP (exact but slow) → seed+extend (BLAST) → BWT/FM-Index (index compression) → minimizers (sketches) — always "index + heuristic" to tame impossible problems.

🌳 演算法選擇樹

Q1:
需要排序？→ 用語言內建 (sorted() / sort())——已經是 TimSort 或最佳實作。千萬別自己寫 BubbleSort 用在 production。
Q2:
圖最短路徑？→ 邊有權重 → Dijkstra (O((V+E) log V))；負權 → Bellman-Ford；所有對 → Floyd-Warshall (O(V³))；無權重 → BFS。
Q3:
序列比對？→ 短 read → BWA-MEM / Bowtie2；長 read → minimap2；RNA-seq → STAR / HISAT2 / salmon；蛋白質 → DIAMOND 或 BLAST。
Q4:
需要分群 (clustering)？→ 已知 k → k-means (O(nki))；不知 k → DBSCAN / HDBSCAN；單細胞 → Leiden (Traag 2019)。
Q5:
找最佳路徑遊戲 AI？→ 啟發式好 → A*；雙人零和 → Minimax + alpha-beta；複雜 → MCTS。
Q6:
最佳子結構問題（背包、編輯距離）？→ 動態規劃 (DP)。先寫遞迴 + memoization，再改 tabulation。
Q7:
n > 10⁸ 不確定能否跑完？→ 先算複雜度。O(n²) 已不行；考慮 近似演算法 (approximation) 或 隨機演算法 (randomized)。
Q1:
Need to sort? → Use the language's built-in — it's TimSort or equally optimal. Never roll your own BubbleSort in production.
Q2:
Shortest path in a graph? → Weighted edges → Dijkstra; negative weights → Bellman-Ford; all pairs → Floyd-Warshall; unweighted → BFS.
Q3:
Sequence alignment? → Short reads → BWA-MEM / Bowtie2; long reads → minimap2; RNA-seq → STAR / HISAT2 / salmon; protein → DIAMOND or BLAST.
Q4:
Clustering? → Known k → k-means; unknown k → DBSCAN / HDBSCAN; single-cell → Leiden (Traag 2019).
Q5:
Game-AI pathing? → Good heuristic → A*; two-player zero-sum → Minimax + alpha-beta; complex → MCTS.
Q6:
Optimal-substructure problem (knapsack, edit distance)? → Dynamic programming. Start with recursion + memoization, then move to tabulation.
Q7:
n > 10⁸ and unsure it'll finish? → Check complexity first. O(n²) won't fly; consider approximation or randomized algorithms.

八、動手寫：Merge Sort 三語

# Merge Sort: O(n log n) 保證最壞 / guaranteed worst-case
def merge_sort(arr: list) -> list:
    if len(arr) <= 1: return arr
    mid = len(arr) // 2
    left  = merge_sort(arr[:mid])     # T(n/2)
    right = merge_sort(arr[mid:])     # T(n/2)
    return _merge(left, right)         # O(n)

def _merge(a, b):
    out, i, j = [], 0, 0
    while i < len(a) and j < len(b):
        if a[i] <= b[j]:
            out.append(a[i]); i += 1
        else:
            out.append(b[j]); j += 1
    out.extend(a[i:]); out.extend(b[j:])
    return out

# 實務：用內建 sorted() / sort() (TimSort)
sorted([3,1,4,1,5,9,2,6])  # [1, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9]
# Merge Sort in R
merge_sort <- function(arr) {
  if (length(arr) <= 1) return(arr)
  mid <- length(arr) %/% 2
  left  <- merge_sort(arr[1:mid])
  right <- merge_sort(arr[(mid+1):length(arr)])
  .merge(left, right)
}

.merge <- function(a, b) {
  out <- integer(length(a) + length(b))
  i <- 1; j <- 1; k <- 1
  while (i <= length(a) && j <= length(b)) {
    if (a[i] <= b[j]) { out[k] <- a[i]; i <- i+1 }
    else            { out[k] <- b[j]; j <- j+1 }
    k <- k+1
  }
  if (i <= length(a)) out[k:(k+length(a)-i)] <- a[i:length(a)]
  if (j <= length(b)) out[k:(k+length(b)-j)] <- b[j:length(b)]
  out
}

// Quick Sort: O(n log n) avg, O(n²) worst
function QuickSort(arr, lo, hi):
    if lo < hi:
        p = Partition(arr, lo, hi)
        QuickSort(arr, lo, p − 1)
        QuickSort(arr, p + 1, hi)

function Partition(arr, lo, hi):
    pivot = arr[hi]                // 取最後一個為 pivot
    i = lo − 1
    for j from lo to hi − 1:
        if arr[j] <= pivot:
            i = i + 1
            swap(arr[i], arr[j])
    swap(arr[i+1], arr[hi])
    return i + 1
minimap2 的演算法堆疊

八-B、minimap2 演算法堆疊案例

Heng Li 2018 年 Bioinformatics 發表的 minimap2 是長 read 比對的事實標準。看看它如何巧妙地疊加四種演算法達到 BWA-MEM 30× 的速度：

  1. 第一層：Minimizer Sketch (Roberts 2004)。把 read 切成 k-mer (k=15)，每個 window (w=10) 內挑出字典序最小的——只存「sketch」而非所有 k-mer。記憶體降為 1/w，且兩個 read overlap 時 minimizer 大致對齊。
  2. 第二層：Hash Table Lookup。Reference 的所有 minimizer 預先索引到 hash table。對每個 query minimizer，O(1) 找到候選位置。遠勝 Suffix Array 的 cache-miss 問題。
  3. 第三層：Chaining (Dynamic Programming)。把多個 minimizer hit 用 DP 串成 chain，找出最佳 chain（共線性 + 不重疊）。這是 minimap2 的核心創新——傳統 BLAST 只看單一 seed，minimap2 看全域結構。
  4. 第四層：Base-level Alignment (Affine-Gap DP)。只在 chain 兩端做精確比對（避免對整條 read 跑 DP）。用 SIMD-加速的 Smith-Waterman（KSW2 演算法）。

速度來源分析：sketch 減 90% 計算 + hash 加速查找 + chain 避免重複 DP + SIMD 加速最後比對 → 4 個 10-30% 改進相乘 = 整體 30× 加速。這就是「演算法分層設計」的力量。

啟示：單一演算法很少能解決真實問題——必須堆疊 4-6 種演算法+資料結構，每層處理一個瓶頸。BWA、STAR、salmon、Cellranger 都是類似多層架構。學會「拆解工具用了哪些演算法」是進階生資人的必備技能。

Heng Li's minimap2 (Bioinformatics 2018) is the long-read alignment standard. Watch how it cleverly stacks four algorithms to achieve 30× BWA-MEM speed:

  1. Layer 1: Minimizer Sketch (Roberts 2004). Slice the read into k-mers (k=15); within each window (w=10) keep only the lexicographically smallest — storing a "sketch" instead of all k-mers. Memory drops to 1/w; minimizers from overlapping reads roughly align.
  2. Layer 2: Hash Table Lookup. Pre-index all reference minimizers into a hash table; each query minimizer finds candidates in O(1). Beats Suffix Array's cache-miss problem.
  3. Layer 3: Chaining (Dynamic Programming). DP-link multiple minimizer hits into chains, picking the best (collinear + non-overlapping). minimap2's core innovation — classical BLAST sees only single seeds; minimap2 sees global structure.
  4. Layer 4: Base-level Alignment (Affine-Gap DP). Run exact alignment only at chain endpoints (skipping DP over the whole read), using SIMD-accelerated Smith-Waterman (the KSW2 algorithm).

Where the speed comes from: sketch cuts 90% of work + hash speeds lookup + chain avoids redundant DP + SIMD speeds final alignment → four 10-30% wins compound to 30× overall. That's the power of "layered algorithmic design."

Takeaway: One algorithm rarely solves a real problem — stack 4-6 of them, each handling one bottleneck. BWA, STAR, salmon, Cellranger follow the same multi-layer pattern. Learning to decompose a tool's algorithmic stack is an essential intermediate-bioinformatics skill.

常見誤解

九、常見誤解

❌ 「O(n²) 永遠慢於 O(n log n)」

常數和小 n 都有差。對 n=20，Insertion Sort (O(n²)) 經常比 Merge Sort 快 2–5×，因為常數小、cache friendly。所以 TimSort 對小子陣列直接切換 Insertion。Big-O 是 n→∞ 的漸近行為，不是「永遠」。

Constants and small n matter. For n=20, Insertion Sort (O(n²)) often beats Merge Sort 2–5× — small constants, cache-friendly. That's why TimSort switches to Insertion for small subarrays. Big-O is the asymptote for n→∞, not "always."

❌ 「演算法重要 > 資料結構」

它們是一體的。Dijkstra 用 array 是 O(V²)，用 binary heap 是 O((V+E) log V)，用 Fibonacci heap 是 O(E + V log V)。同一個演算法，搭不同資料結構，複雜度完全不同。

They're two sides of one coin. Dijkstra with an array is O(V²); with a binary heap O((V+E) log V); with a Fibonacci heap O(E + V log V). Same algorithm, different data structure, completely different complexity.

❌ 「BLAST 找到的是最佳比對」

BLAST 是 啟發式 (heuristic)，不保證最佳——可能漏掉真正最相似的 hit (false negative)。要保證最佳必須用 Smith-Waterman，但慢 50×。BLAST e-value < 1e-5 通常足夠，但發表前要驗證關鍵 hit。

BLAST is heuristic, not guaranteed optimal — it can miss the truly best hit (false negative). Guaranteed optimal requires Smith-Waterman, which is 50× slower. BLAST e-value < 1e-5 usually suffices, but verify key hits before publishing.

❌ 「複雜度等於跑得多快」

Big-O 不考慮：常數、cache、SIMD、平行化、IO。Numpy 的 O(n²) 矩陣相乘比純 Python 的 O(n²·⁸¹) Strassen 還快——因為 BLAS 用 SIMD + cache 把常數壓到極致。實務效能 = 複雜度 × 常數 × cache × 平行。

Big-O ignores: constants, cache, SIMD, parallelism, IO. NumPy's O(n²) matmul beats pure-Python O(n²·⁸¹) Strassen because BLAS crushes constants with SIMD + cache. Real perf = complexity × constants × cache × parallelism.

Amortized Analysis

九-A、均攤分析

「每次操作 O(1)」與「均攤 (amortized) O(1)」常被搞混。均攤的意思：單次操作可能慢，但對 n 次操作取平均後仍是 O(1)。

經典例子：Python list 的 append。list 內部是動態陣列，預留空間滿時會 重新配置成兩倍大（O(n) 操作）。但這個「擴容」發生的頻率是 log n 次，總工作量約 1 + 2 + 4 + ... + n/2 + n = 2n，所以 n 次 append 總共做 2n 的工作 → 均攤 O(1)。

三種分析方法：① Aggregate analysis（總和除以 n）、② Accounting method（為便宜操作「預付」）、③ Potential method（定義勢能函數 Φ）。Sleator & Tarjan 1985 的 splay tree 證明就用 potential method。

生資對應：① Bloom filter 在 n 次 insert 後可能需要 resize（少見但可能）；② Python list 收基因組座標時動態擴容；③ collections.deque 兩端 O(1) 也是均攤；④ hash table 的 load factor > 0.75 時 rehash 整個表是 amortized O(1) per insert。

"O(1) per op" and "amortized O(1)" are easy to confuse. Amortized means: any single op may be slow, but averaged over n ops it's still O(1).

Classic example: Python list append. Internally a dynamic array; when capacity is full, it reallocates to 2× size (an O(n) op). But this growth happens log n times, total work is 1 + 2 + 4 + ... + n = 2n. So n appends cost 2n total → amortized O(1).

Three analysis techniques: (1) Aggregate (sum / n), (2) Accounting (prepay cheap ops), (3) Potential method (define a potential function Φ). Sleator & Tarjan 1985 used the potential method to prove splay trees' bounds.

Bioinformatics mapping: (1) Bloom filters may resize after n inserts (rare); (2) Python list collecting genomic coordinates grows amortized; (3) collections.deque is amortized at both ends; (4) hash-table rehash when load factor > 0.75 is amortized O(1) per insert.

隨機演算法

九-B、隨機演算法

有時隨機性比確定性更強——這是 1970s 以後最重要的演算法觀念之一。兩大派系：

  • Las Vegas 演算法：答案永遠正確，但執行時間是隨機變數。例：隨機化 Quicksort——選 pivot 時隨機抽，最壞 O(n²) 但機率極低，平均 O(n log n) 「幾乎確定」達成。
  • Monte Carlo 演算法：執行時間有界，但答案有小機率錯。例：Miller-Rabin 質數測試——多次測試後錯誤率 (1/4)ᵏ → 跑 30 次幾乎不可能誤判。

2026 年實務的隨機演算法應用：

  • Reservoir Sampling (Vitter 1985)：從未知大小的串流均勻抽 k 個樣本，記憶體 O(k)。FASTQ 抽樣、Twitter 即時流分析必備。
  • MinHash + LSH (Broder 1997)：估計兩集合 Jaccard 相似度。mash (Ondov 2016) 用此演算法做基因組距離計算，比 ANI 快 1000×。
  • Skip List (Pugh 1989)：機率資料結構，期望 O(log n) 但實作比 RB Tree 簡單一半。Redis sorted set 用此。
  • HyperLogLog (Flajolet 2007)：用 12 KB 記憶體估計 10⁹ 個不同元素的數量，誤差 < 2%。Google Bigtable、Redis、生資的 k-mer 計數都用。
  • Bloom filter 本身就是 Monte Carlo（有 false positive 但不會 false negative）。

哲學啟示：「Trade probability for performance」是 21 世紀演算法的核心觀念之一。完美正確不一定是必要——可控的小錯誤率換來巨大速度提升，往往更實用。

Sometimes randomness beats determinism — one of the most important algorithmic ideas post-1970s. Two families:

  • Las Vegas: always correct, but runtime is a random variable. Example: randomized Quicksort — random pivot makes worst-case O(n²) vanishingly rare; expected O(n log n) "almost surely."
  • Monte Carlo: bounded runtime, small chance of wrong answer. Example: Miller-Rabin primality — error rate (1/4)ᵏ after k trials; 30 trials makes mistakes essentially impossible.

2026 practical applications:

  • Reservoir Sampling (Vitter 1985): uniformly sample k items from an unknown-length stream in O(k) memory. Essential for FASTQ sub-sampling and live Twitter analytics.
  • MinHash + LSH (Broder 1997): estimate Jaccard similarity between sets. mash (Ondov 2016) uses this for genome distances, 1000× faster than ANI.
  • Skip List (Pugh 1989): probabilistic, expected O(log n), half the code of an RB Tree. Redis sorted sets use it.
  • HyperLogLog (Flajolet 2007): estimate 10⁹ distinct elements in 12 KB, <2% error. Google Bigtable, Redis, and bio k-mer counters all use it.
  • Bloom filters are themselves Monte Carlo (false positives possible, never false negatives).

Philosophical takeaway: "Trade probability for performance" is a 21st-century algorithmic core idea. Perfect correctness isn't always required — a controllable small error rate for massive speed is often more useful.

近似演算法 vs NP-hard

九-C、近似演算法

許多生資問題是 NP-hard（最佳解需指數時間），例：

  • Multiple Sequence Alignment (MSA)：精確解是 NP-hard。Clustal Omega、MAFFT、T-Coffee 都是啟發式近似。
  • Phylogenetic tree (Maximum Parsimony / ML)：搜尋空間 (2n−5)!! 超指數。RAxML、IQ-TREE 用啟發式搜尋。
  • Genome assembly：完整 Hamiltonian path 是 NP-complete。SPAdes / Canu 都是近似。
  • Protein folding：Levinthal's paradox。AlphaFold 用深度學習近似。

近似演算法的「近似比」：算法產出 A，最佳解 OPT。比例 ρ = A/OPT。設計目標是 有理論保證 的 ρ。例：

  • Vertex Cover 2-approx：簡單貪婪可保證 ρ ≤ 2。
  • TSP (歐式) Christofides 1.5-approx：1976 至今最佳，2020 才有人改進到 1.5−ε。
  • k-means：實際上是 NP-hard，Lloyd's algorithm 是局部最佳近似。

PTAS / FPTAS：Polynomial Time Approximation Scheme，可參數化讓 ρ → 1，代價是執行時間隨 ε⁻¹ 增加。Knapsack 有 FPTAS，TSP 沒有（除非 P=NP）。

Many bioinformatics problems are NP-hard (exact solution needs exponential time):

  • Multiple Sequence Alignment (MSA): exact is NP-hard. Clustal Omega, MAFFT, T-Coffee are heuristic approximations.
  • Phylogenetic tree (MP / ML): search space is (2n−5)!! super-exponential. RAxML / IQ-TREE use heuristic search.
  • Genome assembly: Hamiltonian path is NP-complete. SPAdes / Canu approximate it.
  • Protein folding: Levinthal's paradox. AlphaFold approximates via deep learning.

Approximation ratios: algorithm outputs A, optimum is OPT. Ratio ρ = A/OPT. Goal: provable ρ bound. Examples:

  • Vertex Cover 2-approx: simple greedy gives ρ ≤ 2.
  • TSP (Euclidean) Christofides 1.5-approx: standard since 1976, only improved to 1.5−ε in 2020.
  • k-means: NP-hard; Lloyd's algorithm is a local-optimum approximation.

PTAS / FPTAS: Polynomial Time Approximation Scheme — ρ → 1 as runtime grows with ε⁻¹. Knapsack has an FPTAS, TSP does not (unless P=NP).

線上演算法與串流

九-D、線上 & 串流演算法

離線演算法：先看完全部資料再決定。線上演算法：每讀一筆就要決策，沒有重來機會（如電梯排程、cache 替換、廣告競價）。

競爭比 (Competitive ratio)：線上演算法 / 離線最佳值。經典結果：LRU cache 是 k-competitive（k = cache size）。Move-to-Front linked list 是 2-competitive。

串流模型 (Streaming)：資料是一條 stream（如 ONT nanopore 即時 read、Twitter firehose），總量大到記憶體放不下，單次掃過、用次線性記憶體。

2026 生資的串流演算法應用：

  • Nanopore real-time base-calling：Dorado / Bonito 邊讀邊呼，不能等讀完整個 read。
  • Adaptive sampling：ONT「Read Until」決定當下要不要繼續測這條 read，10 秒內決策。
  • Streaming variant calling：Clair3 / DeepVariant 的串流版本邊讀 BAM 邊 call。
  • Online k-mer counting (KMC3, Jellyfish)：用 disk-backed hash 處理超大 FASTQ。
  • Misra-Gries / Count-Min Sketch：找 frequent k-mer 而不存所有 k-mer。

Offline algorithms: see all data before deciding. Online algorithms: each item arrives and must be acted on immediately, no re-do (elevator scheduling, cache replacement, ad bidding).

Competitive ratio: online / offline-optimum. Classic: LRU cache is k-competitive (k = cache size); Move-to-Front list is 2-competitive.

Streaming model: data flows past as a stream (ONT nanopore reads, Twitter firehose), too big for RAM — one pass, sublinear memory.

2026 bioinformatics streaming applications:

  • Nanopore real-time basecalling: Dorado / Bonito call bases as the read arrives — cannot wait for the full read.
  • Adaptive sampling: ONT "Read Until" decides whether to keep reading within 10 seconds.
  • Streaming variant calling: streaming Clair3 / DeepVariant call variants as BAM is read.
  • Online k-mer counting (KMC3, Jellyfish): disk-backed hash handles massive FASTQ.
  • Misra-Gries / Count-Min Sketch: find frequent k-mers without storing all of them.
並行演算法與 GPU

九-E、並行演算法 + GPU

Amdahl's Law (1967)：speedup = 1 / (s + p/N)，s 是 serial 部分。即使 N→∞，speedup 不會超過 1/s。啟示：若 10% 程式碼是 serial，無論用 1000 個核心都最多加速 10×。

Gustafson's Law (1988)：當問題規模隨核心數成長時，可達近線性 speedup。對「大資料」（單細胞 100 萬細胞、basecalling）此規律才適用。

2026 GPU 在生資的應用：

  • DeepVariant / Clair3：variant calling 用 CNN，GPU 比 CPU 快 10-50×。
  • AlphaFold 3 (2024)：蛋白質結構預測，必須 GPU。
  • Dorado (ONT basecalling)：CPU 5 days vs GPU 5 hours。
  • NVIDIA Parabricks：BWA + GATK 整套 NGS pipeline GPU 版，全流程從 30 小時降到 30 分鐘。
  • RAPIDS cuML / scanpy-GPU (rapids-singlecell)：單細胞分析 GPU 化，10-100× 加速。
  • scGPT / Geneformer / scFoundation：細胞基礎模型訓練需 H100 GPU cluster。

注意：GPU 不是萬靈丹——data transfer (CPU↔GPU) 常常成為瓶頸，且 GPU 對 branch-heavy 演算法（如 BWA-MEM）表現不佳。是否該用 GPU？問題要「data parallel + 大矩陣運算 + 同 kernel 重複執行」三要素到齊。

Amdahl's Law (1967): speedup = 1 / (s + p/N); s is the serial fraction. Even with N→∞, speedup is capped at 1/s. Takeaway: if 10% of your code is serial, 1000 cores still cap speedup at 10×.

Gustafson's Law (1988): when problem size grows with cores, near-linear speedup is achievable. Applies to "big data" (1M single cells, basecalling).

2026 GPU in bioinformatics:

  • DeepVariant / Clair3: CNN variant calling, GPU is 10-50× faster than CPU.
  • AlphaFold 3 (2024): protein structure prediction, GPU required.
  • Dorado (ONT basecalling): CPU 5 days vs GPU 5 hours.
  • NVIDIA Parabricks: GPU-accelerated BWA + GATK pipeline, 30 hours → 30 minutes end-to-end.
  • RAPIDS cuML / scanpy-GPU (rapids-singlecell): GPU single-cell analysis, 10-100× speedup.
  • scGPT / Geneformer / scFoundation: cell foundation models need H100 GPU clusters.

Caveat: GPU isn't magic — CPU↔GPU data transfer often becomes the bottleneck, and GPUs fare poorly on branch-heavy algorithms like BWA-MEM. When to GPU? Need all three: data-parallel + large matrix ops + same-kernel repetition.

💭 延伸思考

十、延伸思考

  1. P = NP 問題若被解開，會發生什麼？所有 NP 問題（蛋白質摺疊、藥物分子最佳化、旅行推銷員、整數因數分解）都會變多項式時間可解。RSA 加密當天崩潰，所有比特幣錢包可被破解，但同時所有疾病的最佳治療方案、最佳蛋白質結構都能算出來。Clay 數學機構懸賞 100 萬美金未解。
  2. What if P = NP gets resolved? All NP problems (protein folding, drug optimization, TSP, integer factorization) become polynomial-time. RSA collapses on day one, all Bitcoin wallets crackable — but also: optimal disease treatments, optimal protein structures all computable. Clay Math Institute's $1M prize remains open.
  3. 量子演算法對生物資訊有何衝擊？Shor (1994) 可在多項式時間做整數因數分解；Grover (1996) 可把資料庫搜尋從 O(n) 降到 O(√n)。對 BLAST 這種「找最相似序列」，Grover 加速可能是 1,000× 級別。但目前量子位元數仍遠不足以執行——2026 IBM 1,121 qubits 還在實驗階段。
  4. Will quantum algorithms reshape bioinformatics? Shor (1994) factors integers in polynomial time; Grover (1996) cuts DB search from O(n) to O(√n). For BLAST-style "find most similar," Grover could yield 1000× speedups. But current qubit counts are far too small — IBM's 1,121-qubit machine (2026) is still experimental.
  5. LLM 寫的演算法是否真的「新」？2023 DeepMind 的 AlphaTensor 找出比 Strassen 還快的 4×4 矩陣相乘演算法；2024 FunSearch 找到新的 cap set 上界。AI 已能發現新演算法——但驗證、解釋、推廣仍需要人類數學家。
  6. Are LLM-generated algorithms really "new"? In 2023 DeepMind's AlphaTensor found a faster-than-Strassen 4×4 matmul; in 2024 FunSearch found new cap-set bounds. AI can now discover new algorithms — but verification, explanation, and generalization still need human mathematicians.
