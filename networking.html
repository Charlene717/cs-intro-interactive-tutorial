電腦網路

從 1969 年第一個「LO」訊息，到你今天用 prefetch 從 NCBI 下載 50 GB FASTQ——本章解開 OSI/TCP-IP 分層、三次握手、HTTP/1→2→3、DNS 與 CDN，以及 bioinformatician 必懂的 Aspera 與 Globus。

From the first "LO" message in 1969 to prefetch-ing 50 GB FASTQ from NCBI today — this chapter unpacks OSI/TCP-IP layers, the 3-way handshake, HTTP/1→2→3, DNS and CDN, plus the Aspera and Globus tools every bioinformatician should know.

"LO" 與網路的誕生

1969 年 10 月 29 日：'LO' 然後當機

1969 年 10 月 29 日 22:30，UCLA 的研究生 Charley Kline 坐在一台 SDS Sigma 7 電腦前，準備透過 ARPANET 連到 480 公里外的 Stanford 研究所 (SRI)。他打算發送 "LOGIN"，登入遠端機器跑程式。他在電話另一頭跟 SRI 的工程師 Bill Duvall 確認："你看到 L 了嗎？" "看到了。" "看到 O 嗎？" "看到了。" 接著 Kline 打了 G——系統當機。

就這樣，網際網路的第一個訊息是 "LO"——意外但詩意（"lo and behold" 的開頭）。一個小時後系統恢復，Kline 成功完成登入。ARPANET（美國國防部高等研究計畫署網路）這個只有 4 個節點的雛形，56 年後變成連結 50 億人、傳輸 100 ZB/年的全球網際網路。

本章帶你看：① OSI 7 層 vs TCP/IP 4 層怎麼把「複雜的網路」拆成可管理的抽象？② TCP 的 3-way handshake 為何需要 3 步而不是 2 步？③ HTTP/1 → /2 → /3 怎麼一路演進到「跑在 UDP 上」？④ 對 bioinformatician 最切身：為何下載 NCBI FASTQ 要用 Aspera 而不只是 HTTP？跨機構傳 1 TB 資料用 Globus 有什麼魔法？

October 29, 1969, 22:30 — UCLA grad student Charley Kline sat at an SDS Sigma 7, preparing to connect over ARPANET to SRI 480 km away at Stanford. He intended to send "LOGIN" and log into the remote machine. He confirmed with SRI's Bill Duvall by phone: "Did you get the L?" "Got it." "Got the O?" "Got it." Then Kline typed G — the system crashed.

So the internet's first message was "LO" — accidental but poetic (the opening of "lo and behold"). An hour later the system was back and Kline finished the login. ARPANET — the US ARPA's 4-node prototype — became, 56 years later, the global internet connecting 5 billion people and moving 100 ZB/year.

This chapter: (1) how do OSI's 7 layers vs TCP/IP's 4 tame "the network" into manageable abstractions? (2) Why does TCP's 3-way handshake need 3 steps, not 2? (3) How did HTTP/1 → /2 → /3 end up running on UDP? (4) For bioinformaticians: why use Aspera, not just HTTPS, for NCBI FASTQ? What magic lets Globus ship 1 TB across institutions?

💡
核心觀念：網路是「每層只解決一個小問題」的分層工程——物理層管電壓、IP 層管位址、TCP 層管可靠傳輸、HTTP 層管應用語意。一層只與相鄰層對話，這就是工程能在 56 年內把「兩台電腦聊天」擴展成「全球 5 G 個裝置互聯」的祕密。 Core idea: The network is layered engineering — each layer solves one small problem. Physical layer handles voltage; IP layer handles addressing; TCP handles reliable transport; HTTP handles application semantics. Layers only talk to neighbors. That's how 56 years of engineering scaled "two computers chatting" to "5 billion devices interconnected."
OSI vs TCP/IP

一、核心概念：分層架構

OSI Model（ISO 1984 正式發布）是理想化的 7 層教學模型；TCP/IP Model（DoD 1980s）是實際運作的 4 層工程模型——後者勝出。每一層只看下面提供的服務、只給上面一個更高階的抽象。

The OSI model (ISO 1984) is the idealized 7-layer teaching model; the TCP/IP model (DoD 1980s) is the 4-layer engineering model that actually runs the internet. Each layer consumes the one below and exposes a cleaner abstraction above.

IPv4 vs IPv6

IPv4 用 32 bit 表達位址（4 G 個），1993 就不夠了，靠 NAT 撐到現在。IPv6 用 128 bit（足夠給地球每粒沙都有 10^18 個位址）。2026 全球 IPv6 流量占 45%。

IPv4 uses 32 bits (4 G addrs) — ran out by 1993, propped up by NAT. IPv6 uses 128 bits (enough for 10^18 addrs per grain of sand). 2026: ~45% of global traffic is IPv6.

🔁

TCP：可靠傳輸

連線導向、有序、可靠、有流量與壅塞控制。HTTP/HTTPS、SSH、FTP 都用 TCP。代價：3-way handshake、ACK 重傳、慢啟動。

Connection-oriented, ordered, reliable, with flow & congestion control. HTTP/HTTPS, SSH, FTP all use TCP. Costs: 3-way handshake, ACK retransmits, slow start.

⚡

UDP：快但不可靠

無連線、不重傳、不保證順序。用於 DNS（單包問答）、VoIP、視訊串流、線上遊戲——丟一兩包比延遲幾百毫秒好。QUIC/HTTP/3 也用 UDP 為底層。

Connectionless, no retries, no ordering. Used by DNS (single-packet Q&A), VoIP, video, games — better to drop a packet than wait 100s of ms. QUIC/HTTP/3 also runs on UDP.

3-way handshake / 壅塞控制 / DNS

三、深入剖析

3.1 3-way handshake

TCP 建立連線的步驟：

  1. SYN（Client → Server）：「我想連你，我的初始序號是 X」
  2. SYN-ACK（Server → Client）：「OK，我的初始序號是 Y，確認你的 X+1」
  3. ACK（Client → Server）：「確認你的 Y+1，開始傳資料」

為何不是 2 步？2 步無法解決「雙向同意 + 序號協商」這件事。即使 client 對 server 說了 SYN，server 也需要確認「自己的 SYN-ACK 真的被 client 收到」——這就是第 3 步的 ACK。少了它，server 不知道 client 是否在線，可能無止盡重送 SYN-ACK。為何不是 4 步？因為 server 的 ACK + SYN 可以合併成一個 SYN-ACK 封包，省一次 RTT。

SYN flood 攻擊：駭客發送大量假 SYN 不發 ACK，讓 server 的半開連線佇列爆滿，拒絕真實使用者。緩解：SYN cookies。

TCP connection setup:

  1. SYN (Client → Server): "I want to connect; my initial seq is X."
  2. SYN-ACK (Server → Client): "OK, my seq is Y, ack your X+1."
  3. ACK (Client → Server): "Ack your Y+1, sending data."

Why not 2 steps? Because two-way agreement + sequence-number negotiation needs both sides to confirm receipt. Even after client SYN, the server must know "my SYN-ACK reached the client" — that's step 3. Without it, the server might retransmit SYN-ACK forever. Why not 4? Because the server's ACK + SYN can be coalesced into one SYN-ACK packet, saving an RTT.

SYN flood attack: send tons of fake SYNs without ACKs to fill the half-open queue and deny real users. Mitigation: SYN cookies.

3.2 壅塞控制

網際網路的「沒有中央調度」是它規模擴展的祕密，但這也代表大家共用線路時必須自律。TCP 用「窗口大小 (congestion window, cwnd)」控制一次飛在路上的封包數：

  • Slow start：cwnd 從 1 開始，每收 1 個 ACK 加倍——指數成長到偵測到擁塞。
  • Congestion avoidance：之後線性成長。
  • Loss detection：丟包 → cwnd 折半（Reno）或調整數學模型（Cubic、預設於 Linux）。
  • BBR（Google 2016）：不依賴丟包當訊號，而是估計頻寬與 RTT 直接逼近最優點。YouTube 流量起初試用，2024 後廣泛部署。

The internet has no central scheduler — that's why it scales — but it also means everyone must self-throttle on shared links. TCP uses a congestion window (cwnd) to cap in-flight packets:

  • Slow start: cwnd starts at 1; doubles on each ACK — exponential until congestion is detected.
  • Congestion avoidance: then linear growth.
  • Loss detection: drop → halve cwnd (Reno) or adjust by a math model (Cubic, default on Linux).
  • BBR (Google 2016): doesn't use loss as a signal; estimates bandwidth and RTT directly. First trialed on YouTube; widespread post-2024.

3.3 DNS 解析

當你 curl https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov：

  1. 瀏覽器先查本地 DNS cache、再查 OS resolver cache。
  2. 沒命中 → 問你 ISP 的 recursive resolver（如 8.8.8.8 Google DNS、1.1.1.1 Cloudflare）。
  3. Resolver 先問 root server（13 組）「.gov 在哪？」→ 回傳 .gov 的 TLD server。
  4. 問 .gov TLD server「nih.gov 在哪？」→ 回 nih.gov 的 authoritative NS。
  5. 問 nih.gov NS「ncbi.nlm.nih.gov 的 A record？」→ 回 IP（如 130.14.29.110）。
  6. 結果快取（TTL 通常 300 s–1 day），下次免再查。

整個流程通常 < 50 ms。DNS over HTTPS (DoH) 與 DNS over TLS (DoT) 加密 DNS 查詢，2020+ 主流瀏覽器預設啟用——隱私改善，但 ISP/IT 部門難做網域過濾。

When you curl https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov:

  1. Browser checks its DNS cache, then the OS resolver cache.
  2. Miss → ask your ISP's recursive resolver (e.g. 8.8.8.8 Google, 1.1.1.1 Cloudflare).
  3. Resolver asks a root server (13 sets): "where is .gov?" → returns the .gov TLD server.
  4. Asks .gov TLD: "where is nih.gov?" → returns nih.gov's authoritative NS.
  5. Asks nih.gov NS: "A record for ncbi.nlm.nih.gov?" → returns the IP (e.g. 130.14.29.110).
  6. Caches the result (TTL usually 300 s–1 day).

Usually < 50 ms end-to-end. DNS over HTTPS (DoH) and DNS over TLS (DoT) encrypt DNS queries — defaults in browsers post-2020. Privacy gain, but domain filtering by ISP/IT gets harder.

3.4 CDN

當你在台灣下載一個由美國伺服器提供的 200 MB 檔案，跨太平洋光纖 RTT 約 150 ms——你的下載 throughput 受限於 TCP 窗口 / RTT，連 1 GB 寬頻都跑不滿。CDN (Content Delivery Network) 在全球部署數千個 edge servers，把熱資料 cache 在離你 30 ms 以內的節點。原理：DNS 解析時根據你的 IP 回傳離你最近的 edge IP（anycast routing）。

Downloading a 200 MB file from a US server in Taiwan crosses the Pacific (~150 ms RTT) — your throughput is capped by TCP window / RTT, can't saturate even 1 Gbps. A CDN (Content Delivery Network) deploys thousands of edge servers worldwide and caches hot content within 30 ms of you. Mechanism: DNS responses are personalized to return the nearest edge IP (anycast).

五個關鍵時刻

四、五個關鍵時刻

1969：ARPANET

1969 年 10 月，ARPANET 連起 UCLA、SRI、UCSB、Utah 四個節點，使用 IMP (Interface Message Processor) 路由器 + NCP 協定（後被 TCP/IP 取代）。第一筆訊息 "LO" 後 1 小時系統復原；3 年後 ARPANET 公開展示 23 個節點；1973 年 Vint Cerf 與 Bob Kahn 開始設計 TCP/IP——他們因此後來被稱為「網際網路之父」。

October 1969 — ARPANET connected UCLA, SRI, UCSB, and Utah using IMP routers (Interface Message Processor) + NCP (later replaced by TCP/IP). First message "LO" — system recovered in an hour. 3 years later ARPANET demoed 23 nodes publicly. In 1973 Vint Cerf and Bob Kahn started designing TCP/IP — later called the "Fathers of the Internet."

1983：Flag Day

1983 年 1 月 1 日，整個 ARPANET 強制從 NCP 切換到 TCP/IP——當天全網一次性升級，沒有過渡期，這就是「Flag Day」。這是史上最大的一次「協定大遷徙」，所有 400 多個節點必須同時準備好。今天網路規模太大，再也不可能 Flag Day——這也是 IPv6 過了 30 年仍未完全取代 IPv4 的根本原因。

January 1, 1983 — the entire ARPANET forcibly switched from NCP to TCP/IP overnight, no transition period — the famous "Flag Day". The largest protocol migration ever; all 400+ nodes had to be ready simultaneously. The internet is now too big for another Flag Day — that's the fundamental reason IPv6 hasn't fully replaced IPv4 after 30 years.

1989：WWW 誕生

1989 年 3 月，英國工程師 Tim Berners-Lee 在歐洲核子研究組織 (CERN) 寫了一份 13 頁的提案《Information Management: A Proposal》，主管 Mike Sendall 在封面寫下「Vague, but exciting」（模糊但令人興奮）批准之。Berners-Lee 隨後發明了 HTTP、HTML、URL——構成 World Wide Web 的三大支柱。1991 年 8 月 6 日他發布世界第一個網站（info.cern.ch，仍可訪問）。他選擇不申請專利、讓 WWW 永遠開放——這個決定塑造了現代互聯網的開放精神。

March 1989 — British engineer Tim Berners-Lee at CERN wrote a 13-page proposal, "Information Management: A Proposal." His manager Mike Sendall scrawled "Vague, but exciting" on the cover and approved it. Berners-Lee invented HTTP, HTML, and URL — WWW's three pillars. On August 6, 1991, he published the world's first website (info.cern.ch, still online). He chose not to patent it, keeping WWW open forever — that decision shaped the modern internet's open ethos.

2011：IPv6 為何尚未取代 IPv4？

2011 年 6 月 8 日，Google、Facebook、Yahoo 等大廠同步啟用 IPv6 一整天，測試問題。2012 年 IPv6 Launch Day 正式永久啟用。然而 15 年後，IPv4 仍佔大半流量。為何？因為 IPv6 與 IPv4 「不直接相容」（不是 IPv4 的超集合），雙方必須跑 dual-stack 或 NAT64 翻譯。家用路由器、企業防火牆、舊系統升級成本巨大——沒有公司願意當第一個只跑 IPv6 的人。NAT 又把 IPv4 壽命「不必要地」延長了。

June 8, 2011 — Google, Facebook, Yahoo enabled IPv6 for a day to test. June 6, 2012 was the permanent IPv6 Launch Day. Yet 15 years later IPv4 still carries most traffic. Why? IPv6 is not backward-compatible with IPv4 (it's not a superset) — both must run dual-stack or NAT64 translation. Home routers, firewalls, legacy systems all need upgrades. No one wants to be the first IPv6-only site. And NAT extends IPv4's life "unnecessarily."

2022：BGP 大停擺

2022 年 6 月 21 日，Cloudflare 工程師對 BGP (邊界閘道協定) 路由設定的一句修改，意外把全球 19 個資料中心從網路上「撤出」。Cloudflare 服務 1/5 全球網站，Discord、Shopify、Coinbase 同時下線約 75 分鐘。原因：BGP 的「信任模型」——所有運營商互相通告自己擁有哪些 IP 段，網路相信你說的。一個錯誤通告就能黑洞掉巨量流量。BGP 是 1989 年設計的「臨時方案」，至今未被取代——網際網路的根基比想像中脆弱。

June 21, 2022 — a Cloudflare engineer's one-line BGP route change accidentally withdrew 19 global data centers from the internet. Cloudflare serves 1/5 of the web; Discord, Shopify, Coinbase went down for ~75 minutes. Cause: BGP's trust model — every ISP announces what IP ranges it owns, and the rest of the world believes you. One bad announcement black-holes massive traffic. BGP was designed in 1989 as a "temporary" solution; never replaced — the internet's foundations are more fragile than they look.

五、互動：子網路計算器

輸入 CIDR 區塊（例如 192.168.0.0/24）自動算 network、broadcast、可用主機。CIDR 是 IPv4 的延命神器。

給生物資訊學家

六、🧬 給生物資訊學家

🧬
① 從 NCBI SRA 下載 FASTQ

NCBI SRA 用 SRA 壓縮格式（私有 FASTQ 容器），不能直接 wget。必須 prefetch SRR123456 + fasterq-dump SRR123456。fasterq-dump（2018+ 取代 fastq-dump）多執行緒解壓快 5–10 倍。一個 50 GB FASTQ：prefetch 20 分 + dump 30 分。

NCBI SRA uses the SRA compressed format (proprietary FASTQ container). Use prefetch SRR123456 then fasterq-dump SRR123456. fasterq-dump (2018+) is 5–10× faster than fastq-dump. 50 GB FASTQ: ~20 min prefetch + 30 min dump.

🧬
② Aspera：UDP-based 高速傳輸

下載 1 TB 跨太平洋 HTTPS 受 TCP 窗口+RTT 限制，可能只跑 50 MB/s。Aspera (ascp) 用 IBM FASP 協定跑在 UDP 上，自己做可靠性，不受 TCP 慢啟動束縛，可塞滿 1 Gbps 光纖。NCBI、EBI、DDBJ、Broad 都有 Aspera 端點。免費替代：UDT、BBCP、GridFTP。

1 TB cross-Pacific HTTPS is capped by TCP window+RTT — maybe 50 MB/s. Aspera (ascp) uses IBM FASP over UDP with its own reliability — bypasses TCP slow start, saturates 1 Gbps fiber. NCBI, EBI, DDBJ, Broad provide Aspera endpoints. Free alternatives: UDT, BBCP, GridFTP.

🧬
③ REST API 自動化

想知道 BRCA1 在小鼠基因組位置？用 Ensembl REST：https://rest.ensembl.org/lookup/symbol/mus_musculus/Brca1，2 秒回傳。Ensembl REST 提供 100+ endpoints；NCBI E-utilities 老牌但仍主力。實務：API 有 rate limit（Ensembl 15 req/s），超過會被 ban；用 time.sleep(0.1)。

Want BRCA1 in mouse? Ensembl REST: https://rest.ensembl.org/lookup/symbol/mus_musculus/Brca1 — 2 second response. 100+ endpoints. NCBI E-utilities is older but still core. APIs have rate limits (Ensembl 15 req/s); use time.sleep(0.1).

🧬
④ Globus 跨機構傳輸

把 1 TB 從台大 HPC 傳到 Harvard FAS RC，scp 一週也未必成功（斷網重來）。Globus（UChicago + Argonne）做 fire-and-forget：選 source/dest endpoint，自動 GridFTP + 續傳 + checksum + email 通知。學術界 99% HPC 都有 Globus endpoint，是 ENCODE/TCGA 跨機構合作的事實標準。

Moving 1 TB from NTU HPC to Harvard FAS RC via scp may take a week with retries. Globus (UChicago + Argonne) is fire-and-forget — pick endpoints, get auto GridFTP + resume + checksum + email. 99% of academic HPCs have Globus endpoints — de facto standard for ENCODE/TCGA collaborations.

🧬
⑤ 雲端流量費的陷阱

上傳 1 TB 到 S3：免費。下載回本地：約 \$90 美元（egress \$0.09/GB）。學術界吃這個悶虧——上雲後發現下載不起。AWS 2024 推出 Open Data Sponsorship；Cloudflare R2、Backblaze B2 完全免 egress。建議：① 評估 egress；② 用 AWS Snowball 物理寄硬碟（>100 TB 比網路便宜）；③ 在雲端做分析，只下結果。

Upload 1 TB to S3: free. Download to local: ~\$90 (egress \$0.09/GB). Academia stung — upload, then can't afford download. AWS Open Data Sponsorship (2024) + Cloudflare R2 + Backblaze B2 are zero-egress. Tips: (1) estimate egress, (2) AWS Snowball physical drives for >100 TB, (3) compute in-cloud, download only results.

九、常見誤解

❌ IPv6 一定取代 IPv4

真相：IANA 中央池 2011 耗盡，但 NAT 把 IPv4 壽命延長 30+ 年。15 年後 IPv4 仍佔大半流量。IPv6 取代是「何時」不是「會不會」，可能 20–30 年慢慢過渡。

Truth: IANA pool exhausted 2011, but NAT extended IPv4 30+ years. After 15 years IPv4 still dominates. IPv6 replacement is "when" not "if" — likely 20–30-year transition.

❌ HTTP/3 = HTTPS

真相：HTTPS 是「HTTP over TLS」（加密層）；HTTP/3 是「HTTP over QUIC over UDP」——換掉了底層 TCP！HTTP/3 必須加密，所以「HTTP/3」≈「最新 HTTPS」但是不同層次。

Truth: HTTPS = "HTTP over TLS" (encryption); HTTP/3 = "HTTP over QUIC over UDP" (replaces TCP!). HTTP/3 mandates encryption, so HTTP/3 ≈ latest HTTPS — but different layers.

❌ 頻寬大一定快

真相：下載 = min(頻寬, TCP 窗口/RTT)。台灣 1 Gbps 到美國 RTT 150 ms，預設 64 KB 窗口時上限只有 427 KB/s。要開 TCP window scaling + BBR。

Truth: Throughput = min(bandwidth, TCP window/RTT). 1 Gbps Taiwan → US with 150 ms RTT and 64 KB window caps at 427 KB/s. Need TCP window scaling + BBR.

❌ HTTPS 絕對安全

真相：HTTPS 只保證傳輸途中加密 + 伺服器經 CA 認證。不保證：伺服器不洩密、不被釣魚域名（ncbı.nlm.nih.gov）、瀏覽器沒被 MITM。資安是縱深防禦。

Truth: HTTPS only guarantees in-transit privacy + CA-verified server. Doesn't prevent: server breach, phishing domains (ncbı.nlm.nih.gov), MITM-installed certs. Security is defense-in-depth.

💭 延伸思考

十、延伸思考

  1. 衛星網路取代海底光纜？
    2026 年 Starlink 已部署 7000+ 顆衛星。光在真空中比光纖快 50%，倫敦-紐約 RTT 衛星 40 ms vs 光纖 76 ms——金融高頻交易先採用。但骨幹 99% 仍跑海底光纜（每條 100+ Tbps）。衛星硬限：軌道擁擠、空間碎片、容量低於單根光纜。對 bioinformatics 國際合作（NIH-EMBL 分享），會混合架構嗎？ By 2026 Starlink has 7000+ satellites. Light is 50% faster in vacuum than fiber: London-NY 40 ms (sat) vs 76 ms (fiber). High-frequency trading adopted first. But 99% of backbone is ~530 undersea cables (100+ Tbps each). Limits: orbital congestion, space debris, capacity. For NIH-EMBL data sharing, will we see hybrid architectures?
  2. DNS over HTTPS 影響隱私？
    傳統 DNS 是明文 UDP；DoH 藏 DNS 在 HTTPS 中。看似隱私改善，實則所有 DoH 流量集中流向 Google/Cloudflare——你的瀏覽歷史不再被 ISP 看見，但全集中到大廠手裡。企業/學校/醫院 DNS-based 過濾被破壞。隱私 vs 中央化，是進步嗎？ Classic DNS is plaintext UDP; DoH hides queries in HTTPS. Seems privacy-positive, but all DoH traffic concentrates on Google/Cloudflare — ISPs no longer see, but Big Tech does. Enterprise/school/hospital DNS filtering breaks. Privacy vs centralization — is that progress?
