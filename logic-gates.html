數位邏輯與布林代數

22 歲的 Claude Shannon 證明：所有運算——包括 AI 推論、bedtools intersect、SQL 查詢——都能拆解為 AND、OR、NOT 三個閘的組合。這是「20 世紀最重要的碩士論文」。

A 22-year-old Claude Shannon proved that every operation — AI inference, bedtools intersect, SQL queries — can be decomposed into AND, OR, NOT. The thesis behind it is "the most important master's thesis of the 20th century."

1937 年的一頁紙

一、Shannon 1937：一頁紙改變世界

1937 年夏天，22 歲的 Claude Shannon 在 MIT 完成了一份 72 頁的碩士論文：《A Symbolic Analysis of Relay and Switching Circuits》。論文的核心觀念簡單到令人錯愕——他發現 80 年前 George Boole 寫的布林代數（純抽象的 0/1 邏輯），可以直接對應電話交換機裡的繼電器電路。

這意味著什麼？意味著所有邏輯運算都可以用電線、開關來實作。意味著 1+1、True AND False、染色體區間 intersect、ChatGPT 的注意力機制——本質上都是同一件事：在電路中傳遞 0 與 1。

Howard Gardner 後來寫道：「這也許是 20 世紀最重要、也是最有名的碩士論文。」沒有 Shannon 的這 72 頁紙，就沒有今天的數位電腦。順帶一提，11 年後 Shannon 又單槍匹馬發明了資訊理論——還有第一個下西洋棋的電腦程式，與一個會雜耍的機械手臂。

Summer 1937. A 22-year-old Claude Shannon finished a 72-page master's thesis at MIT: A Symbolic Analysis of Relay and Switching Circuits. The idea was almost embarrassingly simple — Boolean algebra, written 80 years earlier by George Boole as pure 0/1 logic, maps directly onto telephone-relay switching circuits.

What did this mean? Every logical operation could be implemented with wires and switches. 1+1, True AND False, chromosome-interval intersection, ChatGPT's attention mechanism — all the same thing at heart: pushing 0s and 1s through circuits.

Howard Gardner later called it "possibly the most important — and famous — master's thesis of the 20th century." Without those 72 pages, no digital computer would exist. Side note: 11 years later, Shannon single-handedly invented information theory — plus the first chess program and a juggling robot.

💡
核心觀念：邏輯閘是「輸入 0/1 → 輸出 0/1」的最小運算單元。每個閘的行為由真值表 (truth table) 完整定義，與實作媒介無關（繼電器、真空管、電晶體、量子位元皆可）。 Core idea: A logic gate is the smallest operation: 0/1 inputs → 0/1 output. Its behavior is fully captured by a truth table, independent of medium (relay, vacuum tube, transistor, qubit — all valid).

That's why NAND-gate arrays are the most mass-produced IC building blocks — and why SSD "NAND flash" got its name (cell structure resembles a NAND gate).

布林代數五大定律

四、布林代數五大定律

布林代數的化簡規則讓我們可以把複雜的邏輯式減化為更少的閘——這是邏輯閘合成 (logic synthesis) 的基礎，省晶片面積、降功耗。EDA 軟體 (Synopsys Design Compiler、Cadence Genus) 每天為全球晶片廠完成這項工作。

Boolean simplification reduces complex expressions to fewer gates — the foundation of logic synthesis, saving die area and power. EDA tools (Synopsys Design Compiler, Cadence Genus) do this daily for every chip in the world.

De Morgan 證明（直觀版）：「A 與 B 都不開」等同於「A 沒開 或 B 沒開」嗎？不對——「都不開」應該是「A 沒開 且 B 沒開」。「A 與 B 不都開」（即 ¬(A·B)）才等同於「A 沒開 或 B 沒開」（¬A + ¬B）。把它寫成真值表逐行驗證即可。

Karnaugh map 化簡：把真值表畫成 2D 表格（橫軸 A、縱軸 B），合併相鄰的「1」變成更大的方塊，就能找出最簡布林式。對於 4 變數以下非常實用，5+ 變數則交給 Quine-McCluskey 演算法或 EDA 工具。

De Morgan intuition: Is "neither A nor B is on" the same as "A is off OR B is off"? No — "neither" means "A is off AND B is off." It's "NOT both A and B on" (¬(A·B)) that equals "A is off OR B is off" (¬A + ¬B). Verify line by line in a truth table.

Karnaugh maps: draw the truth table as a 2D grid (A horizontal, B vertical), merge adjacent 1s into larger rectangles, and read off the simplest expression. Great for ≤4 variables; for ≥5 use the Quine-McCluskey algorithm or EDA tools.

⚠️
De Morgan 在程式中也常出現：條件 !(a && b) 等同於 !a || !b。重構巢狀 if 語句時非常實用。
SQL 應用：WHERE NOT (age < 18 AND gender = 'M') 等同於 WHERE age >= 18 OR gender != 'M'——後者通常 query planner 跑得更快。
De Morgan in code: !(a && b) equals !a || !b. Indispensable for refactoring nested ifs.
SQL example: WHERE NOT (age < 18 AND gender = 'M') = WHERE age >= 18 OR gender != 'M' — the latter often runs faster in query planners.
半加器 → 全加器 → 32-bit

五、加法器：算術就是邏輯

單個位元加法只有四種輸入組合：

  • 0+0 = 0（和=0，進位=0）
  • 0+1 = 1（和=1，進位=0）
  • 1+0 = 1（和=1，進位=0）
  • 1+1 = 10（和=0，進位=1）

和 (Sum) = A XOR B；進位 (Carry) = A AND B——這就是半加器 (Half Adder)。把兩個半加器加一個 OR 閘串接，就能處理「上一位的進位」，成為全加器 (Full Adder)。把 32 個全加器串聯，便是 32-bit 整數加法器——CPU 裡的 ALU 核心。每天你電腦做的每一次加法都走過這條路徑。

Adding one bit at a time has only four cases:

  • 0+0 = 0 (sum=0, carry=0)
  • 0+1 = 1 (sum=1, carry=0)
  • 1+0 = 1 (sum=1, carry=0)
  • 1+1 = 10 (sum=0, carry=1)

Sum = A XOR B; Carry = A AND B — a half adder. Chain two half adders + an OR gate for a full adder (handles carry-in). Cascade 32 full adders → 32-bit integer adder — the heart of every ALU. Every add your computer performs walks this path.

三個有趣故事

六、三個有趣故事

📚 Boole 1854：被當哲學書

1854 年 George Boole 出版《思維法則的研究 (An Investigation of the Laws of Thought)》，建立布林代數。當時的書商把它放在哲學區——因為「思維法則」聽起來太抽象，沒人想到它會在 80 年後驅動每一台計算機。Boole 1864 年因為被妻子用冷水「治療」感冒而病逝，享年 49 歲。妻子的「水療法」哲學至今被認為是 19 世紀醫學奇譚。

In 1854, George Boole published An Investigation of the Laws of Thought, founding Boolean algebra. Booksellers shelved it under philosophy — "Laws of Thought" sounded too abstract for anyone to imagine it would drive every computer 80 years later. Boole died at 49 in 1864 after his wife "cured" his cold by dousing him with cold water — a Victorian medical curiosity.

🤹 Shannon 的怪嗜好

Shannon 1937 寫完碩士論文，1948 寫資訊理論《A Mathematical Theory of Communication》。但他下班後愛做的事是：① 設計能解開 Rubik's Cube 的機器人；② 寫第一個下西洋棋的電腦程式 (1950)；③ 造能自動跑出迷宮的機械老鼠 "Theseus"；④ 在 MIT 走廊裡騎著單輪車雜耍。同事說：「Shannon 是史上唯一會邊騎單輪車邊雜耍邊推導定理的人。」

Shannon wrote his thesis in 1937 and A Mathematical Theory of Communication in 1948. After hours he: (1) built a Rubik's-Cube solving robot; (2) wrote the first computer chess program (1950); (3) built "Theseus," a mechanical mouse that solved mazes; (4) rode a unicycle while juggling in the MIT corridors. Colleagues said: "Shannon was the only person to derive theorems while unicycling and juggling at the same time."

🔌 Intel 4004 (1971)

1971 年 Intel 4004——史上第一顆商用微處理器——含 2,300 個電晶體，組成約 數百顆等效 NAND 閘，剛好夠造一台計算機。設計者 Federico Faggin 用矽閘 (silicon gate) 技術，4 位元、108 KHz。2025 年 NVIDIA Blackwell 有 2080 億顆電晶體——是 4004 的 9000 萬倍。但本質上，它們做的事一樣：傳遞 0 和 1。

The 1971 Intel 4004 — the first commercial microprocessor — packed 2,300 transistors forming a few hundred NAND-equivalent gates: just enough for a calculator. Federico Faggin's silicon-gate design ran 4 bits at 108 kHz. In 2025, NVIDIA Blackwell has 208 billion transistors — 90 million times more. But fundamentally, both push 0s and 1s through gates.

🧪 量子閘的差異

古典邏輯閘是「不可逆」的：知道輸出 Y 無法反推 (A, B)，因為兩個輸入位元被合併成一個輸出。量子閘必須可逆——例如 Hadamard 閘 把 \|0⟩ 變成 (\|0⟩+\|1⟩)/√2 的疊加態，CNOT 閘 製造糾纏。Toffoli 閘 (CCNOT) 是「可逆古典 NAND」——但需要 3 個輸入 3 個輸出。所有古典電路理論上都能轉換為可逆量子電路。

Classical gates are irreversible: given output Y, you can't recover (A, B) because two input bits merge into one output. Quantum gates must be reversible — e.g., the Hadamard gate turns \|0⟩ into the superposition (\|0⟩+\|1⟩)/√2; the CNOT gate creates entanglement. The Toffoli (CCNOT) is a "reversible classical NAND" — but needs 3 inputs and 3 outputs. Every classical circuit can in principle be made reversible.

給生資人

八、🧬 給生物資訊學家

🧬
給生物資訊學家：你每天都在做布林運算

你每次寫 bedtools intersect、SELECT WHERE、檢查 SAM flag，都是在做布林代數。理解 AND/OR/NOT 與 De Morgan 定律能讓你寫出更快、更正確的生資管線。

Every bedtools intersect, SELECT WHERE, and SAM-flag check you write is Boolean algebra. Understanding AND/OR/NOT + De Morgan makes your pipelines faster and more correct.

🧬 bedtools 是布林運算

把基因組想成「染色體 + 區間」的集合：

  • bedtools intersect -a A.bed -b B.bed = A AND B（兩者重疊區）
  • bedtools subtract -a A.bed -b B.bed = A AND NOT B（A 扣除 B）
  • cat A.bed B.bed | sort | bedtools merge = A OR B（聯集）
  • bedtools intersect -v -a A.bed -b B.bed = A AND NOT B（與 -v 相同效果）

這就是為何 Quinlan 2010 (Bioinformatics) 的 bedtools 成為生資基礎工具——它把布林代數搬到基因組座標上。

Treat the genome as a set of (chromosome, interval) pairs:

  • bedtools intersect -a A.bed -b B.bed = A AND B (overlap)
  • bedtools subtract -a A.bed -b B.bed = A AND NOT B
  • cat A.bed B.bed | sort | bedtools merge = A OR B (union)
  • bedtools intersect -v implements the NOT-B mask

That's why Quinlan 2010 (Bioinformatics) made bedtools a cornerstone — it lifts Boolean algebra to genome coordinates.

🚩 SAM flag：bitwise 布林

SAM/BAM 的第 2 欄 FLAG 是布林位元組合：

  • 0x1 (1) = paired
  • 0x4 (4) = unmapped
  • 0x10 (16) = reverse strand
  • 0x100 (256) = secondary
  • 0x400 (1024) = duplicate

判斷一條 read 是「成功對應的正向唯一比對」：(flag & 0x4) == 0 AND (flag & 0x10) == 0 AND (flag & 0x100) == 0。samtools view -F 0x904 用 Filter（NOT）一次排除 unmapped + secondary + duplicate。

The SAM/BAM FLAG (column 2) is a bitwise Boolean combo:

  • 0x1 (1) = paired
  • 0x4 (4) = unmapped
  • 0x10 (16) = reverse strand
  • 0x100 (256) = secondary
  • 0x400 (1024) = duplicate

To select "primary forward-mapped reads": (flag & 0x4) == 0 AND (flag & 0x10) == 0 AND (flag & 0x100) == 0. The handy samtools view -F 0x904 uses Filter (NOT) to exclude unmapped + secondary + duplicates in one shot.

🧮 SQL WHERE = 布林

查 TCGA：WHERE age > 50 AND (cancer_type = 'lung' OR cancer_type = 'breast') AND NOT (smoker = 'unknown')。每個 AND/OR/NOT 都是邏輯閘。Query planner 會根據布林代數定律（特別是 De Morgan 與分配律）重組你的查詢，以最少 disk I/O 跑出結果。明白這點，你就能寫出 planner 友善的查詢。

Query TCGA: WHERE age > 50 AND (cancer_type = 'lung' OR cancer_type = 'breast') AND NOT (smoker = 'unknown'). Every AND/OR/NOT is a logic gate. The query planner reorders these using Boolean laws (especially De Morgan and distributive) to minimize disk I/O. Knowing this helps you write planner-friendly queries.

🕸️ 布林網路：GRN 建模

Stuart Kauffman 1969 提出用布林網路建模基因調控網路 (GRN)：每個基因為一個節點 (ON/OFF)，連結代表調控，狀態根據鄰居的布林函數更新。簡單卻能重現細胞分化、振盪、穩態。今天 BoolNet (R 套件，Mussel 2010 Bioinformatics)、CellNOpt、PyBoolNet 都基於此。AlphaFold 雖更先進，但布林網路在「定性建模」與小型 GRN 解釋上仍是首選。

Stuart Kauffman in 1969 proposed Boolean networks for gene regulatory networks (GRN): each gene = a node (ON/OFF), edges = regulation, states updated via Boolean functions of neighbors. Simple, yet reproduces differentiation, oscillation, and steady states. Modern tools BoolNet (Mussel 2010 Bioinformatics), CellNOpt, PyBoolNet all build on this. AlphaFold is more advanced, but Boolean networks remain the go-to for qualitative modeling of small GRNs.

Q1:
「所有條件都需成立」→ 用 AND（門禁：刷卡 && 指紋 && 2FA；SAM filter primary mapped read）。
Q2:
「任一條件成立即可」→ 用 OR（通知：email OR SMS OR push；select cancer_type = 'lung' OR 'breast'）。

「兩者相等才放行」→ 用 XNOR（密碼比對、HMAC 相等性檢測）。
Q5:
「想用最少種類閘做所有事」→ 全用 NAND 或全用 NOR（IC 晶圓廠最愛）。
Q6:
「需要可逆運算（量子）」→ 用 Toffoli / Fredkin / Hadamard / CNOT。
Q1:
"All conditions must hold" → AND (door lock: card && fingerprint && 2FA; SAM filter for primary mapped).
Q2:
"Any one condition" → OR (notify via email OR SMS OR push; cancer_type = 'lung' OR 'breast').
Q3:
"Trigger when they differ" → XOR (parity, OTP encryption, diff detection, SNP vs reference).
Q4:
"Pass only when equal" → XNOR (password compare, HMAC equality).
Q5:
"Use the fewest gate types" → only NAND or only NOR (fab favorite).
Q6:
"Need reversible ops (quantum)" → Toffoli / Fredkin / Hadamard / CNOT.

# 位元運算子 / Bitwise operators: & | ^ ~ << >>
a, b = 0b1100, 0b1010     # 12, 10
print(bin(a & b))    # 0b1000 (AND)
print(bin(a | b))    # 0b1110 (OR)
print(bin(a ^ b))    # 0b0110 (XOR)
print(bin(~a & 0xF))  # 0b0011 (NOT, 4-bit masked)

# 半加器 / Half adder
def half_adder(a, b):
    return a ^ b, a & b      # (sum, carry)

# 全加器 / Full adder (a + b + cin)
def full_adder(a, b, cin):
    s1, c1 = half_adder(a, b)
    s2, c2 = half_adder(s1, cin)
    return s2, c1 | c2          # carry-out = OR of two carries

print(full_adder(1, 1, 1))  # (1, 1)  → 1+1+1 = 3 = 0b11

print(bool(flag & 0x4))   # unmapped? False

# De Morgan 驗證 / Verify De Morgan's law
for a in (0,1):
    for b in (0,1):
        assert (~(a & b) & 1) == ((~a & 1) | (~b & 1))

// 半加器 / Half Adder
function HalfAdder(A, B):
    Sum   = A XOR B
    Carry = A AND B
    return (Sum, Carry)

// 全加器 / Full Adder (handles carry-in)
function FullAdder(A, B, Cin):
    (S1, C1) = HalfAdder(A, B)
    (S2, C2) = HalfAdder(S1, Cin)
    return (S2, C1 OR C2)

// 32-bit ripple-carry adder
function Add32(A[0..31], B[0..31]):
    let carry = 0
    let result = new bit[32]
    for i from 0 to 31:
        (result[i], carry) = FullAdder(A[i], B[i], carry)
    return result

// bedtools intersect 概念 / bedtools intersect concept
function IntervalAnd(intervalsA, intervalsB):
    let result = empty
    for each a in intervalsA:
        for each b in intervalsB:
            if a.chr == b.chr AND a.end >= b.start AND b.end >= a.start:
                add (max(a.start, b.start), min(a.end, b.end)) to result
    return result
五個常見誤解

十一、常見誤解

🚨
誤解 #1：「OR 是排他的」——錯。一般 OR 是 inclusive OR：「至少一個為真」即輸出 1，包括兩者都真。exclusive OR 是 XOR。日常英文「coffee or tea」可能是 inclusive 或 exclusive，要靠上下文猜；但布林代數明確區分。
Myth #1: "OR is exclusive." Wrong. Boolean OR is inclusive: output 1 if at least one input is 1, including both. Exclusive-or is XOR. Natural English "coffee or tea" is ambiguous; Boolean is precise.
🚨
誤解 #2：「&& 與 & 一樣」——危險！&& 是邏輯（短路求值）：回傳 true/false，左邊為假就不再求值右邊；& 是位元 AND：對每個位元逐一運算且兩邊都會求值。if (ptr && ptr->x) 安全，if (ptr & ptr->x) 會 segfault。
Myth #2: "&& equals &." Dangerous! && is logical (short-circuit): returns true/false, skips RHS if LHS is false. & is bitwise: per-bit, evaluates both sides. if (ptr && ptr->x) safe; if (ptr & ptr->x) segfaults.
⚠️
誤解 #3：「NAND 是新發明的閘」——錯。NAND 在 1937 Shannon 論文中就已隱含。它與 NOR 是「萬用閘」的事實，1913 年 Henry Sheffer 已用 Sheffer stroke (∣) 證明過——其實是邏輯學家先發現，工程師後實用化。
Myth #3: "NAND is a new gate." Wrong. NAND is implicit in Shannon's 1937 thesis. Its universality was already proven in 1913 by Henry Sheffer (the "Sheffer stroke" ∣). Logicians discovered it before engineers used it.
⚠️
誤解 #4：「De Morgan 只有一條規則」——錯。De Morgan 有兩條對偶規則：① ¬(A·B) = ¬A + ¬B；② ¬(A+B) = ¬A · ¬B。記憶口訣：「外面的 NOT 鑽進去時，AND 變 OR、OR 變 AND」。
Myth #4: "De Morgan is one rule." Wrong. There are two dual rules: (1) ¬(A·B) = ¬A + ¬B; (2) ¬(A+B) = ¬A · ¬B. Mnemonic: when NOT goes inside, AND ↔ OR.
⚠️
誤解 #5：「ripple-carry 加法器跟 carry-lookahead 一樣快」——錯。32-bit ripple-carry 需 32 個閘延遲（O(n)）；carry-lookahead 用查表預先計算進位，僅 O(log n)。現代 CPU 使用 Kogge-Stone 或 Brent-Kung 等並行進位演算法，64-bit 加法只需約 6 級閘延遲。
Myth #5: "Ripple-carry adders are as fast as carry-lookahead." Wrong. 32-bit ripple-carry needs 32 gate delays (O(n)); carry-lookahead precomputes carries in O(log n). Modern CPUs use Kogge-Stone or Brent-Kung parallel-carry — 64-bit add takes ~6 gate delays.

十二、延伸思考

  1. 為何 NAND/NOR 可單獨組成所有閘？ 試證明 NAND 的功能完整性 (functional completeness)，並推導 NOR 的對偶證明。
  2. Why are NAND/NOR functionally complete? Prove NAND's completeness and derive the dual proof for NOR.
  3. 量子閘 (Hadamard, CNOT) 與古典閘的根本差別？ 為何古典閘可不可逆，量子閘卻必須可逆？這對演算法設計帶來什麼新可能（Shor 演算法、Grover 演算法）？
  4. How do quantum gates (Hadamard, CNOT) fundamentally differ from classical? Why must quantum gates be reversible? What does this enable (Shor's, Grover's algorithm)?
  5. 布林網路為何能建模 GRN？ 真實基因不只有 ON/OFF（mRNA、蛋白質有濃度）。Boolean 抽象的優缺點是什麼？何時該用 ODE / 隨機模型補充？
  6. Why do Boolean networks model GRNs? Real genes aren't strictly ON/OFF (concentrations matter). What are the trade-offs? When should ODEs / stochastic models complement?
  7. SAM flag 為何用 bitwise 而非 boolean array？ 1 個 uint16 vs. 16 個 bool 變數，記憶體與快取效率差多少？
  8. Why does SAM use bitwise FLAG rather than a boolean array? One uint16 vs. 16 booleans — what are the memory and cache trade-offs?
  9. 如果你要設計一個生資新格式（如 GFF4），會把布林屬性怎麼編碼？ 沿用 SAM bitwise flag 還是 JSON-like？各有優劣？
  10. If you design a new bio format (say GFF4), how would you encode booleans? SAM-style bitwise flag or JSON-style? Pros and cons?
