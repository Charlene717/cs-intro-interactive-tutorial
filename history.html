計算機發展簡史

從一位伯爵夫人的詩意預言到 ChatGPT——七十年來，計算機如何從房間大小的鋼鐵巨獸，演變為你口袋中比阿波羅登月電腦快一億倍的玻璃方塊？

From a Countess's poetic prophecy to ChatGPT — how did computers shrink from room-sized iron beasts to a glass slab in your pocket, 100 million times faster than the Apollo Moon-landing computer?

時光機：1843

一、1843：寫在尚未存在的機器上的程式

1843 年的倫敦冬天，27 歲的 Ada Lovelace（拜倫勛爵的女兒）伏案疾書，為一台從未被建造出來的機器——Charles Babbage 設計的分析機 (Analytical Engine)——寫下世界上第一個演算法：用機器計算 Bernoulli 數。她的「Note G」中包含 25 步驟的指令序列，後人尊稱她為「第一位程式設計師」。

更驚人的是 Ada 的預言：她在 Note A 中寫道，這台機器「終將能譜寫精緻、複雜程度任意的科學音樂」——比她預言的時代早了整整 130 年。1965 年，當第一個電腦合成音樂響起時，這預言才終於兌現。

但 Babbage 至死都沒能完成那台機器。他的圖紙裡有 25,000 個齒輪和 8 噸重的銅鐵——維多利亞時代的英國工業根本造不出來。直到 1991 年，倫敦科學博物館才依原圖造出 Difference Engine No. 2——它真的能運作。Babbage 領先時代 150 年。

London, winter of 1843. A 27-year-old Ada Lovelace (daughter of Lord Byron) hunched over her desk, writing the world's first algorithm — for a machine that did not yet exist. Charles Babbage's Analytical Engine was a paper dream of 25,000 brass gears, and Ada's Note G contained a 25-step program to compute the Bernoulli numbers. We honor her as the first programmer.

Even more astonishing was her prophecy: in Note A, she wrote that the machine "might compose elaborate and scientific pieces of music of any degree of complexity" — 130 years before the first computer-synthesized music played in 1965.

But Babbage died without finishing the machine. Victorian England's industry simply could not manufacture his 8-ton apparatus. Not until 1991 did London's Science Museum build Difference Engine No. 2 from his drawings — and it actually worked. Babbage was 150 years ahead of his time.

💡
核心觀念：「計算機」≠「電子產品」。任何能執行演算法、儲存資料、按指令動作的系統都算計算機——從算盤、機械齒輪、繼電器、真空管、電晶體、積體電路，到 2026 年的量子位元，都是同一條故事線。 Core idea: A "computer" is not just an electronic device. It is any system that runs algorithms, stores data, and acts on instructions — from abacuses to gears, relays, vacuum tubes, transistors, ICs, and now in 2026, quantum bits. It's all one story.
五千年的計算演化

二、從算盤到量子位元

整部計算機史可以濃縮為一個主題：如何把「狀態」儲存、操作、傳遞得越來越快、越來越小。每一次儲存媒介的突破，都把計算速度推進三到六個數量級。

The entire history of computing boils down to one theme: how to store, manipulate, and transmit "state" faster and smaller. Each leap in storage medium pushed compute speed by 3–6 orders of magnitude.

🧮 算盤

蘇美人發明，珠子代表狀態。今天仍在用——日本珠算冠軍能比計算機更快算心算除法。

Sumerians, ~2700 BC. Beads encode state. Still used today — Japanese soroban champions beat calculators at mental arithmetic.

⚙️ 帕斯卡計算器

19 歲的 Blaise Pascal 為當稅務官的父親設計，能加減六位數。是史上第一台量產的機械計算機。

Built by 19-year-old Blaise Pascal to help his tax-collector father. Six-digit add/subtract. First mass-produced mechanical calculator.

📜 提花織機

用打孔卡編程紡織圖案——史上第一台「可編程」機器，直接啟發了 Babbage 與後來的 IBM 打孔卡。

Programmed weaving patterns with punched cards — the first "programmable" machine. Directly inspired Babbage and later IBM's punch cards.

🏛️ 分析機

Babbage 設計，含 mill (CPU) + store (記憶體) + 卡片讀取 (I/O)，是現代計算機的祖先。Ada 為它寫了第一個程式。

Babbage's design with mill (CPU) + store (memory) + card reader (I/O). The ancestor of every modern computer. Ada wrote its first program.

🧠 圖靈機

不是實體機器，而是「什麼是可計算？」的數學模型。所有現代計算機在邏輯上等價於圖靈機。

Not a physical machine — a mathematical model of "what is computable?" Every modern computer is logically equivalent to a Turing machine.

⚡ ENIAC

賓州大學造，17,468 顆真空管，重 30 噸，佔地 167 m²。每秒 5,000 次加法——比當時的人類「計算員」快 1,000 倍。

UPenn, 1945. 17,468 vacuum tubes, 30 tons, 167 m². 5,000 adds/sec — 1,000× faster than the human "computers" it replaced.

📡 電晶體

Bell Labs 的 Bardeen、Brattain、Shockley 發明（1956 諾貝爾物理獎）。取代真空管：體積小 1000 倍、耗能少 100 倍、壽命長 1000 倍。

Bell Labs' Bardeen, Brattain, Shockley (1956 Nobel Prize in Physics). Replaced vacuum tubes — 1000× smaller, 100× less power, 1000× longer life.

🔬 積體電路

Jack Kilby（TI）與 Robert Noyce（Fairchild）幾乎同時發明：把多顆電晶體刻在同一塊矽片上，啟動半導體革命。

Jack Kilby (TI) & Robert Noyce (Fairchild) almost simultaneously: etch many transistors on one silicon die. Sparked the semiconductor revolution.

🪐 量子位元

Google Sycamore (2019)、IBM Heron (2023)、Willow (2024)：用疊加態同時表達 0 和 1，挑戰古典計算的極限。

Google Sycamore (2019), IBM Heron (2023), Willow (2024): qubits use superposition to be 0 and 1 at once, pushing past classical limits.

塑造 CS 的十個人

四、十位先驅

🌹 Ada Lovelace (1815–1852)

第一位程式設計師；預言「機器將能譜曲」。美國國防部 1980 年程式語言「Ada」以她命名。

First programmer; foresaw computers composing music. The U.S. DoD's 1980 language "Ada" is named after her.

🧮 Charles Babbage (1791–1871)

差分機與分析機設計者。生前未完成；1991 年依原圖造出 Difference Engine No. 2，運作完美。

Designer of the Difference & Analytical Engines. Never finished; Difference Engine No. 2 built from his drawings in 1991 worked perfectly.

🧠 Alan Turing (1912–1954)

圖靈機、圖靈測試、二戰破解 Enigma 拯救千萬生命。1952 因同性戀被英國政府化學閹割，1954 自殺；2013 才獲女王赦免。

Turing machine, Turing test, WWII Enigma-cracking that saved millions. Chemically castrated by the UK for homosexuality in 1952; suicide 1954; royal pardon 2013.

📡 Claude Shannon (1916–2001)

「資訊理論之父」。1948 定義「位元 (bit)」與通道容量公式。順帶發明了第一個西洋棋程式與雜耍機器人。

Father of information theory. Defined the "bit" in 1948 along with the channel capacity formula. Also built the first chess program and juggling robots.

🏛️ John von Neumann (1903–1957)

1945 EDVAC 報告提出「儲存程式概念」：程式即資料。99% 現代 CPU 仍是 von Neumann 架構。

1945 EDVAC report introduced the stored-program concept: programs are data. 99% of modern CPUs still use von Neumann architecture.

👩‍💻 Grace Hopper (1906–1992)

美國海軍少將；1952 寫出第一個編譯器 (A-0)；共同設計 COBOL；命名「bug / debug」。

U.S. Navy Rear Admiral. Built the first compiler (A-0, 1952); co-designed COBOL; coined "bug" and "debug."

🔋 Gordon Moore (1929–2023)

Intel 共同創辦人。1965 提出摩爾定律：電晶體每 18–24 個月增為兩倍。維持 60 年才放緩。

Intel co-founder. 1965 Moore's Law: transistors double every 18–24 months. Held for 60 years.

🪟 Bill Gates / 🍎 Steve Jobs

1975 微軟、1976 蘋果——讓個人電腦進入家庭。1984 麥金塔 + 1990 Windows 3.0 開啟 GUI 革命。

Microsoft (1975), Apple (1976) — brought PCs into homes. 1984 Macintosh + 1990 Windows 3.0 sparked the GUI revolution.

🐧 Linus Torvalds (1969–)

21 歲寫 Linux kernel (1991)、後來寫 Git (2005)。Linux 驅動 90%+ 雲端、超算、Android。

At 21, wrote the Linux kernel (1991); later Git (2005). Linux powers 90%+ of cloud, supercomputers, and Android.

🌐 Tim Berners-Lee (1955–)

1989 於 CERN 發明 WWW、HTTP、HTML。把網際網路從學術變成全人類的資訊空間。

Invented WWW, HTTP, HTML at CERN, 1989. Transformed the internet from academic tool to global information space.

圖靈機與停機問題

六、進階：圖靈機與停機問題

1936 年，24 歲的 Alan Turing 在《On Computable Numbers》中提出一個極度抽象的機器：

  • 一條無限長的紙帶，分格儲存符號（0/1）。
  • 一個讀寫頭，可以讀目前格、寫入新符號、左移或右移一格。
  • 一張狀態轉移表：根據當前狀態 + 讀到的符號，決定要寫什麼、移到哪、跳到什麼狀態。

這就是計算的最小模型。Church-Turing 論題主張：任何能被「演算法」解決的問題，都可被圖靈機解決。今天的 Python、CPU、量子電腦在「能算什麼」上都不超過圖靈機（雖然量子電腦在「多快算」上更快）。

停機問題 (Halting Problem)：Turing 同時證明了一個驚人結論——「給定任意程式 P 與輸入 I，能否寫一個通用程式 H 來判斷 P(I) 會不會停機？」答案是不可能。這是計算機科學第一個「無法計算」的問題，奠定了可計算性理論的基礎。

In 1936, a 24-year-old Alan Turing introduced an absurdly abstract machine in On Computable Numbers:

  • An infinite tape divided into cells holding symbols (0/1).
  • A read/write head that reads, writes, and shifts one cell at a time.
  • A state-transition table: given (current state, symbol read), decide what to write, which way to move, and which state to enter next.

That's the minimal model of computation. The Church-Turing thesis claims: anything an "algorithm" can solve, a Turing machine can solve. Today's Python, CPUs, even quantum computers cannot compute anything beyond what a Turing machine can (though quantum computers may compute some things faster).

The Halting Problem: Turing also proved a stunning negative result. "Given any program P and input I, can we build a universal program H that decides whether P(I) halts?" The answer is no, impossible. This was the first uncomputable problem in computer science — the foundation of computability theory.

💡
為什麼這對你重要？停機問題告訴我們：永遠寫不出一個「萬能 bug 偵測器」來判斷任意程式會不會無限迴圈、會不會崩潰。這就是為何 software testing 永遠不會被「自動化證明」完全取代。
Why this matters: The Halting Problem says we can never write a "universal bug detector" that decides if any program will loop forever or crash. That is why software testing will never be fully replaced by automated proofs.
五則必聽歷史

七、五則必聽的歷史故事

🐛 1947：真的有蟲

Grace Hopper 操作 Harvard Mark II 時，機器突然故障。她打開繼電器，發現一隻 真的蛾 卡在裡面。她把蛾貼到實驗日誌上，寫下「First actual case of bug being found」。「Debug」這個詞從此誕生，那頁日誌至今仍保存在華盛頓 Smithsonian 博物館。

Grace Hopper was operating the Harvard Mark II when it failed. Opening the relay, she found an actual moth jammed inside. She taped it into the log, writing "First actual case of bug being found." Thus was born "debug," and that page still sits in the Smithsonian.

💔 Turing 的悲劇

二戰時 Turing 領導布萊切利園破解 Enigma 密碼，估計縮短戰爭 2–4 年、救了 1400 萬條人命。1952 因「同性戀罪」被英國政府逼迫接受化學閹割（注射雌激素一年）。1954 年咬下沾有氰化物的蘋果自殺，年僅 41 歲。2013 年伊麗莎白二世女王頒布皇家赦免。Apple Logo 上那顆缺一口的蘋果，常被謠傳為紀念 Turing——雖然 Apple 官方否認。

At Bletchley Park, Turing led the team that cracked Enigma — estimated to have shortened WWII by 2–4 years and saved 14 million lives. In 1952 he was forced to undergo chemical castration for being gay. He died in 1954 by biting a cyanide-laced apple, age 41. Queen Elizabeth II granted a royal pardon in 2013. The bitten-apple Apple logo is often (incorrectly) rumored to honor him.

🚀 阿波羅 AGC：64KB 上月球

1969 年阿波羅 11 號的登月導引電腦 (AGC) 由 MIT 設計，只有 64 KB ROM、4 KB RAM、時脈 2.048 MHz。比現在 USB 充電器內的晶片還弱。登月時 AGC 多次出現「1202 alarm」——記憶體溢位——但程式設計師 Margaret Hamilton 設計的優先排程讓它自動丟棄低優先工作、繼續導航。阿姆斯壯說：「沒有 Margaret 的軟體，我們不會降落。」

The Apollo 11 Guidance Computer (AGC), built at MIT, had just 64 KB ROM, 4 KB RAM, and a 2.048 MHz clock — weaker than today's USB charger chips. During landing it threw multiple "1202 alarms" (memory overflow), but Margaret Hamilton's priority scheduler auto-dropped low-priority work and kept navigating. Armstrong: "Without Margaret's software, we would not have landed."

📺 1984：個人電腦革命

1984 年 1 月，Apple 在 Super Bowl 播出傳奇廣告《1984》：女運動員砸碎象徵 IBM 的「老大哥」螢幕。三天後，第一台麥金塔 (Macintosh) 上市——第一台真正普及的 GUI 個人電腦。售價 $2,495 ≈ 今天的 $7,500。從此「電腦」不再是科學家專屬。

January 1984: Apple's legendary "1984" Super Bowl ad showed a female athlete smashing "Big Brother" (IBM). Three days later, the first Macintosh shipped — the first mass-market GUI personal computer. Price: $2,495 (~$7,500 today). Computers stopped belonging only to scientists.

📜 Ada 的預言成真

1843 年 Ada 在 Note A 寫道：「分析機……終將能譜寫精緻、複雜程度任意的科學音樂。」當時所有人都覺得她瘋了。1957 年第一首電腦合成音樂《Illiac Suite》在伊利諾大學奏響；1965 年第一張電腦合成專輯發行；2024 年 OpenAI 的 MuseNet、Google MusicLM 已能生成符合任意風格的整首曲子。Ada 領先時代 180 年。

In Note A (1843), Ada wrote: "The Analytical Engine might compose elaborate and scientific pieces of music of any degree of complexity." Everyone thought her mad. 1957: the first computer-composed piece, Illiac Suite, played at the University of Illinois. 1965: first computer-music LP. 2024: OpenAI's MuseNet and Google's MusicLM generate full songs in any style. Ada was 180 years ahead.

🧬 ENIAC 的女程式員

1945 年 ENIAC 首批六位程式員全部都是女性：Kathleen McNulty、Frances Bilas、Betty Snyder、Marlyn Wescoff、Ruth Lichterman、Jean Bartik。當時「programmer」被視為低階文書職，連照片上她們的名字都沒寫出。直到 1986 年才被歷史學家「重新發現」。

The first six ENIAC programmers in 1945 were all women: Kathleen McNulty, Frances Bilas, Betty Snyder, Marlyn Wescoff, Ruth Lichterman, Jean Bartik. "Programmer" was then seen as low-status clerical work — their names weren't even captioned in photos. Historians only "rediscovered" them in 1986.

九、🧬 給生物資訊學家

🧬
給生物資訊學家：計算機史就是生資史

生物資訊學 (bioinformatics) 一詞 1970 年代由 Paulien Hogeweg 提出，但學科真正誕生與計算機普及完全同步。你今天每一行 BWA、STAR、AlphaFold 程式碼，都站在 80 年計算機發展之上。

The term "bioinformatics" was coined by Paulien Hogeweg in the 1970s, but the field truly emerged alongside the rise of computing. Every BWA, STAR, AlphaFold line you run today stands on 80 years of computing history.

📚 1982：GenBank 與 PC 同年

1981 年 IBM 推出 PC，1982 年 NIH 建立 GenBank——史上第一個基因序列公共資料庫，當時只有 606 條序列、680,338 bp。2026 年 GenBank 已收錄 4.4 兆 bp，成長 600 萬倍——與摩爾定律完美同步。

IBM PC launched in 1981; NIH founded GenBank in 1982 — the first public DNA sequence database, with just 606 sequences and 680,338 bp. By 2026, GenBank holds 4.4 trillion bp — a 6-million-fold increase, lockstep with Moore's Law.

🧬 人類基因組計畫

13 年、30 億美元、20 個國家合作完成首次人類基因組定序。它幾乎憑一己之力催生了整個計算生物學領域——序列比對演算法 (BLAST 1990)、組裝演算法 (Phrap, Celera)、註解工具 (Ensembl 1999) 全在這段時間誕生。

13 years, $3 billion, 20 nations completed the first human genome. It almost single-handedly birthed computational biology — alignment (BLAST 1990), assembly (Phrap, Celera), annotation (Ensembl 1999) all emerged in this window.

💰 定序成本超越摩爾

2003 年首次人類定序成本 ≈ $2.7 億美元。2024 年 Illumina NovaSeq X 一份人類全基因組 < $200——21 年成本下降 130 萬倍，遠超摩爾定律 (僅 ~30 倍)。這是史上最快的價格下跌曲線。

The first human genome cost $2.7 billion in 2003. By 2024, an Illumina NovaSeq X whole genome costs under $200 — a 1.3-million-fold drop in 21 years, vastly outpacing Moore's Law (~30×). The fastest cost decline in history.

🤖 AlphaFold：AI 圖靈時刻

2021 DeepMind AlphaFold2 一夜之間解決了 50 年的蛋白質摺疊問題 (Jumper 2021 Nature)，2024 共同獲得諾貝爾化學獎。這是 Turing 1936 論文之後生物學等待最久的「AI 圖靈時刻」——計算機真正改寫了一個學科的方法論。

DeepMind's AlphaFold2 (Jumper 2021 Nature) overnight solved a 50-year protein folding problem, sharing the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry. This is biology's most-awaited "AI Turing moment" since 1936 — computing genuinely rewrote a discipline's method.

十一、動手試

# 比較摩爾定律 vs. 定序成本下跌 / Moore's Law vs. sequencing cost
def moore_predict(year, start_year=1971, start=2300, doubling_months=18):
    months = (year - start_year) * 12
    return start * (2 ** (months / doubling_months))

def cost_decline(year, start_year=2003, start_cost=2.7e8, halving_months=5):
    # 觀察：2008-2015 NGS 成本每 5 個月減半（快過摩爾）
    months = (year - start_year) * 12
    return start_cost / (2 ** (months / halving_months))

print(f"2024 Moore prediction: {moore_predict(2024):,.0f} transistors")
print(f"2024 genome cost (pre-2015 trend): ${cost_decline(2024):,.2f}")

  months <- (year - start_year) * 12
  start * 2^(months / doubling_months)
}

    let months = (Y − start_year) × 12
    let doublings = months ÷ doubling_months

五個常見誤解

十二、常見誤解

🚨
誤解 #1：「von Neumann 發明了計算機」——錯。他在 1945 年 EDVAC 報告中總結並推廣儲存程式概念，但實際發明者是 Eckert 與 Mauchly。歷史學界至今仍在爭論誰的功勞最大。
Myth #1: "von Neumann invented the computer." Wrong. He summarized and popularized the stored-program concept in the 1945 EDVAC report, but the actual inventors were Eckert & Mauchly. Historians still debate credit.
🚨
誤解 #2：「Alan Turing 設計了 ENIAC」——錯。Turing 從未到過賓州大學。ENIAC 由 Eckert & Mauchly 設計、ENIAC 6 位女性程式員程式化；Turing 戰時在英國 Bletchley Park 破解 Enigma，戰後在 NPL 設計 ACE。
Myth #2: "Alan Turing designed ENIAC." Wrong. Turing never visited UPenn. ENIAC was designed by Eckert & Mauchly and programmed by the six ENIAC women. Turing cracked Enigma at Bletchley Park and designed the ACE at NPL.
⚠️
誤解 #3：「Apple Logo 的咬一口是紀念 Turing 自殺的氰化物蘋果」——浪漫但 錯。設計師 Rob Janoff 親口澄清：那只是為了讓蘋果看起來不像櫻桃。
Myth #3: "The bite in Apple's logo honors Turing's cyanide apple." Romantic, but false. Designer Rob Janoff confirmed: it's just to make the apple not look like a cherry.
⚠️
誤解 #4：「摩爾定律是物理定律」——錯。它是一個經驗觀察與產業預測，由 Intel 為自己的 roadmap 努力維持。物理極限 (~1 nm) 已迫在眉睫。
Myth #4: "Moore's Law is a physical law." Wrong. It is an empirical observation and industry projection, kept alive by Intel's effort. Physical limits (~1 nm) are now imminent.
⚠️
誤解 #5：「量子電腦會取代古典電腦」——不會。量子電腦只在特定問題（質因數分解、模擬量子系統）上有優勢。日常運算 (Word、瀏覽器、編譯) 仍是古典 CPU 的天下。
Myth #5: "Quantum computers will replace classical." No. Quantum computers excel only at specific problems (factoring, quantum simulation). Day-to-day computing (Word, browsers, compilers) stays classical.

💭 延伸思考

十三、延伸思考

  1. AGI 何時到？ 圖靈 1950 年預測「公元 2000 年機器能通過圖靈測試」——他低估了多少？2026 年的 GPT-5/Claude Opus 5 能算「通過」嗎？
  2. When will AGI arrive? In 1950, Turing predicted machines would pass his test "by 2000." How wrong was he? Do today's GPT-5 / Claude Opus 5 "pass"?
  3. 摩爾定律真的死了嗎？ 物理上 3nm 已接近原子極限。chiplet、3D 堆疊、光子運算能撐多久？「每瓦效能」是不是更該注意的指標？
  4. Is Moore's Law dead? Physically, 3nm nears atomic limits. How long can chiplets, 3D-stacking, and photonics extend it? Should "performance per watt" be the new metric?
  5. 量子優勢 (quantum advantage) 對生資有意義嗎？ 量子蛋白質摺疊、量子分子模擬可能比 AlphaFold 更精準——但 NISQ 時代何時結束？
  6. Does "quantum advantage" matter for bio? Quantum protein folding & molecular simulation may exceed AlphaFold — but when does the NISQ era end?
  7. 歷史的偶然性：如果 Turing 沒被迫害自殺、活到 90 歲，現代 AI 會早幾年到？如果 Ada 的分析機 1843 年真的造出來，工業革命會不會直接跳過？
  8. Historical counterfactuals: Had Turing lived to 90, would modern AI have arrived earlier? Had Babbage finished his engine in 1843, would the Industrial Revolution have leapt forward?
  9. 下一個典範轉移：2030 年代計算機的主流會是什麼？神經型態 (neuromorphic)？生物計算 (DNA storage 已商業化)？光子？
  10. Next paradigm shift: In the 2030s, what dominates? Neuromorphic chips? DNA storage (already commercial)? Photonics?
