資訊安全與密碼學

從 Diffie-Hellman 1976 年寫下的革命性論文，到 2024 NIST 後量子標準——50 年的數學如何守護你的銀行、醫療紀錄與基因組。

From Diffie-Hellman's 1976 revolution to NIST's 2024 post-quantum standards — 50 years of math that protect your bank, medical records, and genome.

一段改寫世界的數學

一、1976 年的一篇論文

1976 年 11 月，史丹佛大學兩位研究員 Whitfield Diffie 與 Martin Hellman 在 IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 發表了一篇論文，題目樸實到驚人：「New Directions in Cryptography」。

在那之前，所有的加密都有一個致命假設——雙方必須先「面對面」交換一把共同的鑰匙。二戰時 Enigma 機器靠每天更換的金鑰本；冷戰時期 KGB 與 CIA 互派情報員手提箱送密碼。如果你今天想跟一個從未見過的陌生人安全通訊？不可能。

Diffie-Hellman 用一個簡單卻天才的數學技巧（模指數運算的單向性）讓兩個陌生人能在公開頻道喊話、結束後卻共享一把只有他們知道的密鑰。竊聽全部對話的 NSA 也算不出來。

50 年後的今天，你每買一杯網路咖啡（HTTPS）、解鎖手機（Secure Enclave）、看醫療紀錄、傳 LINE 訊息、刷實聯制——背後跑的都是這個數學。資安不是 IT 部門的工作，它是現代社會的基礎設施。

In November 1976, two Stanford researchers — Whitfield Diffie and Martin Hellman — published a paper in IEEE Transactions on Information Theory with a strikingly plain title: "New Directions in Cryptography".

Before that, every encryption scheme had one fatal assumption: both parties must meet face-to-face first to share a key. WWII Enigma rotated daily codebooks; Cold-War KGB/CIA agents flew across continents to deliver briefcases of keys. Want to talk privately with a stranger you've never met? Impossible.

Diffie-Hellman's brilliant trick (the one-way nature of modular exponentiation) let two strangers shout messages over a public channel and walk away sharing a secret. The NSA could log every byte and still not compute the key.

Fifty years later, every internet coffee purchase (HTTPS), every phone unlock (Secure Enclave), every medical record, LINE message, and contact-tracing log runs on this math. Security isn't an IT-team chore; it's the infrastructure of modern life.

💡
2025 年的代價：IBM Cost of a Data Breach 報告指出，2025 全球資料外洩事件的平均成本已達 488 萬美元，醫療業更高達 1,098 萬。勒索軟體攻擊每 11 秒發生一次。「不懂資安」在今天的工程界等同於「不會綁安全帶」。 The 2025 price tag: IBM's Cost of a Data Breach report puts the global average at USD 4.88 M, with healthcare at 10.98 M. A ransomware attack occurs every 11 seconds. "Not knowing security" today is the engineering equivalent of "not knowing how to fasten a seatbelt".
給初學者的入口

二、CIA 三要素

資安教科書都會從 CIA 三要素開始——這是 1980 年代美國國防部首次正式提出的目標，至今仍是業界共識。

Every security textbook starts with the CIA triad, first formalized by the US DoD in the 1980s and still the industry consensus.

🤐

Confidentiality 機密性

只有授權對象能讀到內容。靠加密 (encryption)達成。例：你的 LINE 對話只有對方解得開、磁碟加密讓筆電遺失也不外洩。

Only authorized parties can read the content — achieved by encryption. Examples: LINE chats only your friend can decode; full-disk encryption keeps your laptop's data safe even if stolen.

✅

Integrity 完整性

資料未被篡改。靠雜湊 (hash)與數位簽章達成。例：軟體更新檔附 SHA-256，Git 用 commit hash 偵測竄改。

Data has not been tampered with — achieved by hashes and digital signatures. Examples: software updates ship a SHA-256; Git uses commit hashes to detect tampering.

⚡

Availability 可用性

合法使用者需要時服務可用。靠備援、抗 DDoS、災難復原達成。例：銀行網銀承諾 99.99% uptime；Cloudflare 緩衝 DDoS 攻擊。

Service is reachable when legitimate users need it — via redundancy, DDoS mitigation, disaster recovery. Examples: banks promise 99.99 % uptime; Cloudflare absorbs DDoS storms.

OWASP Top 10 2025

三、OWASP Top 10 排名動態

OWASP Top 10 是「全世界最危險的 10 類 Web 應用弱點」排行榜，由 OWASP 基金會每 3–4 年更新一次。2025 版有兩個重大改動：

  • A01 Broken Access Control 連續四屆冠軍——「權限沒寫好」是業界最常見的 bug。
  • A06 改為 Software Supply Chain Failures——納入 2020 SolarWinds、2021 Log4Shell、2024 XZ Utils backdoor 等供應鏈攻擊。
  • A02 升為 Security Misconfiguration——AWS S3 bucket 公開、Kubernetes 密碼明文寫死，這類「設定錯誤」竄上第二名。

OWASP Top 10 ranks the world's most dangerous web-app risks; the OWASP Foundation refreshes it every 3–4 years. The 2025 edition has two big shifts:

  • A01 Broken Access Control — four-time champion: "auth wasn't checked correctly" is the industry's #1 bug.
  • A06 renamed Software Supply Chain Failures: now covers SolarWinds (2020), Log4Shell (2021), and the XZ Utils backdoor (2024).
  • A02 elevated to Security Misconfiguration: open S3 buckets, hard-coded Kubernetes secrets — config errors climbed to #2.

進階：AES / RSA / ECC / TLS 1.3 內部

四、深入剖析

AES-256 — 14 輪替換

AES (Advanced Encryption Standard) 由比利時兩位密碼學家 Joan Daemen 與 Vincent Rijmen 設計（原名 Rijndael），1997 年 NIST 公開徵選後於 2001 標準化。AES-256 對 128-bit 資料區塊執行 14 輪操作，每輪包括：

  1. SubBytes：用 S-Box 查表非線性替換（核心抗差分攻擊）。
  2. ShiftRows：行內位元組向左循環移位，破壞對齊。
  3. MixColumns：行列在 GF(2⁸) 上做矩陣乘法，達成擴散。
  4. AddRoundKey：與輪金鑰 XOR（由主金鑰擴展而來）。

歷經 25 年無人破解；2010 年起 Intel 推出 AES-NI 硬體指令，現代 CPU 每核每秒可加密 5–10 GB。實務一律使用 AES-GCM（含完整性驗證）或 AES-XTS（磁碟加密）。

AES was designed by Belgian cryptographers Joan Daemen and Vincent Rijmen (originally "Rijndael"), chosen by NIST in 2000 after an open competition and standardized in 2001. AES-256 processes 128-bit blocks through 14 rounds, each round consisting of:

  1. SubBytes — non-linear S-Box substitution (anti-differential).
  2. ShiftRows — cyclically shift each row to break alignment.
  3. MixColumns — multiply columns in GF(2⁸) for diffusion.
  4. AddRoundKey — XOR with the round key derived from the master key.

Unbroken in 25 years. Intel's AES-NI hardware instructions (since 2010) let modern CPUs encrypt 5–10 GB/s per core. Always use AES-GCM (authenticated) or AES-XTS (disk encryption) in production.

RSA — 質因數分解

RSA 由 Rivest, Shamir, Adleman 1977 在 MIT 提出。原理是：給你兩個大質數 p、q（各 1024 bit），乘起來得 n = p×q 很容易，但只給你 n 要找回 p、q 在計算上極難。

金鑰生成：① 選大質數 p, q；② 計算 n = pq, φ(n) = (p−1)(q−1)；③ 選 e（通常 65537）使 gcd(e, φ) = 1；④ 算 d = e−1 mod φ。公鑰 = (n, e)、私鑰 = (n, d)。

加密：c = me mod n；解密：m = cd mod n。背後是 Euler 1763 的定理 mφ(n) ≡ 1 (mod n)。

RSA-2048（n 為 2048-bit）今天估計需要 10²⁰ CPU-年破解；但夠大的量子電腦用 Shor 演算法只需幾小時——後量子轉換刻不容緩。

RSA was introduced by Rivest, Shamir, Adleman at MIT in 1977. Premise: given two big primes p, q (1024 bits each), computing n = p × q is easy, but recovering p and q from n is computationally infeasible.

Key generation: (1) pick large primes p, q; (2) compute n = pq, φ(n) = (p−1)(q−1); (3) choose e (usually 65537) with gcd(e, φ) = 1; (4) compute d = e−1 mod φ. Public key = (n, e); private key = (n, d).

Encrypt: c = me mod n. Decrypt: m = cd mod n. The math rests on Euler's 1763 theorem mφ(n) ≡ 1 (mod n).

RSA-2048 takes ~10²⁰ CPU-years today, but a large enough quantum computer using Shor's algorithm cracks it in hours — PQ migration is urgent.

ECC — 為何省更多

橢圓曲線密碼學 (Elliptic Curve Cryptography) 把運算搬到橢圓曲線群上，「離散對數問題」更難解。安全強度對照：

Elliptic Curve Cryptography moves the math to an elliptic-curve group where the discrete-log problem is much harder. Strength comparison:

ECC 金鑰小 12 倍、簽章速度快 10 倍——這正是行動裝置、IoT、區塊鏈（比特幣用 secp256k1）首選 ECC 的原因。Ed25519（Bernstein 2011）目前是 SSH、Tor、Signal 的主流。

ECC keys are 12× smaller and 10× faster to sign — that's why mobile, IoT, and blockchains (Bitcoin uses secp256k1) prefer it. Ed25519 (Bernstein, 2011) dominates SSH, Tor, and Signal.

TLS 1.3 — 1-RTT 握手

ClientHello

瀏覽器送出：支援的密碼套件清單、TLS 版本、隨機數、ECDHE 公鑰份額（X25519 預設）。

Browser sends: supported cipher suites, TLS versions, random, and an ECDHE key share (X25519 by default).

ServerHello + Certificate

伺服器選一組密碼套件、附上 X.509 憑證鏈（已被 CA 簽名）、自己的 ECDHE 公鑰份額。雙方各自算出共享密鑰 K = a·B = b·A。

Server picks a cipher suite, sends the X.509 cert chain (CA-signed), and its own ECDHE share. Both sides derive the same secret K = a·B = b·A.

Finished (encrypted)

雙方用衍生金鑰加密 Finished 訊息驗證對方真的解出來了。TLS 1.3 砍掉 TLS 1.2 三次往返中的兩次，僅需 1-RTT。回訪可達 0-RTT（但 0-RTT 有重放風險）。

Both sides exchange encrypted Finished messages proving they derived the same key. TLS 1.3 collapses TLS 1.2's three round-trips into 1-RTT; resumption can hit 0-RTT (with replay caveats).

Application data

之後 HTTP 內容皆用 AES-256-GCM 或 ChaCha20-Poly1305（AEAD）加密 + 完整性驗證。

All subsequent HTTP traffic is protected with AES-256-GCM or ChaCha20-Poly1305 (AEAD).

Argon2 — 為何 SHA-256 存密碼不夠？

SHA-256 是「快」雜湊（每秒幾 GB），這對密碼儲存反而是缺點——攻擊者拿到 hash 後可每秒嘗試 100 億組密碼。Argon2id（Password Hashing Competition 2015 冠軍）刻意設計成「慢」且需要大量 RAM（記憶體硬性），讓 GPU/ASIC 攻擊極度困難。實務建議參數：64 MB RAM、3 iterations、4 threads。

SHA-256 is a fast hash (GB/s) — bad for password storage, because attackers can try 10 billion passwords/second on a GPU. Argon2id (2015 PHC winner) is deliberately slow and memory-hard, defeating GPU/ASIC cracking. Recommended params: 64 MB RAM, 3 iterations, 4 threads.

資安史上 7 個關鍵時刻

五、歷史故事

1976 — 公鑰密碼學誕生

Diffie 與 Hellman 在 Stanford 發表「New Directions」，把「先交換鑰匙」這個 2000 年的假設徹底打破。2015 年兩人共同獲頒圖靈獎。

Diffie and Hellman publish "New Directions" at Stanford, demolishing the 2,000-year-old assumption that keys must be pre-shared. They share the 2015 Turing Award for this work.

1977 — 非對稱加密實作

MIT 三位學者 Rivest、Shamir、Adleman 在 1977 年某個逾越節晚餐後，Rivest 通宵推出 RSA 演算法。RSA 公司現估值超 30 億美元。

After a 1977 Passover dinner, MIT's Rivest, Shamir, Adleman crank out the RSA algorithm overnight. RSA Corp is now valued at $3 B+.

2013 — 大規模監控曝光

Edward Snowden 公開 NSA 全球監聽計畫（PRISM、BULLRUN），讓 IETF 加速 TLS 1.3 設計（2018 RFC 8446）：強制 PFS、刪除 RC4/MD5/SHA1，握手只需 1-RTT。

Edward Snowden exposes NSA mass surveillance (PRISM, BULLRUN), pushing IETF to fast-track TLS 1.3 (RFC 8446 in 2018): mandatory PFS, RC4/MD5/SHA1 removed, 1-RTT handshake.

2014 — OpenSSL 大流血

OpenSSL 一行 missing bounds check 讓任何人能讀取伺服器記憶體 64 KB——包含 TLS 私鑰、密碼、信用卡。影響全球 17% 的 HTTPS 伺服器，推動 Linux Foundation 成立 Core Infrastructure Initiative。

A missing bounds check in OpenSSL let anyone read 64 KB of server memory — including TLS private keys, passwords, credit cards. Hit 17 % of HTTPS servers worldwide, spurring the Linux Foundation's Core Infrastructure Initiative.

2017 — 史上最大勒索軟體

2017 年 5 月，WannaCry 蠕蟲利用 NSA 洩漏的 EternalBlue 漏洞，48 小時內加密 30 萬台電腦，包含英國 NHS、台積電產線。教訓：及時打補丁 + 離線備份。

May 2017: WannaCry, weaponizing the leaked NSA EternalBlue exploit, encrypts 300,000 machines in 48 hours — including UK NHS hospitals and TSMC fabs. Lessons: timely patching + offline backups.

2020 — 供應鏈惡夢

俄羅斯駭客（APT29）入侵 SolarWinds Orion 軟體更新流程，植入後門。18,000 個組織（美國國務院、財政部、Microsoft、FireEye）被滲透。從此「軟體物料清單 SBOM」成為合規必備。

Russian APT29 compromises SolarWinds Orion's update pipeline, planting a backdoor. 18,000 orgs infected — US State, Treasury, Microsoft, FireEye. Software Bills of Materials (SBOM) become compliance-mandatory.

2024 — FIPS 203/204/205

2024 年 8 月 NIST 公布前三套後量子標準：ML-KEM（FIPS 203，原 Kyber）、ML-DSA（FIPS 204，原 Dilithium）、SLH-DSA（FIPS 205，原 SPHINCS+）。Apple iMessage 在 2024 推出 PQ3 協定，Google Chrome 已預設啟用 X25519+ML-KEM 混合。

August 2024: NIST publishes the first three PQC standards — ML-KEM (FIPS 203, was Kyber), ML-DSA (FIPS 204, was Dilithium), SLH-DSA (FIPS 205, was SPHINCS+). Apple iMessage shipped PQ3 in 2024; Chrome enables X25519+ML-KEM hybrid by default.

生物資訊學家的關鍵知識

七、生資連結點

🧬
基因組資料的特殊性

基因組是你終身不變的識別碼，比信用卡更敏感——洩漏一次等於洩漏一輩子。而且你的基因與父母、子女、手足有 50% 重疊，洩漏你的等於洩漏家族。再者，臨床全基因組保存週期 50+ 年，今天加密的若用 RSA-2048，未來量子電腦成熟後將全部可被「現在收割、未來解密」。

A genome is a lifetime-immutable identifier — more sensitive than a credit card; leaked once = leaked forever. Plus, your genome overlaps 50 % with parents/children/siblings, so leaking yours leaks your family. Clinical WGS is also retained 50+ years, meaning RSA-2048-encrypted records today are vulnerable to "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" once quantum is mature.

🔐

HIPAA / GDPR / dbGaP

美國 HIPAA（1996）與歐盟 GDPR（2018）皆把基因組列為「特殊類別」個資。NIH dbGaP (database of Genotypes and Phenotypes) 對 controlled-access dataset（如 TCGA、UK Biobank）要求填寫 Data Use Certification (DUC)、IRB 核可、6 個月複審。違規重罰：2025 年某學術中心因 USB 遺失 5000 筆 WGS 被罰 1,300 萬美元。

The US HIPAA (1996) and EU GDPR (2018) classify genomes as "special category" data. NIH's dbGaP controlled-access (TCGA, UK Biobank) requires a Data Use Certification (DUC), IRB approval, and 6-month renewals. Penalties bite: in 2025 one US academic centre paid $13 M for losing 5,000 WGS records on a USB.

🎯

Homer 攻擊

2008 年 Nils Homer 在 PLOS Genetics 證明：只要有受害者個人的 SNP 陣列 + 公開 GWAS 的 allele frequency 摘要統計，就能判斷他是否在 case 或 control 群（隱含疾病狀態）。NIH 因此立刻將所有 GWAS summary statistics 從公開區下架，至今仍是 controlled-access。差異隱私 (differential privacy) 後來成為解法。

In 2008 Nils Homer showed in PLOS Genetics that an attacker holding a victim's SNP array + a publicly released GWAS summary can tell whether the victim is in cases or controls (implying disease status). NIH pulled all GWAS summary stats from public access overnight — they remain controlled-access today. Differential privacy emerged as the fix.

🌐

聯邦學習

NVIDIA FLARE、Owkin、Google TFF 等框架讓多家醫院在原始資料不離院的前提下共同訓練 AI 模型：每家本地訓練、僅把模型梯度加密上傳，中央彙整後下發新權重。2024 EXAM (COVID prediction) 與 2024 BraTS 腦瘤分割競賽都採用此模式。

NVIDIA FLARE, Owkin, and Google TFF let multiple hospitals jointly train an AI model without raw data leaving the premises: each site trains locally and uploads only encrypted gradients; the central server aggregates and sends new weights back. The 2024 EXAM COVID predictor and 2024 BraTS brain-tumor challenge used this pattern.

📊

差異隱私

NIH All of Us（目標招募 100 萬美國人 WGS）在公開查詢介面加入 Laplace 噪音，數學保證單一個體加入或退出查詢結果幾乎無法被偵測。2020 美國人口普查也用 ε-DP 取代傳統 swapping。生資人查詢資料時看到的彙總數字會有微幅誤差，這是隱私換來的代價。

NIH All of Us (recruiting 1 M Americans for WGS) injects Laplace noise into public query results so any single individual joining/leaving is statistically undetectable. The 2020 US Census switched to ε-DP from old-school swapping. Bioinformaticians see tiny errors in aggregate counts — that's the privacy tax.

🔮

後量子轉換

臨床 WGS 依 FDA / 衛福部法規須保存 25–50 年。若你今天用 RSA-2048 加密儲存，假設 2035 年量子電腦能跑 Shor on 2048-bit，所有今天的記錄將被回溯解密。NIH 已要求新 grant 提交 PQ 遷移計畫；Cloud Provider 自 2025 起在 KMS 預設加上 ML-KEM。

Clinical WGS retention is 25–50 years per FDA/regional regulators. RSA-2048-encrypted records today are toast once quantum can run Shor on 2048 bits (~2035 est.) — everything you stored becomes retroactively decryptable. NIH now requires PQ migration plans in new grants; cloud KMS services have rolled out ML-KEM defaults in 2025.

⚖️

TCGA 申請流程

Step 1：在 dbGaP 註冊 eRA Commons 帳號。Step 2：機構簽署 Data Use Certification（PI + Signing Official + IT Director）。Step 3：審查約 2–4 週。Step 4：批准後僅能在 institutional firewall 內分析、禁止重新識別、禁止下載到雲端（除非 GDC Cloud Pilot 白名單）。違反 = 永久禁用 + 機構黑名單。

Step 1: register an eRA Commons account in dbGaP. Step 2: institution signs a Data Use Certification (PI + Signing Official + IT Director). Step 3: 2–4 weeks of review. Step 4: once approved, analysis only inside the institutional firewall, no re-identification, no cloud download (except GDC Cloud Pilot whitelist). Violation = permanent ban + institutional blacklist.

該用什麼密碼學

八、決策樹

🌳 我該用什麼密碼學方案？

Q1:
只想驗證資料完整性（不需加密）？→ 用 SHA-256（單純檢核）或 HMAC-SHA256（防止竄改 + 驗證來源）。
Q2:
儲存使用者密碼？→ 用 Argon2id（首選）或 bcrypt；絕對不要用 MD5、SHA1、SHA256-直接、PBKDF2-僅 1k 輪。
Q3:
加密大量資料（檔案、磁碟、串流）？→ 對稱 AES-256-GCM（伺服器有 AES-NI）或 ChaCha20-Poly1305（行動裝置/IoT）。
Q4:
兩端建立共享金鑰？→ ECDHE (X25519)；高機敏資料改為 X25519 + ML-KEM 混合（Chrome 預設）。
Q5:
簽章與身分驗證？→ Ed25519（現在）或 ML-DSA（後量子）；憑證用 X.509 + Let's Encrypt 自動續簽。
Q6:
保存基因組或臨床資料 25+ 年？→ 立即啟用 混合 PQC，重要私鑰用 HSM（如 AWS CloudHSM、YubiHSM）；定期密鑰輪替。
Q1:
Just verify integrity (no encryption)? → Plain SHA-256 for checksums, HMAC-SHA256 when you also need authenticity.
Q2:
Storing user passwords? → Argon2id first, bcrypt as fallback. Never MD5, SHA1, plain SHA256, or low-iteration PBKDF2.
Q3:
Encrypting bulk data (files/disks/streams)? → AES-256-GCM (server with AES-NI) or ChaCha20-Poly1305 (mobile/IoT).
Q4:
Establish a shared key? → ECDHE (X25519); for sensitive data add ML-KEM hybrid (Chrome default).
Q5:
Signatures & identity? → Ed25519 today, ML-DSA for PQ; X.509 certs auto-renewed via Let's Encrypt.
Q6:
Retaining genome/clinical data 25+ years? → Switch to hybrid PQC now; store critical private keys in an HSM (AWS CloudHSM, YubiHSM); rotate regularly.

陷阱與迷思

十、常見誤解

❌ 「Base64 是加密」

Base64 只是編碼（把二進位轉成可印字元），任何人都能直接還原。常被誤用於「藏密碼」，毫無安全性。加密的最低標準是對稱密鑰 + AEAD。

Base64 is just encoding (binary → printable chars), trivially reversible. People often misuse it to "hide passwords" — that's zero security. Minimum bar for encryption: symmetric key + AEAD.

❌ 「MD5 還能用啦」

MD5 自 2004 年王小雲教授發現碰撞攻擊後正式退役；2008 年荷蘭/瑞士團隊用 MD5 碰撞偽造 CA 憑證。只有非安全用途（如非加密的去重 checksum）才可繼續使用。

MD5 was retired in 2004 after Prof. Xiaoyun Wang found collisions; in 2008 a Dutch/Swiss team forged a CA cert using MD5. Only safe for non-security checksums (e.g., dedupe).

❌ 「自己 roll 加密更安全」

密碼學最痛的教訓：「Don't roll your own crypto」。即使是專業密碼學家也常出錯（Heartbleed 是 OpenSSL 內部 bug）。用經審計的標準庫：cryptography (Python)、openssl/sodium (R)、libsodium、Tink。

Crypto's most painful lesson: "Don't roll your own crypto". Even expert cryptographers slip up (Heartbleed was inside OpenSSL itself). Use audited standard libraries: cryptography (Python), openssl/sodium (R), libsodium, Tink.

❌ 「沒 HTTPS 也沒差」

HTTP 明文傳輸——任何同 Wi-Fi 的人能讀取你的 cookie 並冒充你登入。2018 後 Chrome 把 HTTP 標示為「不安全」；2025 全球 HTTPS 普及率達 96%。Let's Encrypt 免費，沒理由不啟用。

HTTP is plaintext — anyone on the same Wi-Fi can steal cookies and impersonate you. Since 2018 Chrome marks HTTP as "Not Secure"; 96 % of 2025 traffic is HTTPS. Let's Encrypt is free — no excuse.

❌ 「VPN = 100% 匿名」

VPN 只是把信任從 ISP 轉給 VPN 提供商，對方仍能看到所有流量。商用 VPN 在強制請求下會交出日誌（2017 PureVPN 案）。真匿名需要 Tor，但流量分析仍有風險。

A VPN merely shifts trust from your ISP to the VPN provider — they still see all your traffic. Under subpoena they hand over logs (PureVPN 2017 case). True anonymity requires Tor, though traffic analysis can still bite.

❌ 「加密了就萬無一失」

加密保護「資料在傳輸/儲存中」，但不防：① 端點被入侵（鍵盤側錄）；② 內部人員濫權；③ 量子電腦未來破解；④ side-channel 攻擊（時序、功耗）。資安是縱深防禦 (defense in depth)，加密只是其中一層。

Encryption protects "data at rest / in transit" but doesn't stop: (1) endpoint compromise (keylogger); (2) insider abuse; (3) future quantum attack; (4) side-channels (timing, power). Security is defense in depth — encryption is just one layer.

隱私保護計算三劍客

十之二、隱私保護計算

傳統加密保護「靜態」與「傳輸中」資料，但運算時必須先解密——這是基因組多中心研究最大的痛點。三個來自 1970-90 年代的數學工具終於在 2020 後成熟，讓「不解密就能算」變為可能：

Classical encryption protects data at rest and in transit, but you must decrypt before computing — the biggest pain point for multi-center genomics. Three mathematical tools from the 1970s–90s finally matured after 2020 to enable "compute without decrypting":

🔐

ZKP Zero-Knowledge Proof

1985 Goldwasser-Micali-Rackoff 提出。讓 Prover 對 Verifier 證明「我知道 X」卻不洩漏 X。2020 後 zk-SNARKs / zk-STARKs 落地 Zcash、StarkNet、Polygon zkEVM。生資用例：證明「我的 BRCA1 變異符合臨床指引收案條件」，不必交出完整 VCF。

1985 Goldwasser-Micali-Rackoff. A Prover proves "I know X" to a Verifier without revealing X. zk-SNARKs / zk-STARKs power Zcash, StarkNet, Polygon zkEVM. Genomic use: prove "my BRCA1 variant matches the trial inclusion criteria" without surrendering the full VCF.

🧮

HE Homomorphic Encryption

2009 Gentry 第一個 FHE (Fully Homomorphic)。允許直接在密文上做加法、乘法。Microsoft SEAL、IBM HElib、OpenFHE、Lattigo 為主流庫；常用 CKKS / BFV / BGV scheme。代價：運算慢 1000–10⁶×。已實用於 iDASH 競賽 GWAS 隱私運算、Apple iOS Live Caller ID Lookup。

2009 Gentry built the first FHE. Add/multiply directly on ciphertexts. Microsoft SEAL, IBM HElib, OpenFHE, Lattigo are the main libraries (CKKS/BFV/BGV schemes). Cost: 1000–10⁶× slowdown. Already practical for iDASH GWAS challenges and Apple's iOS Live Caller ID Lookup.

🤝

SMC / MPC Secure Multi-Party Compute

1982 Yao 百萬富翁問題起源。N 方各持私有輸入，共同計算 f(x₁..xₙ)，誰也不知道對方的輸入。Google Private Join-and-Compute、Meta Crypten 為代表。生資用例：跨醫院合算 GWAS 但原始基因型從不離院；瑞士 MedCo (2018) + UK Sano Genetics 已部署。

1982 Yao's Millionaires' Problem. N parties hold private inputs and jointly compute f(x₁..xₙ) — no one sees the others' input. Google Private Join-and-Compute, Meta Crypten lead the field. Genomic use: cross-hospital GWAS where raw genotypes never leave; Swiss MedCo (2018) + UK Sano Genetics deployed.

⚠️
後量子演算法細節（2024 NIST 標準）： ① ML-KEM (FIPS 203)——前身 CRYSTALS-Kyber，Module-Lattice 結構，金鑰交換主力，公鑰 800 bytes、密文 768 bytes（Kyber-512）；② ML-DSA (FIPS 204)——前身 CRYSTALS-Dilithium，數位簽章，簽章 ~2.4 KB；③ SLH-DSA (FIPS 205)——前身 SPHINCS+，Hash-based 簽章，不依賴任何「困難問題」假設（最保守選項），代價是簽章 17–49 KB；④ Falcon 預計 2025 標準化（NTRU lattice，簽章小但實作難）。Chrome 135 (2025) 已預設 X25519MLKEM768 混合金鑰交換。 PQC algorithm details (2024 NIST standards): (1) ML-KEM (FIPS 203) — formerly CRYSTALS-Kyber, Module-Lattice; main KEM; 800 B public key + 768 B ciphertext (Kyber-512). (2) ML-DSA (FIPS 204) — formerly CRYSTALS-Dilithium; ~2.4 KB signatures. (3) SLH-DSA (FIPS 205) — formerly SPHINCS+; Hash-based, relies on no "hardness assumption" (most conservative), at the cost of 17–49 KB signatures. (4) Falcon coming 2025 (NTRU lattice; small sigs but tricky implementation). Chrome 135 (2025) defaults to X25519MLKEM768 hybrid key exchange.

💭 延伸思考

十一、延伸思考

  1. 量子威脅時間表：Google Willow（2024，105 qubit）已實現「below threshold」糾錯。你認為量子電腦真的會在 2030 年前打破 RSA-2048 嗎？我們是該全速衝刺 PQC，還是再觀察？
  2. Quantum timeline: Google Willow (2024, 105 qubits) achieved "below-threshold" error correction. Do you believe a quantum computer will break RSA-2048 before 2030? Should we sprint to PQC or wait?
  3. 區塊鏈與「被遺忘權」：歐盟 GDPR 賦予個人「被遺忘權」（要求刪除個資），但區塊鏈設計上「不可變」。如果你的全基因組摘要寫進某個醫療區塊鏈，可以刪除嗎？兩者如何共存？
  4. Blockchain vs Right-to-be-Forgotten: GDPR grants individuals the right to erasure, but blockchains are immutable by design. If your genome summary is committed to a medical blockchain, can it be deleted? How do these coexist?
  5. 「Harvest Now, Decrypt Later」的倫理：如果你今天在臨床機構工作，知道現有加密 30 年後將不安全，但更換密鑰需要新基礎建設與經費。你會如何說服管理階層投資？
  6. The HNDL ethics question: if you work at a hospital today knowing current encryption will be broken in 30 years, but migration costs infrastructure dollars, how do you make the business case to leadership?
  7. 個人實踐：你目前用了密碼管理器（1Password / Bitwarden）嗎？所有重要帳號都開了 2FA（最好是 hardware key 如 YubiKey）嗎？如果沒有，這週就行動。
  8. Personal practice: Do you use a password manager (1Password / Bitwarden)? Is 2FA (ideally hardware keys like YubiKey) enabled on all critical accounts? If not — do it this week.
  9. 強制醫療資料端到端加密？有人主張所有電子病歷 (EHR) 與基因組必須端到端加密，連雲端服務商都不能讀。但這會讓 AI 輔助診斷、跨院 referral 變慢甚至無法執行。你會支持立法強制嗎？
  10. Mandate E2E encryption for medical data? Some argue all EHRs and genomes must be end-to-end encrypted so even cloud providers can't read them. But this slows AI-assisted diagnosis and cross-hospital referrals. Would you legislate it?
  11. 「AI 處理我的基因組」你願意嗎？OpenAI/Claude/Gemini 都已開放 API。如果你把自己的 WGS upload 給 ChatGPT 詢問風險，會發生什麼？條款裡的「不用於訓練」承諾你信嗎？2024 年 OpenAI 已被多次質疑日誌留存。
  12. Would you let an AI read your genome? OpenAI/Claude/Gemini all expose APIs. If you uploaded your WGS to ChatGPT for risk advice, what happens? Do you trust the "won't be used for training" clause? OpenAI was repeatedly questioned about log retention in 2024.
  13. SBOM 與 Sigstore 落地：你的實驗室 Snakemake / Nextflow pipeline 有產出 SBOM 嗎？如果某個 R package 突然下架或被植入後門，你 24 小時內能列出所有受影響的分析嗎？這是 NIH 2025 新規的要求。
  14. SBOM & Sigstore in practice: Does your Snakemake / Nextflow pipeline emit an SBOM? If one R package is pulled or backdoored, can you list every affected analysis within 24 h? That's the new NIH 2025 expectation.
