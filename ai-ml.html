CHAPTER 14 / 16

人工智慧與機器學習

從 1958 年 Perceptron 兩度寒冬，到 2024 AlphaFold 3 預測蛋白質-DNA-小分子複合體——七十年神經網路的曲折翻身，與它正在改寫的生命科學。

From the 1958 Perceptron through two AI winters to 2024's AlphaFold 3 predicting protein-DNA-ligand complexes — seven decades of neural networks, and how they're rewriting life science.

AlphaFold 的奇蹟

一、AlphaFold 的奇蹟

1972 年諾貝爾化學獎得主 Christian Anfinsen 在頒獎演說中提出「Anfinsen's dogma」——蛋白質的胺基酸序列完全決定它的 3D 結構。聽起來像個應該很快被破解的數學問題。

結果，這個問題折磨了結構生物學家整整 50 年。每年的 CASP 競賽（Critical Assessment of Structure Prediction）就像蛋白質界的奧運，從 1994 開始每兩年舉辦一次。傳統演算法（同源建模、能量最小化）能在簡單蛋白質達到 30–60 GDT_TS，碰到沒有同源結構的就崩潰。

2020 年 12 月，CASP14 的結果震驚全場：DeepMind 的 AlphaFold 2 平均達到 92.4 GDT_TS——相當於實驗解析結構的精度。期刊 Nature 標題寫道：「It will change everything」。2022 年釋出 2 億個蛋白質結構預測庫；2024 年 AlphaFold 3 把蛋白質-DNA-RNA-小分子-離子的複合體一起預測，發表於 Nature（Abramson et al. 2024）。Demis Hassabis 與 John Jumper 因此榮獲 2024 諾貝爾化學獎。

AI 已從「未來」變成「現在式」——它不只是寫詩聊天的玩具，是科學研究的基礎工具。

In his 1972 Nobel lecture, Christian Anfinsen proposed "Anfinsen's dogma" — a protein's amino-acid sequence fully determines its 3D structure. Sounds like a tidy math problem.

That problem tormented structural biologists for 50 years. The biennial CASP (Critical Assessment of Structure Prediction) competition, running since 1994, is the protein Olympics. Traditional methods (homology modeling, energy minimization) reached 30–60 GDT_TS on easy targets and broke down on novel folds.

In December 2020, CASP14 produced a shockwave: DeepMind's AlphaFold 2 averaged 92.4 GDT_TS — experimental accuracy. Nature's headline: "It will change everything". By 2022 they had released 200 million predicted structures; in 2024 AlphaFold 3 (Abramson et al., Nature) extended to protein-DNA-RNA-ligand-ion complexes. Demis Hassabis and John Jumper won the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry.

AI has shifted from "future" to "present tense" — no longer a chatbot toy but a fundamental research tool.

💡
AI、ML、DL、LLM 是什麼關係？AI（人工智慧）是最廣的概念；ML（機器學習）是 AI 的一個子集，「讓電腦從資料學習」；DL（深度學習）是 ML 的子集，「用多層神經網路」；LLM（大型語言模型）是 DL 在文字領域的特例。AI ⊃ ML ⊃ DL ⊃ LLM。 AI vs ML vs DL vs LLM: AI is the broadest concept; ML is a subset of AI ("learn from data"); DL is a subset of ML ("multi-layer neural networks"); LLMs are a DL specialty for text. AI ⊃ ML ⊃ DL ⊃ LLM.
給初學者的入口

二、三大典範

🎯

監督式學習

給模型「題目 + 答案」對。最常見：分類（垃圾郵件 / 非垃圾）、回歸（預測房價）。經典演算法：線性回歸、邏輯回歸、SVM、隨機森林、XGBoost、神經網路。

Model sees (input, label) pairs. Most common: classification (spam / not) and regression (house prices). Classic algorithms: linear/logistic regression, SVM, Random Forest, XGBoost, neural nets.

🔍

非監督學習

只有「題目」，沒有「答案」。任務是發現結構：分群 (k-means、DBSCAN、Leiden)、降維 (PCA、t-SNE、UMAP)、異常偵測。scRNA-seq 細胞分群就是典型應用。

Only inputs, no labels — discover structure: clustering (k-means, DBSCAN, Leiden), dim-reduction (PCA, t-SNE, UMAP), anomaly detection. Cell clustering in scRNA-seq is a flagship use case.

🎮

強化學習

agent 在環境中行動，靠獎勵訊號學會最佳策略。DeepMind AlphaGo（2016 擊敗李世乭）、AlphaZero（2017 自學圍棋/西洋棋/將棋）、ChatGPT 的 RLHF（從人類偏好學習）皆屬此類。

An agent acts in an environment and learns via reward signals. DeepMind AlphaGo (beat Lee Sedol 2016), AlphaZero (self-taught Go/chess/shogi 2017), and ChatGPT's RLHF (learning from human preferences) all fall here.

Train / Val / Test Split

把資料分三份是 ML 的第一誡命：

  • Training set (60–70%)：模型實際學習的資料。
  • Validation set (15–20%)：調超參數（學習率、層數、L2 強度）；模型「看得到但不用來學」。
  • Test set (15–20%)：最後一刻才碰，模擬「真實世界從未見過的資料」。絕不能用 test set 調超參——那叫「資料外洩 (data leakage)」，會嚴重高估模型表現。

更嚴謹的做法是 k-fold cross-validation（k=5 或 10），尤其在資料量不大時。

Splitting data is ML's first commandment:

  • Training (60–70 %): data the model actually learns from.
  • Validation (15–20 %): tune hyperparameters (lr, layers, L2); model "sees but doesn't learn from".
  • Test (15–20 %): touched only at the end — proxy for "unseen real-world data". Never tune on the test set — that's "data leakage" and inflates reported accuracy.

For small data, use k-fold cross-validation (k = 5 or 10).

進階：神經網路 / Transformer / MoE

四、深入剖析

一顆神經元的數學

一個神經元做的事：把輸入 x 與權重 w 內積、加上 bias b，再丟進「激活函數」。最常用的激活函數：

  • Sigmoid σ(x) = 1/(1+e−x)：輸出 (0, 1)；早期 NN 主流，但易梯度消失。
  • tanh：輸出 (−1, 1)；類似 sigmoid 但對稱於 0。
  • ReLU = max(0, x)：2010 年後深度學習標配，計算極快、解決梯度消失。
  • GELU / SwiGLU：Transformer 主流，平滑版的 ReLU。

反向傳播 (Backpropagation)：1986 Rumelhart、Hinton、Williams 在 Nature 重新發表。本質是鏈式法則：把 loss 對最後一層權重的偏導，從後往前一層層傳播回去。配合 SGD / Adam / AdamW 等優化器更新權重。

One neuron: dot-product input x with weights w, add bias b, feed through an activation:

  • Sigmoid σ(x) = 1/(1+e−x): output (0,1); early NN staple but vanishing-gradient prone.
  • tanh: output (−1, 1); symmetric around 0.
  • ReLU = max(0, x): post-2010 deep-learning default — blazing fast, fixes vanishing gradients.
  • GELU / SwiGLU: Transformer mainstays, smooth variants of ReLU.

Backpropagation (Rumelhart, Hinton, Williams in Nature, 1986): really just the chain rule — propagate the loss's gradient backward through the network, then update weights with SGD / Adam / AdamW.

三大網路架構

🖼️

CNN （影像）

1989 LeCun 為郵局郵遞區號辨識設計 LeNet-5。「卷積」= 同一組濾波器掃過整張圖，擷取局部特徵（邊緣、紋理）。2012 AlexNet 在 ImageNet 大勝啟動深度學習革命。應用：醫學影像、自駕車視覺、CryoEM 重建。

LeCun designed LeNet-5 in 1989 for US Postal ZIP-code reading. A "convolution" applies the same filter across the image to extract local features (edges, textures). AlexNet's 2012 ImageNet win kicked off the deep-learning revolution. Uses: medical imaging, self-driving vision, CryoEM reconstruction.

📜

RNN / LSTM （序列）

Recurrent NN 把前一個時步的輸出當下一步的輸入，處理變長序列。LSTM（Hochreiter-Schmidhuber 1997）用 gate 機制解決長距依賴。直到 2017 仍是 NLP / 語音主流，現多被 Transformer 取代但仍用於即時串流。

RNNs feed each time-step's output as input to the next. LSTM (Hochreiter & Schmidhuber, 1997) added gating to handle long-range dependencies. Dominated NLP/speech until 2017; mostly replaced by Transformers but still used in real-time streams.

🎯

Transformer （萬能）

2017 Vaswani et al. 「Attention is All You Need」。核心是 self-attention：每個 token 都能直接「看到」序列中任何位置（不像 RNN 只能一格一格傳）。並行性極佳，吃 GPU 吃得飽。今天的 GPT、Claude、Gemini、AlphaFold、ESMFold 全是 Transformer 變體。

Vaswani et al. 2017, "Attention is All You Need". The core is self-attention: every token sees every other position directly (unlike RNNs' chain). Massively parallel and GPU-friendly. Every modern LLM (GPT, Claude, Gemini) and biology FM (AlphaFold, ESMFold) is a Transformer variant.

2025 新興技巧

  • MoE (Mixture of Experts)：模型有 100B 參數但每次只啟動 17B「專家」。Mixtral、DeepSeek-V3、GPT-4 都用此架構降低推理成本。
  • 量化 (Quantization)：把 FP16 權重壓到 INT8、INT4，記憶體與速度可省 2–4 倍，精度幾乎不掉。llama.cpp、GPTQ、AWQ 是工具代表。
  • RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)：用人類偏好訓練 reward model，再用 PPO 微調 LLM。ChatGPT 之所以「聽話」的核心技術。2024 起 DPO (Direct Preference Optimization) 簡化此流程。
  • RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)：LLM + 向量資料庫（Pinecone、Weaviate、pgvector），讓模型查私有知識回答，減少幻覺。
  • Agent / 工具呼叫：LLM 自主呼叫工具（Python interpreter、檢索、shell）解決多步驟任務。Claude Code、Devin、AutoGPT 是代表。
  • MoE: 100 B-param model but only 17 B "experts" active per token. Mixtral, DeepSeek-V3, GPT-4 use this to slash inference cost.
  • Quantization: squeeze FP16 weights to INT8/INT4 — 2–4× memory & speed gain with little accuracy loss. llama.cpp, GPTQ, AWQ are workhorses.
  • RLHF: train a reward model on human preferences, then PPO-fine-tune the LLM. The reason ChatGPT "behaves". DPO (2024) simplifies this pipeline.
  • RAG: LLM + vector DB (Pinecone, Weaviate, pgvector) — model retrieves from private knowledge to ground answers, cutting hallucination.
  • Agents / tool use: LLMs autonomously call tools (Python, retrieval, shell) for multi-step tasks. Claude Code, Devin, AutoGPT exemplify.
AI 七十年

五、歷史故事

1958 — 第一個「人工神經元」

Frank Rosenblatt 在 Cornell 用 IBM 704 打造 Perceptron——可學習權重的線性分類器。《紐約時報》當時聲稱「未來能走、能說、能複製自己」。事實上它連 XOR 都學不會。

Frank Rosenblatt at Cornell builds the Perceptron on an IBM 704 — a linear classifier with learnable weights. The New York Times claims it will soon "walk, talk, and reproduce itself". In reality it can't even learn XOR.

1969 — Minsky 的致命一書

Marvin Minsky 與 Seymour Papert 出版《Perceptrons》，數學證明單層感知器無法解 XOR——美國政府幾乎抽掉所有 AI 經費，NN 沉寂 17 年。

Minsky and Papert publish Perceptrons, proving single-layer networks can't solve XOR. The US government pulls AI funding; NNs lie dormant for 17 years.

1986 — Rumelhart-Hinton 解封

Rumelhart、Hinton、Williams 在 Nature 發表「Learning representations by back-propagating errors」，證明多層 NN 用反向傳播可解 XOR——但 1990 年代 SVM 與 kernel methods 興起後 NN 又陷低潮。

Rumelhart, Hinton, Williams publish "Learning representations by back-propagating errors" in Nature, showing multi-layer NNs with backprop can solve XOR. But the 1990s SVM/kernel boom relegates NNs again.

2012 — ImageNet 大勝

Krizhevsky、Sutskever、Hinton 用 2 顆 GTX 580 GPU 訓 8 層 CNN，ImageNet 錯誤率從 26.2% → 15.3%，第二名遠落後 10 個百分點。GPU + 大資料 + CNN 三位一體點燃深度學習。

Krizhevsky, Sutskever, Hinton train an 8-layer CNN on two GTX 580 GPUs — ImageNet error 26.2 % → 15.3 %, 10 points ahead of second place. GPUs + big data + CNNs trigger the deep-learning revolution.

2017 — "Attention is All You Need"

Google 八位作者發表 Transformer 架構，捨棄 RNN 的循環、純靠 self-attention。三年內取代了幾乎所有 NLP 模型，並成為一切現代 LLM 的骨幹。

Eight Google authors publish the Transformer — pure self-attention, no recurrence. Replaces almost all NLP models within three years and becomes the backbone of every modern LLM.

2020 — 深度學習震撼生科

11 月 GPT-3（175 B 參數）展示 few-shot 學習；12 月 CASP14 AlphaFold 2 達到實驗精度——兩個「scaling law 奏效」的代表案例。

November: GPT-3 (175 B params) shows few-shot learning. December: AlphaFold 2 reaches experimental accuracy at CASP14. Two flagship cases of scaling laws working.

2022.11 — 五天破百萬用戶

OpenAI 發布 ChatGPT，五天內破百萬用戶、兩個月破億——AI 從研究室走進大眾客廳。微軟隨後投資 100 億美元，Google 緊急宣布 Gemini 計畫。

OpenAI launches ChatGPT: 1 M users in 5 days, 100 M in 2 months. AI leaps from lab to living room. Microsoft invests $10 B; Google scrambles to ship Gemini.

2024 — Test-time compute

5 月 AlphaFold 3 預測蛋白-DNA-小分子複合體；9 月 OpenAI o1 證明「推理時花更多算力」（chain-of-thought）可顯著提升數學/科學表現——AI 進入「思考」時代。

May: AlphaFold 3 predicts protein-DNA-ligand complexes. September: OpenAI o1 shows test-time chain-of-thought compute markedly lifts math/science performance — AI enters the "reasoning" era.

2025–2026 — Opus 4.7、GPT-5.5、Gemini 3

2025 起進入「代理 AI (agentic AI)」階段：Claude Opus 4.7、GPT-5.5、Gemini 3 Pro 能長時間自主執行多步驟任務（程式碼倉庫重構、論文閱讀整理、實驗規劃）。SWE-bench Verified 突破 80%。

2025 enters the "agentic AI" era: Claude Opus 4.7, GPT-5.5, Gemini 3 Pro autonomously execute long-horizon multi-step tasks (codebase refactors, literature review, experiment design). SWE-bench Verified breaks 80 %.

激活函數玩具

六、激活函數玩具

調整單一神經元的 weight w 與 bias b，切換激活函數，觀察輸出曲線。直觀感受「為何 ReLU 取代 sigmoid」。

Adjust a single neuron's weight w and bias b, switch activations, and see the output curve. Build intuition for "why ReLU replaced sigmoid".

提示：注意 ReLU 在 x < 0 完全為 0（梯度也為 0），而 sigmoid 兩端飽和梯度趨近 0——這就是「梯度消失」的根源。Tip: ReLU is exactly 0 for x < 0 (and gradient = 0), while sigmoid saturates at both ends with near-zero gradient — that's the root of "vanishing gradients".

AI × 生命科學

七、生資連結點

🧬
生命科學的 ChatGPT 時刻

2020 年 AlphaFold 2 對生命科學的衝擊，相當於 2022 年 ChatGPT 對社會的衝擊。從那刻起，AI 不再是「生資中可用可不用的工具」，而是每個生資工作流的基礎元件。本節整理你必須認得的關鍵模型。

AlphaFold 2 in 2020 hit biology the way ChatGPT hit society in 2022. Since then AI isn't an optional bioinformatics tool — it's a foundational component of every workflow. This section lists the key models you must recognize.

🧪

AlphaFold 2 / 3 （結構）

AlphaFold 2（Jumper et al. Nature 2021）：MSA → Evoformer → Structure module，達到實驗精度。AlphaFold 3（Abramson et al. Nature 2024）：用 diffusion model 預測蛋白質-DNA-RNA-小分子-離子複合體。線上免費（不可商用）：alphafoldserver.com；DeepMind + EBI 已釋出 2 億預測結構庫 alphafold.ebi.ac.uk。

AlphaFold 2 (Jumper et al., Nature 2021): MSA → Evoformer → Structure module, reaching experimental accuracy. AlphaFold 3 (Abramson et al., Nature 2024): diffusion model for protein-DNA-RNA-ligand-ion complexes. Free non-commercial use at alphafoldserver.com; DeepMind + EBI ship 200 M predictions at alphafold.ebi.ac.uk.

🔬

ESMFold / OmegaFold （單序列）

Meta ESMFold（Lin et al. Science 2023）：在 6.5 億蛋白質序列上預訓 ESM-2 語言模型，不需 MSA，速度比 AlphaFold 2 快 60 倍，特別適合「孤兒蛋白」與宏基因組。ESM-3（2024）整合序列、結構、功能。

Meta ESMFold (Lin et al., Science 2023): pretrains ESM-2 on 650 M protein sequences. No MSA needed; 60× faster than AlphaFold 2 — perfect for orphan proteins and metagenomes. ESM-3 (2024) jointly models sequence, structure, and function.

🧬

scGPT / Geneformer / scFoundation （單細胞）

「單細胞基礎模型 (cell FM)」是 2023–2024 熱點：scGPT（Cui et al. Nat Methods 2024）在 3300 萬細胞訓練、Geneformer（Theodoris et al. Nature 2023）3000 萬、scFoundation（Hao et al. Nat Methods 2024）5000 萬、UCE（Rosen et al. 2023）360 萬細胞跨物種。下游可細胞型別註釋、攝動預測、批次校正。

"Cell foundation models" became hot in 2023–2024: scGPT (Cui et al. Nat Methods 2024) trained on 33 M cells; Geneformer (Theodoris et al. Nature 2023) 30 M; scFoundation (Hao et al. Nat Methods 2024) 50 M; UCE (Rosen et al. 2023) 3.6 M cross-species. Downstream: cell-type annotation, perturbation prediction, batch correction.

🧪

DeepVariant （變異呼叫）

Google Brain DeepVariant（Poplin et al. Nat Biotech 2018）：把 NGS 變異呼叫變成「影像分類」——pileup 視為圖片，CNN 判斷 SNP/indel。其準確率超越 GATK HaplotypeCaller，特別在 PacBio HiFi 與 Oxford Nanopore 長讀長表現更佳。已成為 Illumina DRAGEN、PacBio HiFi 預設 pipeline 之一。

Google Brain's DeepVariant (Poplin et al., Nat Biotech 2018) treats NGS variant calling as image classification — pileups become images, a CNN decides SNP/indel. Beats GATK HaplotypeCaller, especially on PacBio HiFi and Oxford Nanopore long reads. Now a default pipeline in Illumina DRAGEN and PacBio HiFi.

💊

DiffDock / RFdiffusion （生成式）

2023–2024 擴散模型 (diffusion) 進入生命科學：MIT DiffDock（Corso et al. ICLR 2023）將分子對接從「打分」轉為「生成」；Baker Lab RFdiffusion（Watson et al. Nature 2023）能從零設計結合特定靶點的新蛋白——已有實驗驗證的 picomolar 親和力結合物。

Diffusion models entered life science in 2023–2024: MIT's DiffDock (Corso et al., ICLR 2023) reframes docking from scoring to generation; Baker Lab's RFdiffusion (Watson et al., Nature 2023) de novo designs proteins binding given targets — with experimentally validated picomolar binders.

🎯

DeepBind / DeepCRISPR / Enformer （基因調控）

DeepBind（Alipanahi et al. Nat Biotech 2015）：CNN 學 TF binding。DeepCRISPR：預測 Cas9 切割效率與脫靶。Enformer（Avsec et al. Nat Methods 2021）：Transformer 預測長距 (200 kb) 的基因表現。Borzoi（Linder et al. 2024）：把 Enformer 擴大到 524 kb。

DeepBind (Alipanahi et al., Nat Biotech 2015): CNN for TF binding. DeepCRISPR: Cas9 efficiency & off-target prediction. Enformer (Avsec et al., Nat Methods 2021): Transformer predicting expression over 200 kb. Borzoi (Linder et al., 2024) extends to 524 kb.

💊

MolGAN / Atomwise / Insilico Medicine （藥物 AI）

MolGAN（De Cao & Kipf 2018）：GAN 生成新型小分子；REINVENT 4（AstraZeneca 2024）目前最強分子生成器。Atomwise 用 CNN-based AtomNet 篩選 10⁹ 化合物。Insilico Medicine 的 INS018_055（生成式設計的抗肺纖維化）2024 進入 Phase IIa 臨床，是首個全由 AI 設計的進入人體試驗的小分子。

MolGAN (De Cao & Kipf, 2018): GAN-generated small molecules. REINVENT 4 (AstraZeneca 2024) is the current strongest generator. Atomwise screens 10⁹ compounds with AtomNet CNN. Insilico Medicine's INS018_055 (AI-designed anti-fibrotic) reached Phase IIa in 2024 — the first fully AI-designed small molecule in human trials.

🧠

scBERT / UCE / GeneCompass （跨物種）

第二波 cell FM 主打跨物種、跨組織：scBERT（Yang et al. Nat MI 2022）首批 BERT 風格；UCE (Universal Cell Embedding)（Rosen et al. 2023）含 360 萬細胞、8 個物種；GeneCompass（Yang et al. 2023）人鼠對比預訓。2024 評估警示（Kedzierska et al. Genome Biol）：cell FM 對細胞型別分類「常輸給線性 baseline」——FM 真正價值仍在攝動預測與罕見細胞型別。

The 2nd wave of cell FMs focus on cross-species, cross-tissue: scBERT (Yang et al., Nat MI 2022) — first BERT-style; UCE (Rosen et al., 2023) — 3.6 M cells, 8 species; GeneCompass (Yang et al., 2023) — joint human-mouse pretraining. 2024 benchmark warning (Kedzierska et al., Genome Biol): cell FMs often lose to linear baselines on cell-type classification — their real value is in perturbation prediction and rare cell types.

🩺

MedSAM / RETFound / CheXzero （影像基礎模型）

醫學影像也進入「基礎模型」時代：MedSAM（Ma et al. Nat Comm 2024）改 Meta SAM，2D 醫學影像通用分割；RETFound（Zhou et al. Nature 2023）視網膜眼底圖預訓，下游同時診斷糖網、心血管、Parkinson；CheXzero (Tiu et al. Nat BME 2022) 零樣本胸 X 光分類。FDA 2024 已核可 600+ AI/ML 醫療裝置（vs 2020 僅 64 個）。

Medical imaging enters the "foundation model" era: MedSAM (Ma et al., Nat Comm 2024) adapts Meta SAM for universal 2D medical segmentation; RETFound (Zhou et al., Nature 2023) is pretrained on retinal fundus, downstream diagnoses diabetic retinopathy, cardiovascular events, Parkinson's; CheXzero (Tiu et al., Nat BME 2022) is zero-shot chest X-ray classification. FDA cleared 600+ AI/ML devices by 2024 (vs 64 in 2020).

Always verify citations. (2) Data leakage — pasting PHI / patient IDs into ChatGPT violates HIPAA. For hospital use, deploy HIPAA-compliant Azure OpenAI or Anthropic Bedrock private endpoints.

// ---------- 梯度下降的核心 ----------
function GradientDescent(model θ, data D, lr η, epochs T):
    for t = 1 to T:
        for each mini-batch (X, y) in shuffle(D):
            ŷ  = model.forward(X, θ)
            L  = loss(ŷ, y)
            g  = ∇_θ L          // 反向傳播（鏈式法則）
            θ  = θ − η · g       // 參數更新
    return θ

// ---------- Adam 優化器 ----------
function AdamStep(θ, g, m, v, t, lr, β1=0.9, β2=0.999, ε=1e-8):
    m = β1·m + (1−β1)·g           // 一階動量
    v = β2·v + (1−β2)·g²          // 二階動量
    m̂ = m / (1 − β1^t)            // bias correction
    v̂ = v / (1 − β2^t)
    θ = θ − lr · m̂ / (√v̂ + ε)
    return θ, m, v

// ---------- Train / Val / Test 嚴格分割 ----------
function ProperPipeline(D):
    split D into (train 70%, val 15%, test 15%)
    for each hyperparam config H:
        model = Train(train, H)
        val_score = Eval(model, val)
    pick best H, retrain on train+val

陷阱

十、常見誤解

❌ 「AI 會思考」

目前所有 LLM 都是模式匹配（pattern matching on tokens）。它沒有信念、沒有目標、沒有自我意識。看起來「會推理」是因為訓練資料夾雜大量推理範例。混淆「能模擬推理」與「真的有意識」是人類心理學偏誤，不是科學事實。

All current LLMs are pattern matchers on tokens. They have no beliefs, goals, or self-awareness. They "reason" because training data contained reasoning. Conflating "simulates reasoning" with "is conscious" is a human psychological bias, not science.

❌ 「DL 永遠贏」

在表格資料（醫療記錄、實驗測量、客戶 CRM），XGBoost / Random Forest 經常打贏 DL。Grinsztajn et al. 2022 在 NeurIPS 用 45 個 benchmark 系統證明這點。除非你有 100 萬+ 筆資料、特徵有局部結構（影像、文字、序列），否則先用 GBT。

For tabular data (clinical records, lab measurements, CRM), XGBoost / RF often beat DL. Grinsztajn et al. (NeurIPS 2022) showed this across 45 benchmarks. Unless you have 1 M+ rows with local structure (images, text, sequences), start with GBTs.

❌ 「LLM 不會錯」

LLM 會幻覺 (hallucinate)——自信地編造不存在的事實，特別是 PubMed PMID、Bioconductor 函數簽名、化學分子式。2024 醫學研究顯示連 GPT-4 在臨床問答仍有 5–10% 顯著錯誤率。永遠驗證引用、永遠 code-review LLM 寫的程式。

LLMs hallucinate — confidently fabricate PMIDs, Bioconductor signatures, chemical formulas. 2024 medical studies show GPT-4 still makes 5–10 % material errors in clinical QA. Always verify citations; always code-review what an LLM wrote.

❌ 「Accuracy 高就好」

癌症篩檢：99% 健康 + 1% 患病。「永遠預測健康」就有 99% accuracy，但抓不到任何病人。在不平衡資料用 F1、PR-AUC、Sensitivity@Specificity；臨床尤其關注 NPV / PPV。

Cancer screening: 99 % healthy + 1 % sick. "Always predict healthy" hits 99 % accuracy but misses every patient. On imbalanced data use F1, PR-AUC, Sensitivity@Specificity; clinics also need NPV / PPV.

❌ 「資料越多越好」

髒資料（標籤噪音、bias）越多越糟。Andrew Ng 提倡「data-centric AI」：花時間清洗 1000 筆高品質資料勝過 1 萬筆爛資料。Garbage in, garbage out。

Garbage in, garbage out. Dirty data (label noise, bias) gets worse at scale. Andrew Ng's "data-centric AI" mantra: 1,000 well-curated rows beat 10,000 sloppy ones.

❌ 「Scaling 永無止境」

2020–2023 大家相信「再大 10 倍就好」。2024 起多家報告 pretraining 邊際效益遞減，OpenAI、Anthropic、Google 紛紛轉向 test-time compute（推理時花更多算力，如 chain-of-thought）。「scaling = AGI」是過去式。

2020–2023 we all believed "just 10× bigger". From 2024 multiple labs report diminishing pretraining returns; OpenAI, Anthropic, Google pivoted to test-time compute (CoT, search). "Scaling = AGI" is past tense.

❌ 「開源永遠輸」

2024 起開源模型已大幅縮短差距：DeepSeek-V3 / R1（2025）以 1/10 訓練成本逼近 GPT-4 等級；Llama 4、Qwen 3、Mistral Large 2 在多基準與封閉模型互有勝負。對生資人更有意義：可本地部署，PHI 不出院、可審計、可微調。差距從「24 個月」縮到「3–6 個月」。

Open weights closed the gap fast in 2024: DeepSeek-V3 / R1 (2025) approach GPT-4 at 1/10 the training cost; Llama 4, Qwen 3, Mistral Large 2 trade blows with frontier closed models. For bioinformatics this matters more: local deployment, PHI stays on-prem, audit-friendly, fine-tunable. The "24-month gap" shrank to 3–6 months.
