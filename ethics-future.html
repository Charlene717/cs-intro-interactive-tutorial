CHAPTER 16 / 16

資訊倫理與新興科技

從 1951 年 Henrietta Lacks 的 HeLa 細胞，到 2024 EU AI Act 與 IBM 量子晶片——技術中性，但使用方式不是。最後一章不是「課程結束」，是邀請你開始持續的倫理對話。

From Henrietta Lacks's 1951 HeLa cells to the 2024 EU AI Act and IBM quantum chips — technology is neutral, but its use is not. The final chapter isn't an ending; it's an invitation to start a lifelong ethical conversation.

HeLa 細胞與一場 70 年的倫理對話

一、Henrietta Lacks 的細胞

1951 年 2 月，美國馬里蘭州 Johns Hopkins 醫院，一位 31 歲的非裔美國婦女 Henrietta Lacks 因子宮頸癌就醫。主治醫師 Howard Jones 在切片時取走了她的腫瘤組織，沒有經過她或家屬的同意。

那塊組織後來被培養出第一個能在實驗室無限增殖的人類細胞株—HeLa。HeLa 細胞參與了小兒麻痺疫苗開發、IVF 技術、HPV/HIV 研究、太空輻射實驗、基因體解碼……至今已被引用 11 萬篇論文、創造數十億美元的生技產業。Henrietta 本人於同年 10 月過世，享年 31 歲。她的家族則直到 1973 年才得知此事，因醫療帳單與基因隱私問題抗爭了 50 年——2013 年才獲得 NIH 對 HeLa 基因組存取權的完整協議。

這個故事是一面鏡子：技術本身是中性的——HeLa 救了百萬人——但「誰被取走了什麼、誰得到了什麼、誰被諮詢過」這些倫理問題從未失效。本章我們會看到，1951 年的議題以新的形式（AI bias、基因組隱私、AI 武器、演算法歧視）持續上演。

February 1951, Johns Hopkins Hospital, Maryland. A 31-year-old Black woman, Henrietta Lacks, came in with cervical cancer. Dr. Howard Jones took a piece of her tumour for biopsy without her or her family's consent.

That tissue gave rise to the first human cell line that could proliferate indefinitely in the lab — HeLa. HeLa cells went on to drive the polio vaccine, IVF, HPV/HIV research, space-radiation experiments, and the Human Genome Project — cited in 110,000+ papers and underpinning a multi-billion-dollar biotech industry. Henrietta died in October 1951 at age 31. Her family wasn't told the cells were being used until 1973, then fought medical bills and genetic-privacy battles for 50 years — not until 2013 did they finally have a formal NIH agreement on HeLa genome access.

The story is a mirror. The technology is neutral — HeLa saved millions — but the ethical questions of "who was taken from, who benefited, who was consulted" never go out of date. This chapter shows the same 1951 issues reappearing in new forms: AI bias, genome privacy, AI weapons, algorithmic discrimination.

給初學者的入口

二、倫理五大支柱

🔐

隱私

GDPR（歐盟 2018）、HIPAA（美國 1996）、PDPA（台灣 2010 / 大幅修訂中）規範個資的蒐集、處理、刪除、跨境傳輸。違規最高罰金可達年營收 4%。

GDPR (EU, 2018), HIPAA (US, 1996), PDPA (Taiwan, 2010 / overhaul pending) govern the collection, processing, deletion, and cross-border transfer of personal data. Fines reach 4 % of annual revenue.

⚖️

AI 倫理

核心四項：Fairness 公平、Accountability 究責、Transparency 透明、Explainability 可解釋（合稱 FATE）。新增 Safety（不傷害）、Robustness（對抗攻擊）。

Core four: Fairness, Accountability, Transparency, Explainability (FATE). Plus Safety (do no harm) and Robustness (against adversarial attacks).

🌐

數位落差

全球仍有 26 億人沒有網路（ITU 2024）。AI 與雲端可能拉大階級差距：高收入機構享有 H100 訓練 vs 偏鄉醫院連 PACS 都沒升級。包容性設計 (inclusive design)、WCAG 2.2 無障礙是必要措施。

The world still has 2.6 billion people offline (ITU 2024). AI and cloud can widen class gaps — wealthy institutions train on H100s while rural hospitals can't even upgrade PACS. Inclusive design and WCAG 2.2 accessibility are essentials.

📜

智慧財產

專利 (CRISPR 戰)、版權 (AI 訓練資料官司)、商標、商業秘密。開源授權：MIT / BSD（最寬）→ Apache 2.0（含專利條款）→ GPL（強 copyleft）→ AGPL（連 SaaS 用都需公開）。

Patents (CRISPR wars), copyright (AI training-data lawsuits), trademarks, trade secrets. OSS licenses: MIT/BSD (most permissive) → Apache 2.0 (with patent grant) → GPL (strong copyleft) → AGPL (covers SaaS use).

🌱

綠色運算

資料中心已占全球用電 2–4%（IEA 2024）。訓練一個 GPT-3 估計排放 552 噸 CO₂（≈ 跨大西洋來回 300 次）。PUE（Power Usage Effectiveness）、液冷、核能 PPA（Microsoft 簽 Three Mile Island）成為新議題。

Data centres now consume 2–4 % of global electricity (IEA 2024). Training a single GPT-3 emitted an estimated 552 t CO₂ (≈ 300 round-trip transatlantic flights). PUE, liquid cooling, and nuclear PPAs (Microsoft contracted Three Mile Island) are now mainstream.

⚔️

武器化

AI 是雙刃劍：協助診斷癌症，也能用於致命自主武器系統 (LAWS)、Deepfake 假訊息、選舉操控（2024 多國選舉年）。聯合國《特定常規武器公約》仍在辯論 LAWS 全球禁令。

AI cuts both ways: it spots cancer and powers lethal autonomous weapon systems (LAWS), deepfakes, and election manipulation (2024 was a global election supercycle). The UN CCW still debates a global LAWS ban.

進階：EU AI Act / ACM Code / WCAG

四、深入剖析

EU AI Act — 2024 全球首部 AI 法律

2024 年 8 月正式生效，分階段執行至 2027。AI 系統按風險分四級：

  • ① Unacceptable 不可接受（禁止）：社會評分、即時公共空間人臉辨識、操弄性 AI（如針對兒童）。違者最高罰 3,500 萬歐元或全球年營收 7%。
  • ② High 高風險（嚴格規範）：醫療診斷、招募、信用評分、執法、教育評等、關鍵基礎設施。必須完成 conformity assessment、CE marking、人為監督、保留紀錄 10 年。
  • ③ Limited 有限風險（透明度）：聊天機器人、Deepfake——必須明示「這是 AI 生成」。
  • ④ Minimal 最小風險（自願）：垃圾郵件過濾、推薦系統——遵守 Code of Conduct 即可。

GPAI / Foundation Models（GPT-5、Claude Opus 4.7 等）有獨立規範：須揭露訓練資料摘要、版權合規、評估系統性風險（> 10²⁵ FLOPs 訓練）。

Came into force August 2024, phasing in through 2027. AI systems by risk tier:

  • ① Unacceptable (banned): social scoring, real-time public-space face recognition, manipulative AI (e.g. targeting children). Fines up to €35 M or 7 % of global turnover.
  • ② High-risk (strict): medical diagnosis, hiring, credit scoring, law enforcement, education grading, critical infrastructure. Requires conformity assessment, CE marking, human oversight, 10-year logging.
  • ③ Limited risk (transparency): chatbots, deepfakes — must disclose "AI-generated".
  • ④ Minimal risk (voluntary): spam filters, recommenders — Code of Conduct suffices.

GPAI / foundation models (GPT-5, Claude Opus 4.7 etc.) face dedicated rules: training-data summaries, copyright compliance, systemic-risk evaluation (above 10²⁵ FLOPs training).

ACM Code of Ethics 7 大原則

美國 ACM (Association for Computing Machinery) 1992 公布、2018 大修。所有計算機專業人員的七大基本原則：

  1. 1.1 對社會與人類福祉做出貢獻——預設立場是「行善」。
  2. 1.2 避免傷害——任何時候 do no harm 優先。
  3. 1.3 誠實值得信賴——不誤導、不掩蓋系統限制。
  4. 1.4 公平、不歧視——主動審視 AI bias。
  5. 1.5 尊重智慧財產與創作。
  6. 1.6 尊重隱私——資料最小化原則。
  7. 1.7 維護機密——但發現重大風險須揭發 (whistleblowing)。

The US ACM Code (1992, major rewrite 2018). Seven foundational principles for every computing professional:

  1. 1.1 Contribute to society and human well-being — default to "do good".
  2. 1.2 Avoid harm — first, do no harm.
  3. 1.3 Be honest and trustworthy — don't mislead about system limits.
  4. 1.4 Be fair, take action against discrimination — actively audit AI bias.
  5. 1.5 Respect IP and creative work.
  6. 1.6 Respect privacy — data minimization by default.
  7. 1.7 Honour confidentiality — but blow the whistle on serious harms.

GDPR Article 22 — 不被演算法定罪的權利

GDPR 第 22 條規定：「資料主體有權不受僅基於自動化處理（包括 profiling）所作出對其產生法律效果或重大影響的決策約束。」例：銀行純 AI 拒貸、學校演算法 fail 學生、招募系統淘汰履歷——須提供「人為審查 + 解釋權」。這對 deep learning 是嚴峻挑戰：黑盒模型如何「解釋」？SHAP、LIME、Integrated Gradients 等 XAI 工具因此成為合規必備。

GDPR Article 22: data subjects have the right "not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing (including profiling) which produces legal effects or similarly significant effects". Examples: a bank denying credit purely by AI, a school flunking via algorithm, an HR system rejecting CVs — these require human review and the right to explanation. A serious challenge to deep learning: how do you "explain" a black box? XAI tools like SHAP, LIME, and Integrated Gradients become compliance-essential.

WCAG 2.2 — 無障礙 4 大原則

Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 由 W3C 維護，2.2 版（2023 發布）。POUR 四原則：

  • Perceivable 可感知：圖片需有 alt text、影片需字幕、色彩對比足夠（≥ 4.5:1）。
  • Operable 可操作：所有功能可用鍵盤操作、不會誘發癲癇、提供足夠時間。
  • Understandable 可理解：文字清楚、操作可預測、錯誤訊息明確。
  • Robust 穩健：相容輔助科技（screen reader）、語意 HTML、ARIA 屬性。

合規等級：A（最低）、AA（業界標準）、AAA（最高）。歐盟 European Accessibility Act 2025 強制公共網站達 AA。

Maintained by W3C; 2.2 released 2023. POUR principles:

  • Perceivable: alt text for images, captions for video, sufficient contrast (≥ 4.5:1).
  • Operable: all features keyboard-accessible, no seizure-inducing flashes, ample time.
  • Understandable: clear text, predictable interactions, explicit error messages.
  • Robust: works with assistive tech (screen readers), semantic HTML, ARIA attributes.

Conformance levels: A (min), AA (industry standard), AAA (highest). The 2025 EU European Accessibility Act mandates AA on public-facing sites.

9 個改寫倫理對話的案例

五、歷史案例

1951 — 未經同意的細胞

HeLa 細胞推動現代醫學（小兒麻痺疫苗、IVF、HPV/HIV 研究），但取得未經 Henrietta 同意。家族 50 年抗爭至 2013 才有 NIH 完整協議。啟示：知情同意 + 利益共享 + 對少數族裔的尊重。

HeLa drove modern medicine (polio vaccine, IVF, HPV/HIV) but was taken without consent. The family fought 50 years until a formal NIH agreement in 2013. Lessons: informed consent, benefit sharing, respect for minoritized communities.

1985–87 — 史上最致命軟體 bug

加拿大 AECL 的放射治療儀 Therac-25 在 1985–87 因 race condition 軟體 bug，在 6 名患者身上施加致命劑量（百倍正常值）。其中 3 人死亡。啟示：軟體即生命安全；醫療軟體必須有 fail-safe 與獨立硬體互鎖。

Canadian AECL's Therac-25 radiation machine, due to a race-condition bug, delivered lethal (100×) doses to 6 patients in 1985–87. Three died. Lessons: software is life-safety; medical software demands fail-safes and independent hardware interlocks.

2008 — 演算法操弄民主

劍橋分析利用 8,700 萬 Facebook 帳號心理特徵建模，用於 2016 美國總統選舉與英國脫歐廣告投放。事件揭發後 Facebook 在 2019 被 FTC 罰 50 億美元，歐盟 GDPR 即此事件後加速通過。

Cambridge Analytica psychographed 87 M Facebook profiles to target ads in the 2016 US election and Brexit. After exposure, the FTC fined Facebook $5 B in 2019; the EU's GDPR was finalized in this aftermath.

2018 — 員工拒絕 AI 武器

3,000 名 Google 員工連署，反對為美國國防部開發 AI 軍事影像分析（Project Maven）。Google 最終放棄合約並發布 AI Principles。微軟員工也曾類似反對 HoloLens 軍用合約。啟示：技術人有拒絕參與的道德權利。

3,000 Google employees signed a letter opposing AI for the US DoD's Project Maven. Google dropped the contract and published its AI Principles. Microsoft staff similarly resisted HoloLens military contracts. Lessons: engineers have a moral right to refuse.

2020 — 「Stochastic Parrots」

Google 倫理 AI 主管 Timnit Gebru 共同撰寫論文「On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots」，警告 LLM 的環境成本、編碼偏見、潛在傷害。Google 要求她撤回論文，她拒絕後被解僱。事件揭露大型科技公司對內部倫理研究的緊張關係。

Google's Ethical AI co-lead Timnit Gebru co-authored "On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots" warning of LLMs' environmental cost, encoded bias, and potential harms. Google asked her to retract; she refused and was fired. The case exposed Big Tech's tension with internal ethics research.

2020 — 演算法把學生 fail 掉

因 COVID 取消大考，英國 Ofqual 用演算法估分。結果下調 40% 私校學生分數、影響弱勢學生入學，引發全國抗議。一週後政府撤回演算法，改用教師預測分數。啟示：高風險決策的演算法必須 audit、必須可上訴。

With exams cancelled due to COVID, the UK's Ofqual used an algorithm to estimate grades. It downgraded 40 % of students disproportionately from disadvantaged backgrounds, sparking nationwide protests. A week later the algorithm was scrapped for teacher predictions. Lessons: high-stakes algorithms need audits and appeals.

2023 — 透明度新基準

OpenAI 發布 GPT-4 時附 System Card（98 頁）詳述 red-teaming、潛在風險、限制，成為新業界標準。Anthropic Constitutional AI、Model Card、Responsible Scaling Policy跟進。研究模型偏見與安全成為顯學。

OpenAI shipped GPT-4 with a 98-page System Card detailing red-teaming, risks, and limits — setting a new industry standard. Anthropic's Constitutional AI, Model Cards, Responsible Scaling Policy followed. Studying model bias and safety became mainstream.

2024 — 全球首部 AI 法

歐盟 AI Act 於 2024 年 8 月生效，全球首部全面 AI 監管法律。美國加州 2024 SB 1047（AI Safety）雖被否決，但同年加州通過多項 AI 揭露與兒童保護法。台灣《AI 基本法》草案於 2024 公開徵詢中。

The EU AI Act came into force August 2024 — the world's first comprehensive AI law. California's 2024 SB 1047 (AI Safety) was vetoed but the state passed several AI disclosure and child-protection laws the same year. Taiwan's "AI Basic Act" draft entered public consultation in 2024.

2024–2025 — Google Willow & IBM Nighthawk

2024 Google Willow（105 qubit）首次實證「below threshold」表面碼糾錯——加 qubit 真的能降低邏輯錯誤率。2025 IBM Nighthawk 120 qubit + Heron 處理器；PsiQuantum 朝光子百萬 qubit。後量子密碼遷移因此成為國家級議題。

2024 Google Willow (105 qubits) first demonstrated "below-threshold" surface-code error correction — adding qubits actually reduces the logical error rate. 2025 IBM Nighthawk: 120 qubits + Heron processor; PsiQuantum targets photonic million-qubit systems. Post-quantum cryptography migration became a national-level agenda.

倫理 × 生命科學

七、生資連結點

🧬
為什麼是你？

生資工作直接觸及個體身分（基因組）、家族隱私（遺傳資訊 50% overlap）、族裔健康公平（GWAS 主要來自歐裔人群）。你寫的每一行 pipeline、每一個 AI 模型，都可能影響真實病人的人生。倫理素養不是「人文加分項」，是專業必修。

Bioinformatics directly touches individual identity (genomes), family privacy (50 % overlap with relatives), and ethnic health equity (GWAS data still skew European). Every pipeline line and AI model you ship can change a real patient's life. Ethics literacy isn't a "humanities bonus" — it's a professional requirement.

🧬

HeLa 案的當代回響：基因組隱私 HeLa 的延伸

2013 年 Lacks family 才獲得 HeLa 基因組存取的完整協議——但 HeLa 序列早已公開十多年。教訓：基因隱私不只「資料安全」，更是對族群的尊重。今日相關規範：GA4GH Framework for Responsible Sharing、Indigenous Data Sovereignty（CARE Principles 2020）、NIH Genomic Data Sharing Policy。

The Lacks family didn't get a formal NIH HeLa-genome agreement until 2013 — though the sequence had been public for over a decade. Lesson: genomic privacy is more than "data security"; it's respect for communities. Modern frameworks: GA4GH Framework for Responsible Sharing, Indigenous Data Sovereignty (CARE Principles, 2020), NIH Genomic Data Sharing Policy.

📋

GINA、PDPA 與基因保險歧視 GINA

美國 GINA Act 2008（Genetic Information Nondiscrimination Act）禁止健康保險與雇主依基因資訊歧視。但不涵蓋人壽、長照、傷殘保險。台灣 PDPA 對基因資料保護有限，醫療基因檢測前須充分告知保險使用風險。2024 美國數個壽險公司因藏匿基因檢測史拒保被起訴。

The US GINA Act 2008 bans health insurers and employers from discriminating on genetic information — but life, long-term care, and disability insurance are exempt. Taiwan's PDPA gives limited genetic-data protection; clinics must clearly warn patients about insurance implications. Several US life insurers were sued in 2024 for using undisclosed genetic-test history.

🎨

All of Us 與差異隱私 All of Us

NIH All of Us（2018 啟動，2024 已招募 80 萬+ 人）特別注重多元族裔（> 50% 來自代表性不足族群）。資料以三層架構保護：① 公開層加 ε-DP 噪音；② 受控層需 IRB；③ 原始資料絕不出 NIH Researcher Workbench（雲端 enclave）。是隱私 + 效用 + 包容性的範本。

NIH's All of Us (launched 2018, 800 k+ enrolled by 2024) prioritises diversity (> 50 % from under-represented populations). Three-tier protection: (1) Public Tier uses ε-DP noise; (2) Controlled Tier requires IRB; (3) Raw data never leaves the NIH Researcher Workbench (cloud enclave). A canonical "privacy + utility + inclusion" model.

AI 偏見與臨床健康公平 AI bias

🌳 ③ 我的模型有偏誤嗎？

Step 1:
檢查訓練資料的族群分布（性別、族裔、年齡）。GWAS 80% 來自歐裔人群是事實。
Step 2:

Step 5:

Step 1:
Check training-data demographics (sex, ethnicity, age). 80 % of GWAS data is European — that's a fact.
Step 2:
Run disaggregated evaluation: are accuracy, F1, PPV consistent across subgroups?
Step 3:
Use fairness metrics: Demographic Parity, Equalized Odds, Calibration (note Kleinberg's impossibility theorem — you usually can't satisfy all three).
Step 4:
Use XAI tools: SHAP, LIME, Integrated Gradients to see which features the model relies on.

Fix: (1) rebalance data; (2) use fairlearn, AIF360, or themis-ml; (3) co-design with affected communities.

十、常見誤解

❌ 「匿名化就安全」

2008 Narayanan 從 Netflix 匿名觀影紀錄重識別個體；2013 MIT 從「匿名」手機定位點即可識別 95% 個體；2018 從基因組 + 親屬 DNA 庫可重識別匿名 GEDmatch 樣本（Golden State Killer 案）。真正的隱私保護需要 differential privacy 或 secure enclaves，不是改名字。

2008: Narayanan re-identified Netflix users from anonymized ratings; 2013: MIT showed 95 % of people are identifiable from anonymous mobile-location pings; 2018: the Golden State Killer was caught via genome + a relative's match in "anonymous" GEDmatch. Real privacy needs differential privacy or secure enclaves, not renaming.

❌ 「沒授權就免費」

剛好相反——無授權 = ALL RIGHTS RESERVED（依預設著作權法）。你看到 GitHub 上的 code 沒有 LICENSE 檔案，嚴格意義你不能用。發布 OSS 一定要加 LICENSE 檔，否則沒人能合法使用你的成果。

The opposite — no license = ALL RIGHTS RESERVED by default copyright law. Code on GitHub without a LICENSE file? Technically you can't use it. Always add a LICENSE — otherwise nobody can legally use your work.

❌ 「量子明年破 RSA」

媒體常誇大。實用破 RSA-2048 估計需要約 2000 萬個 logical qubit（每個 logical qubit 又需 1000+ physical qubit 糾錯），現實量子電腦離這個還很遠。但「現在收割未來解密」使敏感長期資料現在就該轉 PQC。

Media often hype. Cracking RSA-2048 needs an estimated 20 M logical qubits (each needing 1000+ physical qubits for error correction) — we're nowhere near. But "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" means sensitive long-retention data should migrate to PQC now.

❌ 「倫理交給法務」

法務通常在「事後」介入。工程師才是「設計階段」的決策者。Therac-25 案、Boeing 737 MAX MCAS 案都顯示：等到法務階段，人已經死了。倫理是工程素養，不是「他們的事」。

Lawyers usually intervene after; engineers decide at design time. Therac-25 and Boeing 737 MAX MCAS show: by the time lawyers arrive, people are already dead. Ethics is engineering literacy, not "someone else's job".

❌ 「加資料就修好偏見」

偏見根源往往是歷史不平等（標籤本身有偏），加同一來源更多資料只會放大。需要的是：① 多元採樣；② 與受影響社群 co-design；③ 重新定義「成功」指標；④ 接受某些情境下「不部署」才是正確答案。

Bias often stems from historical inequity (the labels themselves are biased); more same-source data amplifies it. Real fixes: (1) diverse sampling; (2) co-design with affected communities; (3) redefine "success" metrics; (4) accept that "don't deploy" is sometimes the right answer.

❌ 「綠色運算只是行銷」

2024 全球資料中心耗電已逼近全日本年用電量。Microsoft 為了 AI 訓練電力，2024 簽下與 Three Mile Island 核電廠的 20 年購電合約。AWS、Google 也大舉投資核能。這不是 CSR——是實質運營成本與基礎設施戰略。

2024 global data-centre power use approaches Japan's annual electricity consumption. Microsoft signed a 20-year PPA with Three Mile Island in 2024 just to feed AI training; AWS and Google are heavily investing in nuclear too. This isn't CSR — it's real operational cost and infrastructure strategy.

  2. Who owns AI-generated work? The US Copyright Office ruled in 2023 that "pure AI output has no copyright"; courts globally are still hearing 2024 cases. When AI-written code or images make millions — who owns it? The training-data authors? The AI company? The prompter?
  3. 全球 AI 治理可能嗎？EU AI Act 是區域性的；GPAI 規範對中國模型 (DeepSeek、Qwen) 有效嗎？聯合國 2024 通過首個 AI 決議，但無強制力。如果各國各行其是，會出現「AI 監管套利」（在最寬鬆地區開發）。你認為全球治理的可能路徑是？
  4. Is global AI governance possible? The EU AI Act is regional. Does GPAI regulation reach Chinese models (DeepSeek, Qwen)? The UN passed its first AI resolution in 2024 but it's non-binding. If countries diverge, "AI regulatory arbitrage" emerges. What viable global-governance path do you see?

  5. 「Right to Explanation」會限制 deep learning 嗎？如果每個臨床 AI 都必須能「解釋為什麼診斷你癌症」，黑盒 DL 必須改為可解釋模型嗎？還是 SHAP / LIME 等事後解釋就夠？這會不會反而讓 AI 部署更保守、進步更慢？
  6. Will "right to explanation" cripple deep learning? If every clinical AI must "explain why you have cancer", do we have to abandon black-box DL for interpretable models? Or are post-hoc SHAP/LIME enough? Could this slow AI deployment overall?

  7. 我願意分享我的全基因組嗎？個人題：若 NIH All of Us（或台灣國家級生醫資料庫）邀請你貢獻 WGS + 臨床表型，你會嗎？為什麼會 / 不會？這個決定會如何影響你 50 年後的子女？
  8. Would I share my own genome? Personal question: if NIH All of Us (or Taiwan's planned national biobank) invited you to contribute WGS + clinical phenotype, would you? Why or why not? How does this decision affect your children 50 years from now?

  9. 從本課程帶走的一句話：從計算機歷史的 ENIAC 到本章的 Henrietta Lacks——16 章告訴你的最重要事情是什麼？寫下一句你會記得 10 年的話，分享給未來的自己。
  10. One sentence from this course: From ENIAC in Chapter 1 to Henrietta Lacks here in Chapter 16 — what's the single most important thing you'll take away? Write one sentence you'll still remember in 10 years, and share it with your future self.
  11. 50 年後計算機看起來會怎樣？當下我們已看到量子糾錯臨界、神經型晶片 (Intel Loihi, IBM NorthPole)、DNA 儲存（微軟 +華盛頓大學）。50 年後最可能延續至今的技術是什麼？最可能完全消失或改變的是？
  12. What will computing look like in 50 years? We now see quantum error correction at threshold, neuromorphic chips (Intel Loihi, IBM NorthPole), and DNA storage (Microsoft + UW). Which of today's technologies are most likely to still be around in 50 years? Which will vanish or be unrecognizable?
  13. 對自己的工程實踐立下一個承諾：挑一個你未來 12 個月會做的具體倫理行動——例如「為每個 ML 專案做 disaggregated evaluation」、「公司 OSS 一律加 LICENSE」、「拒絕參與設計不能審計的醫療 AI」。寫下來、告訴一位同事，請對方半年後問你是否做到。
  14. Make one engineering-ethics commitment to yourself: pick a concrete action you'll take in the next 12 months — e.g., "run disaggregated evaluation on every ML project", "always add a LICENSE to company OSS", "decline to design clinical AI without auditability". Write it down, tell a colleague, and ask them to check in 6 months from now.
  15. 強制醫療資料端到端加密 vs AI 輔助診斷的權衡：有人主張所有 EHR + 基因組必須端到端加密。但這會讓 AI 輔助診斷、跨院 referral、population research 變慢甚至無法執行。同態加密、聯邦學習、安全多方計算能不能成為兩全方案？你願犧牲多少效能換隱私？
  16. Mandatory E2E encryption vs AI-assisted diagnosis trade-off: Some argue all EHRs + genomes must be end-to-end encrypted. But this slows AI diagnosis, cross-hospital referral, and population research. Can homomorphic encryption, federated learning, secure multi-party compute bridge the gap? How much performance would you sacrifice for privacy?
