資訊倫理與新興科技

從 GDPR、EU AI Act、無障礙、開源授權到量子運算、區塊鏈、XR 與腦機介面——技術是中性的，使用方式不是。

From GDPR, EU AI Act, accessibility, open-source licenses to quantum, blockchain, XR, and BCI — technology is neutral, its use is not.

概覽

為什麼工程師要懂倫理？

你寫的程式可能決定誰拿到貸款、誰的履歷被刷掉、誰的車能在路上自駕。2026 年全球至少有 130 個國家或地區制定資料保護或 AI 法規，「不知者無罪」已不適用。本章覆蓋三大主題：① 隱私與法規；② AI 倫理；③ 智財與綠色運算；並回顧五項正在改變未來十年的新興技術。

The code you write may decide who gets a loan, whose résumé is screened out, whose car may self-drive. By 2026 at least 130 countries/regions have data-protection or AI laws — ignorance is no longer a defence. This chapter covers (1) privacy and regulation, (2) AI ethics, (3) IP and green computing, and surveys five emerging technologies reshaping the next decade.

💡
核心觀念：Lawrence Lessig 1999 提出 「Code is law」——軟體預設值有時比立法更能塑造人類行為。Facebook 的演算法、Google 的搜尋排序、TikTok 的推薦……都是隱形的「規則」。所以負責任地設計系統，就是負責任地塑造社會。 Core idea: Lawrence Lessig (1999): "Code is law." Software defaults can shape human behavior more than statutes do. Facebook's algorithm, Google's ranking, TikTok's feed — all are invisible "rules." Designing systems responsibly is shaping society responsibly.

EU AI Act 風險分級：① 不可接受（禁用）：社會評分、利用弱勢族群、即時公共生物辨識；② 高風險（嚴格規範）：招募、信用、教育、執法、關鍵基礎建設；③ 有限風險（透明義務）：聊天機器人需告知是 AI；④ 最小風險（自由使用）：垃圾郵件過濾、遊戲 AI。違反最高罰 3,500 萬歐元或 7% 全球營收。
EU AI Act tiers: (1) Unacceptable — banned (social scoring, exploiting vulnerable groups, real-time public biometrics); (2) High — strict rules (hiring, credit, education, law enforcement, critical infra); (3) Limited — transparency (chatbots must disclose); (4) Minimal — free use. Max fine: €35M or 7% of global revenue.
AI 倫理與無障礙

二、AI 倫理、無障礙、授權

AI 倫理四支柱：

  • 偏誤 (Bias)：訓練資料反映社會不平等。亞馬遜 2018 履歷篩選 AI 對女性不利，後被棄用。
  • 公平性 (Fairness)：常用指標包括「群體公平」(demographic parity)、「機會公平」(equal opportunity)，且彼此可能互斥。
  • 可解釋性 (XAI)：高風險決策（信用、醫療）必須能解釋。工具：SHAP、LIME、Integrated Gradients。
  • 責任 (Accountability)：AI 出錯時誰負責——開發者、部署者、還是 AI？目前法規傾向「部署者」。

數位落差 (Digital Divide) & 無障礙 (a11y)：全球仍有近 30% 人口沒有寬頻網路。無障礙網頁標準 WCAG 2.2（2023 發布）涵蓋鍵盤操作、色彩對比、螢幕閱讀器、字型放大等 50+ 項準則。

Four pillars of AI ethics:

  • Bias: training data reflects social inequities. Amazon's 2018 résumé AI penalized women and was retired.
  • Fairness: common metrics include demographic parity and equal opportunity — and they can conflict.
  • Explainability (XAI): high-stakes decisions (credit, healthcare) must be explainable. Tools: SHAP, LIME, Integrated Gradients.
  • Accountability: when AI errs, who is liable — developer, deployer, or the AI? Regulators lean toward the deployer.

Digital divide & accessibility (a11y): nearly 30% of humans still lack broadband. The WCAG 2.2 standard (2023) lists 50+ guidelines for keyboard operation, color contrast, screen readers, scalable fonts.

Copyright of AI-generated content: The US Copyright Office (2023) ruled that purely AI-generated works are not protectable; the EU AI Act requires generative-AI training-data transparency. Mainstream approach: copyright attaches only when a human contributes substantively.

綠色運算

三、資料中心與 AI 能源

PUE (Power Usage Effectiveness) = 資料中心總耗電 ÷ IT 設備耗電。理想 = 1.0；2024 全球平均 1.58；Google / Meta 自家頂級水冷可達 1.10。

國際能源署 (IEA) 2026 報告估計：全球資料中心 + AI + 加密貨幣占全球用電 ~3.5%，預計 2030 上升至 7%。GPT-4 訓練估計耗電 ~1,300 MWh（相當於 130 個美國家庭一年用電）。

PUE = total facility energy ÷ IT equipment energy. Ideal = 1.0; global average 1.58 (2024); top-tier Google/Meta water-cooled sites hit 1.10.

IEA's 2026 report estimates data centers + AI + crypto consume ~3.5% of global electricity, projected to reach 7% by 2030. Training GPT-4 used ~1,300 MWh — roughly the annual electricity of 130 US households.

五大新興科技

四、五項新興科技

⚛️ 量子運算Quantum Computing

2026 兩大里程碑：IBM Nighthawk（120 qubit，相連性大幅提升）與 Google Willow（首度實現「below threshold」錯誤更正——增加 qubit 反而降低錯誤率）。Shor 演算法仍需數千邏輯 qubit，估計 2030+ 才能威脅 RSA。

Two 2026 milestones: IBM Nighthawk (120 qubits, far better connectivity) and Google Willow (first "below-threshold" error correction — adding qubits actually lowers error). Shor still needs thousands of logical qubits; threat to RSA likely 2030+.

⛓️ 區塊鏈 / Web3Blockchain / Web3

Bitcoin (2009)、Ethereum (2015) 為代表。2022 Ethereum「The Merge」轉為 Proof-of-Stake，耗電降 99.95%。NFT、DeFi、Web3 經歷 2022–24 寒冬後，2026 重心轉向真實世界資產代幣化 (RWA) 與穩定幣支付。

Bitcoin (2009), Ethereum (2015). The 2022 Ethereum "Merge" cut energy use by 99.95%. After the 2022–24 winter, NFTs/DeFi/Web3 have pivoted in 2026 to real-world asset tokenization and stablecoin payments.

🕶️ XR / AR / VRXR / AR / VR

Apple Vision Pro（2024 上市，2026 第二代降價）、Meta Quest 4、Pico 5。重點從消費娛樂轉向企業訓練、遠距協作、醫療規劃。「空間運算 (spatial computing)」成為新關鍵詞。

Apple Vision Pro (2024 launch, 2026 cheaper Gen 2), Meta Quest 4, Pico 5. Focus has shifted from consumer entertainment to enterprise training, remote collaboration, and surgical planning. "Spatial computing" is the new buzzword.

🧠 神經型態運算Neuromorphic

模仿腦神經元的「脈衝 (spike)」運算方式。代表晶片：Intel Loihi 2、IBM NorthPole。在邊緣 AI、感測器融合上能效比 GPU 高 10–100 倍，特別適合永遠在線 (always-on) 場景。

Chips that mimic neurons via "spikes." Notable: Intel Loihi 2, IBM NorthPole. 10–100× more energy-efficient than GPUs for edge AI and sensor fusion — ideal for always-on workloads.

🧪 腦機介面 (BCI)Brain-Computer Interface

2024 Neuralink 首位人類植入者用腦波下棋與打 X；同時 Synchron 採微創血管路徑進行多人臨床試驗。短期應用：癱瘓患者溝通、義肢控制；長期：醫療輔助與認知擴展。倫理爭議：思想隱私、不可逆植入。

2024 Neuralink first human implant played chess and posted on X by thought; Synchron ran multi-patient trials via a less invasive vascular route. Near-term: communication for paralyzed patients, prosthesis control. Ethical concerns: mental privacy, irreversibility.

合規檢查

五、決策樹：合規檢查

🌳 我的 AI 系統合規嗎？

Q1:
會處理歐盟使用者個資？→ 必須符合 GDPR：明確同意、被遺忘權、DPO（資料保護官）。
Q2:
用於招募 / 信用 / 教育 / 執法？→ 屬 EU AI Act 高風險，需風險管理系統、人類監督、CE 標記。
Q3:
會與用戶互動的聊天機器人？→ 必須揭露使用者正在與 AI 對話。
Q4:
用敏感族群（性別、種族、宗教）資料訓練？→ 加做偏誤稽核（fairlearn、aif360）並記錄差異。
Q5:
會做出影響個人權益的決策？→ 提供解釋（SHAP/LIME）與申訴管道。
Q1:
Process EU users' personal data? → Must comply with GDPR: explicit consent, right to be forgotten, DPO.
Q2:
Used for hiring / credit / education / law enforcement? → High-risk under EU AI Act — risk-management system, human oversight, CE marking.
Q3:
Chatbot interacting with users? → Must disclose that the user is talking to AI.
Q4:
Trained on sensitive group data (gender, race, religion)? → Run bias audits (fairlearn, aif360) and document disparities.
Q5:
Decisions that affect individual rights? → Provide explanations (SHAP/LIME) and an appeal channel.

六、用 fairlearn 檢測偏誤

# pip install fairlearn scikit-learn
from sklearn.linear_model import LogisticRegression
from sklearn.model_selection import train_test_split
from fairlearn.metrics import MetricFrame, demographic_parity_difference
from sklearn.metrics import accuracy_score

# 假設我們有信用貸款資料，sensitive_feature 是性別 / Loan data, sensitive = gender
X_tr, X_te, y_tr, y_te, A_tr, A_te = train_test_split(X, y, gender, test_size=0.3)

clf = LogisticRegression(max_iter=1000).fit(X_tr, y_tr)
y_pred = clf.predict(X_te)

# 整體準確率 / Overall accuracy
print("Acc:", accuracy_score(y_te, y_pred))

# 分群準確率 / Per-group accuracy
mf = MetricFrame(metrics=accuracy_score, y_true=y_te, y_pred=y_pred,
                 sensitive_features=A_te)
print(mf.by_group)               # 看是否各組差異過大

# 群體公平差距 / Demographic-parity gap (越接近 0 越公平)
gap = demographic_parity_difference(y_te, y_pred, sensitive_features=A_te)
print(f"DP gap = {gap:.3f}")
# 法規常見門檻 < 0.1；若超過則須調整模型或重新採樣資料
// AI 倫理檢查清單 / AI Ethics Checklist (deploy-time)
function EthicsReview(model, dataset, use_case):
    checks = []

    // 1. 法規分類
    if use_case in {hiring, credit, education, law}:
        checks.add("high_risk: requires risk-mgmt + human oversight")

    // 2. 偏誤稽核
    for each protected_attr in {gender, race, age, disability}:
        gap = DemographicParity(model, dataset, protected_attr)
        if |gap| > 0.1:
            checks.add("bias gap on " + protected_attr)

    // 3. 透明度
    if model.is_chatbot():
        checks.add("must disclose AI to user")

    // 4. 可解釋性
    if use_case.affects_rights():
        checks.add("provide SHAP/LIME explanation")

    // 5. 隱私
    if dataset.contains_PII():
        checks.add("GDPR consent + retention policy")

    return checks
